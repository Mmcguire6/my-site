About This Project

DataFuse is a Shopify analytics app that consolidates store data into clear, actionable revenue insights — no spreadsheet required. It pulls together sales, traffic, and product data into a single clean dashboard, giving store owners a real-time view of what's working and what isn't without having to dig through Shopify's fragmented reports.

The problem it solves is simple: Shopify's native analytics are scattered across multiple screens and require manual cross-referencing to get any meaningful picture. DataFuse centralises all of that into one view, with the metrics that actually matter to a store owner front and centre.
