— The Northern Peak method —

Five steps. One connected system.

Every build follows the same proven path — from understanding your business to growing it month after month.

1

Understand

We start by learning your business — your goals, your customers, and what’s actually slowing you down. No discovery-call runaround and no jargon; just the questions that matter so we build the right thing from day one.

2

Design

We tailor the intake, voice, and flow to your business — the questions it asks, how it books, where the lead goes. You hear it working before any money changes hands.

3

Build

Once you’re in, we build the full system — your AI front desk plus the booking, follow-up, and reviews your business needs, connected to your calendar and CRM. All done for you, all handled in-house.

4

Connect

This is where most businesses fall down. We connect every tool — website, CRM, calendar, and reviews — into one system that talks to itself, so no lead slips through the cracks and nothing lives on an island.

5

Improve

Launch is the starting line, not the finish. Every month we monitor, analyze, and optimize — tuning what’s working, fixing what isn’t, and keeping your system sharp as your business grows.
