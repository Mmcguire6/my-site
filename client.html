          Common questions

          How long until my website is live?

          Most businesses are live within 7–10 days of completing setup. Once you submit your setup, your target launch dates appear on Home.

          Can I change my setup answers?

          Before you submit, everything saves as you go and you can edit any step. After you submit, email or call us and we’ll update it for you.

          How do I send more photos or files?

          Add them any time in Files: images, PDF, AI, EPS or ZIP, up to 25 MB each.

          Do you need my passwords?

          No. Never send us passwords. If we need access to your domain, Google profile or phone provider, we’ll arrange it with you securely.

          How do I update my card or get a receipt?

          Use Update card in Billing. Stripe, our payment processor, emails a receipt for every payment, and your invoices are listed in Billing.

          How do I cancel?

          Your plan is month-to-month. Email us to cancel; cancellation takes 30 days’ written notice, and you keep your domain and content. See our terms.
