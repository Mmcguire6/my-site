What’s in · what’s out

Transparent on both sides.

The studio plan covers everything most professional services firms need to launch and run a hand-built site. It does not try to be every possible feature for every possible buyer. Below: what’s included, and what’s explicitly out of scope (or quoted separately as add-ons).

In the plan

Everything you actually need.

  • Up to a six-page brochure site (home, about, services, team, contact, plus one)
  • Custom typography, palette, and imagery direction drawn around your firm
  • Real, hand-written code — deployed to Netlify, fast-loading, accessible
  • Domain registration, SSL, analytics, contact-form wiring
  • Two rounds of revisions inside scope, before launch
  • Ongoing content edits via email, handled by me, no CMS to learn
  • Security patches, monitoring, hosting maintenance — forever
  • Search-engine submission, sitemap, schema markup, foundational on-page SEO
Not in the plan

Quoted separately.

  • E-commerce / Shopify or other shopping-cart integrations
  • Booking systems, scheduling integrations, custom calculators
  • Custom blog architecture beyond a simple posts page
  • Multi-location / multi-language site networks
  • Paid advertising management (Google Ads, Meta Ads — not offered)
  • SEO content writing or ongoing campaigns (foundational SEO is included; ongoing campaigns are not)
  • Photography, video production, custom illustration commissions
  • Major redesigns initiated mid-subscription — quoted and rolled in

The honest part

It’s a subscription, not a buyout.

The most important thing to understand before signing up: this is a subscription model. $0 down + $200/month buys you ongoing access to a hand-built site that the studio designs, builds, hosts, and maintains. If your subscription ends, the site goes offline at the end of the notice period.

The plain math: it takes a one-person studio about two-to-three years of $200/month to recoup what a comparable lump-sum build would have cost up front. That’s how the $0-down pricing is financially viable. It only works if clients stay long enough to make the math work — so departures with the site can’t be permitted. That’s the trade.

  • Within the first twelve months — cancellation requires payment of $3,800 (the studio’s lump-sum build equivalent) minus what you’ve already paid. Example: pay $1,000 over 5 months and choose to cancel, the early-termination fee is $2,800.
  • After the first twelve months — cancel any time with thirty days’ written notice. No exit fee. The site is taken offline at the end of the notice period.
  • You always keep your domain — registered in your firm’s name from day one, transferred to a registrar of your choosing if needed.
  • You always keep anything you provided — logo files, photos, written content, brand assets — yours to take to whatever new vendor you engage.
  • You don’t keep the built site — its code, design, custom typography work, and deployed URL remain the studio’s. There is no buyout option.

If long-term ownership of the code matters more than $0 down, this isn’t the right model for your firm — and that’s a fine answer. Email me and we can talk about a one-time lump-sum build instead. Otherwise, the subscription is the offer, and the trade is on the table in plain language.
