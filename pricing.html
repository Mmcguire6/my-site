Not sure which to pick?

Most clients choose the monthly plan because it works out cheaper over time and includes everything — design, hosting, edits, and ongoing support. The lump sum option is for clients who prefer to pay once and own the site outright. Both options use the same design and build process — the only difference is what’s included after launch.

Important: the monthly subscription is a service relationship — if you cancel after the 12-month minimum, the built site goes offline. You always keep your domain. If long-term ownership of the code matters more than ongoing service, choose the lump sum option.

All prices in Canadian dollars.
