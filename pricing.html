Is there really no setup fee?

Really. We design and build your website free, and you start paying the monthly when it goes live. No build invoice, no onboarding fee, no surprise line items.

What does the $97/month actually cover?

Everything that keeps your site alive and working: hosting, security, backups, unlimited edits and updates, the local SEO foundations, and direct support from the person who built it.

What's the difference between the two plans?

The $97 plan is the website, hosted and cared for. The $297 Peak System adds everything that captures and converts leads — missed-call text-back, the 5-star review funnel, automated follow-up, marketing campaigns, and CRM with appointment booking.

Can I start with the website and upgrade later?

Yes. The website plan is built on the same foundations, so upgrading to the full system later is a switch-on, not a rebuild.

What happens if I cancel?

You stop paying and keep your domain and any content you provided. Month to month means exactly that — stay because it works, not because you're locked in.

Are there per-call or usage fees?

No. Both plans are flat. We'd rather keep you for years at a fair rate than nickel-and-dime you for being busy.
