The questions every firm asks

Does it give legal advice?

Never. It gathers details and books the consultation — if a caller asks for advice, it warmly explains the attorney will cover that, and keeps taking the intake. No attorney-client relationship is formed on the call.

What about confidentiality and our data?

Calls are handled securely, recordings and transcripts are retained only as long as you want them, and data is handled with Canadian privacy rules in mind. We’ll walk through the specifics for your firm during setup.

Does it replace my receptionist or staff?

No — it’s an overflow and after-hours front desk. It catches the calls your team can’t: nights, weekends, lunch, and when everyone’s already on the phone.

Can it check for conflicts?

It captures the opposing party’s name during intake and flags it, so a conflict is caught before anyone follows up. The attorney always makes the final call.

What if it mishandles an upset caller?

It leads with empathy and stays inside a tight script — take the details, reassure them, book the callback. For true emergencies it can escalate to your on-call line. And you hear every call, because recording is on.

How long does setup take?

We do it for you. Your intake questions, practice areas, calendar, and Clio connection are configured and tested before you go live — typically within a few days.
