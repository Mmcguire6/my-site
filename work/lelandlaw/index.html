About Leland Kimpinski

Four Decades ofServing Saskatchewan.

The law firm was founded by Dave Leland in 1984 and became Leland Kimpinski in 1995. The lawyers of Leland Kimpinski LLP have a diverse set of skills and backgrounds.

We provide assistance in commercial financing, corporate and business law, real estate, land development, landlord-and-tenant relations, debt enforcement, bankruptcy and insolvency, foreclosure, estate planning, wills, estates and trusts, family law, and general litigation.

Leland Kimpinski LLP is committed to providing a high level of expertise and advice to its clients, whether that client is an individual, a small corporation, or a large institution.
