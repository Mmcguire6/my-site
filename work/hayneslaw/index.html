The Lawyer

Meet Bill Haynes.

Sole lawyer on every file — born and raised in Medicine Hat, practising here since the year he was called.

WH

Lawyer

William (Bill) Haynes

LL.B. · University of Alberta, 1992

William (Bill) Haynes received his Law Degree from the University of Alberta in 1992. Born and raised in Medicine Hat, he returned here after completing law school and has practised here ever since. Bill is the current President of the Medicine Hat and Area Bar Association and a past member of the Medicine Hat Kinsmen, having held various positions in the club.

Outside of the office, Bill is a backpacker and ex-runner (for now), and has coached volleyball, baseball, and badminton over the years. He has two children, who both graduated in Medicine Hat and have recently completed university programs.
