Principal Counsel

Brady Mertz

Twenty-five years in Salem. Counting.

Brady was born and raised in Aberdeen, Washington. He attended the University of Washington, graduating in 1990 with a BA in Economics, then moved to Salem, Oregon in 1993 to attend Willamette University College of Law, graduating in 1996. Brady started practicing law in 1997, and opened his own firm on January 1, 2001. Over the past twenty plus years, Brady has built a successful law practice through dedication and hard work.
