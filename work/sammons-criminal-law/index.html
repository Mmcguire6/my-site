Practice Area I

Sex Offender Registration & Relief

A statewide registration practice few lawyers attempt.

Anna Sammons and her team litigate Oregon sex offender registration and risk level cases statewide. They aggressively challenge initial risk classifications and fight for orders removing clients from the sex offender registry. The firm represents people in Juvenile Courts and Circuit Courts, before the Oregon Board of Parole and Post-Prison Supervision, before the Psychiatric Security Review Board, and in the Oregon Court of Appeals.

  • Dozens of risk level reductions and registry removal orders secured across Oregon
  • Lead counsel in Thomsen v. Board of Parole — reshaped Oregon's sex offender classification law
  • OPDC-qualified expert; regularly consulted by other attorneys on sex offender registration cases
