Scott Moriarity

University of Washington School of Law · J.D.

Scott Moriarity is a Tacoma traffic and infraction attorney and the owner of Law Office of Scott Moriarity. He is exceptionally well qualified to provide strong and effective representation to clients facing a wide range of driving offenses. As a law student at the University of Washington, Scott volunteered at the public defender's office and won his first jury trial while still in law school. After graduating from the University of Washington School of Law, Scott joined the law firm of Bauer and Balerud as an associate attorney in 1999, was made partner in 2005, and established the Law Office of Scott Moriarity in 2007.
