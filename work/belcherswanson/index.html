Brad Swanson

Member

J.D. · B.S. Engineering, Magna Cum Laude · P.E.

Pete Dworkin

Member

J.D., Order of the Coif · B.A. Political Science

Pete Dworkin holds an Order of the Coif J.D. from the University of Oregon (2000), where he served on the National Moot Court Team and published a law review article cited by State and Federal Courts. Before joining Belcher Swanson, he served as Assistant Chief Criminal Deputy for Whatcom County. His practice focuses on commercial and civil litigation, administrative land use appeals, insurance defense, and real estate disputes.

Mark Lackey

Josh Fox

Partner

J.D. (Litigation concentration) · B.S. Psychology

Kristen A. Cavanaugh
