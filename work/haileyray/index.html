№ 02 About

Joining your team — with clinical expertise and deep compassion

I have experience walking with people through life's most tender, complex and most courageous moments. At the heart of my work is the simple truth: I count it as an honor to be a holder of hope and come alongside men and women who are carrying more than they are meant to carry. I want to help you through tough times with care, skill, and dignity.

I welcome people of all backgrounds and all belief systems. My office is a place where you don't have to hide your pain or your story. My clients feel seen, heard and encouraged toward growth and healing. I offer a grace-filled pace. Let's navigate your journey together.
