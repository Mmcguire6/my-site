Meet Dan Seeley-Baechler.

Lifelong resident of the Cambridge area — raised in Ayr, educated at Waterloo and Osgoode Hall, and practicing law in the community he calls home since 2005.

DS

Principal Counsel

Dan Seeley-Baechler, LL.B

Dan Seeley-Baechler is a lifelong resident of the Cambridge area. Raised in the town of Ayr, he attended high school at Southwood Secondary and went on to study political science at the University of Waterloo. He completed his LL.B at Osgoode Hall Law School and was called to the bar in 2004 — articling and practicing at a well-known firm in Brantford before returning to Cambridge in 2005.

In June 2008, Dan opened his own practice on Grand Avenue South, specializing in real estate, wills and powers of attorney, and corporate/commercial law. He and his wife Jocelyn stay busy running after their five kids — Gavin, Brynn, Claudia, Nathan, and Ben. In the time between his family and his work, Dan enjoys reading, gardening, football, and general tinkering.
