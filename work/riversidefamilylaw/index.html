Practice Area · II

Mediation

Resolve disputes voluntarily — with a solution your family helps build.

Mediation is a form of dispute resolution where one impartial third party assists two people to resolve their dispute. You will craft a solution that is tailored to your family's very specific needs, preserving and improving communication between the parties. Finding a resolution that both parties have chosen voluntarily increases the likelihood that the agreement will be respected long into the future.

  • Tailored to Your Family's Specific Needs
  • Preserves Communication & Working Relationships
  • Voluntary Agreement — More Likely to Be Honoured
