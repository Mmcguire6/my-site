№ 03 What we provide

Compassionate, evidence-based care

Two complementary services — medication management for a wide range of mental health conditions, and ketamine therapy for those who need a different path forward.

i.

Medication Management

  • Compassionate, evidence-based medication management for a range of mental health conditions
  • Accepting new patients ages 6 and up
  • In-person and virtual appointments available
  • First appointment scheduled within three weeks
ii.

Ketamine / Esketamine

  • Esketamine (Spravato) nasal spray for adults with treatment-resistant depression
  • IV ketamine for depression, PTSD, or chronic pain

№ 04 A specialty within a specialty

Ketamine & Esketamine Therapy

For patients who haven't found relief through conventional treatment, we offer a different path — delivered with the same individualized, closely-monitored care.

i. Nasal spray

Esketamine (Spravato)

A nasal spray for adults with treatment-resistant depression — administered on-site by trained professionals with close monitoring throughout the process.

ii. IV infusion

IV Ketamine

For depression, PTSD, or chronic pain — delivered in a safe, supportive environment with personalized care and continual oversight.
