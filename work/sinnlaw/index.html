The Lawyer

Meet Jon Sinn.

Principal Attorney at The Law Offices of Jon Sinn, Esq. — widely regarded as one of the top criminal defense lawyers in the state of Ohio.

Principal Attorney · Esq.

Jon Sinn

In 1994, Attorney Jon Sinn walked out of the University of Akron and into the Summit County Prosecutors office where he quickly rose through the ranks becoming a top major crimes prosecutor and ultimately Chief of his division.

In the year 2000, Attorney Jon Sinn grew tired of locking folks away and instead found a passion for helping folks in need. Switching sides, Attorney Jon Sinn struck out on his own and quickly became the go-to criminal defense attorney for good people in bad situations in the Akron area and throughout the state of Ohio.
