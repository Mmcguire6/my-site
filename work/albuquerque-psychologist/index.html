№ 02 About me

A warm, adaptable approach — built around you

I have been practicing psychology for over 10 years and have my doctorate degree from the Chicago School of Professional Psychology. I am a Licensed Clinical Psychologist and a Licensed School Psychologist. The primary reasons people come to see me are depression, anxiety, and relationship difficulties — I can help you understand the underlying issues and make changes that bring you more balance and fulfillment.

We can work short or long term, insight or solution-focused, depending on your needs. I draw from many approaches — behavioral, cognitive behavioral, existential, interpersonal, family systems, and psychodynamic.
