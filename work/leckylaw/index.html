Lawyer & Owner

Kenneth G. Lecky

Kenneth G. Lecky is the lawyer and owner of Lecky Law, primarily focusing on property (transactional) law — the purchasing and sale of homes and land, along with refinancings. Kenneth also practises extensively in business and corporate law, wills and estates, and administrative law for a select number of clients including large non-profit corporation and regulated professions.

Operating from a heritage Queen Anne Revival building at 55 Fitzroy Street in the heart of downtown Charlottetown — with a satellite office in Montague — Kenneth is dedicated to serving Prince Edward Island families and businesses with compassion, diligence, and efficiency.
