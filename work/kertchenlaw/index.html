Background Checks

Firearm background checks in Washington state are handled by either the FBI's National Instant Criminal Background Check System (NICS) or local law enforcement agencies.

NICS Denial Override FBI State Check

III

Unlawful Possession of a Firearm

Unlawful possession of a firearm or “felon in possession” laws in Washington state are described in RCW 9.41.040. Possession of a firearm is unlawful if you have been convicted of a felony.

RCW 9.41.040 Felon in Possession Defense
Learn More
IV

Expungement — Felony

RCW 9.94A.640 controls the expungement of a felony in Washington state. These rules only apply to adult convictions. Juvenile convictions are controlled by a different statute.

Felony Record Clearing RCW 9.94A.640
Learn More
V

Expungement — Misdemeanor

RCW 9.96.060 controls the expungement of a misdemeanor in Washington state. These rules only apply to adult convictions. Juvenile convictions are controlled by a different statute.

Misdemeanor Vacation of Conviction Record Clearing
Learn More
VI

Juvenile Record Sealing

RCW 13.50.260 controls the sealing of juvenile records in Washington state. These rules only apply to offenses charged in juvenile court. Adult offenses are controlled by a different statute.

Juvenile Record Sealing Youth Offenses
Learn More
VII

SeaTac Airport Firearm Possession

Possession of a firearm at SeaTac Airport is made unlawful by RCW 9.41.300, which prohibits weapons in the restricted access areas of a commercial airport.

SeaTac Airport Weapons Charge

VIII

False Swearing

The crime of false swearing occurs when a person “makes a false statement, which he or she knows to be false, under an oath required or authorized by law.” False swearing is a gross misdemeanor.
