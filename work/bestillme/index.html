№ 02 My approach

Healing happens on multiple levels

I believe healing happens on multiple levels—not just through talking, but through deeper awareness, emotional processing, and connection to your inner self. As a trauma-informed therapist in Tulsa, I work with adults experiencing anxiety, PTSD, and emotional overwhelm, helping them feel safe, grounded, and more in control of their lives.

My approach blends evidence-based therapy (EMDR, CBT, CPT) with more integrative methods like hypnosis and deeper inner exploration. This allows you not only to understand your experiences—but also to process and release them, so you can move forward with clarity, confidence, and emotional stability.
