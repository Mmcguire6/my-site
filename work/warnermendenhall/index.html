Our Flagship

Practice I · Whistleblower

False Claims Act & Whistleblower

For those who dare to speak up against fraud.

The False Claims Act, 31 U.S. Code § 3729–3733, is a tool for private citizens to stop fraud and abuse and assist the federal government in recovering ill-gotten gains from corporations who defraud the taxpayer. Our clients collect substantial rewards for blowing the whistle. Common frauds stopped by whistleblowers include Medicare and Medicaid fraud, Anti-Kickback Statute violations, government contract fraud, and municipality grants fraud.

  • Over $21 million settlement — Cleveland Clinic / Akron General Health System
  • Whistleblower retaliation protection and recovery
  • Federal and state qui tam representation nationwide

The Founding Attorney

Meet Warner Mendenhall.

A former two-term Akron City Councilman who went to law school precisely because the city law department wasn't giving him straight answers — and built a mission-oriented practice from there.

Founding Attorney

Warner Mendenhall

I have been active politically all my life. Beginning with two terms on Akron City Council in the early 1990s, I have helped citizens fight corruption and the abuse of power of local governments throughout Ohio. After getting licensed in the late 1990s, I won my first cases exposing block grant fraud and defending Charter Amendments in Akron and knew that I had found my calling. Since then, I've been building a mission-oriented practice to hold local, state government, and corporations accountable — anchored by a passion for helping ordinary people stand up to government abuses, corporate fraud, and bank malfeasance.
