About the park

Big, simple, and right in the city.

Shakers Acres RV Park & Campground sits in west Edmonton with 163 sites that take RVs, motorhomes and campers of every size. About three-quarters have full hookups — power, water and sewer — and the rest have power and water.

It's a no-fuss, spacious, pet-friendly place to park: lots of room to spread out, the essentials done well, and the whole city minutes away.

At a glance

  • 163 sites — RVs & campers of all sizes
  • ~75% full hookup, ~25% power & water
  • Free showers & free sani-dump
  • Laundry facilities & spacious grounds
  • Pet-friendly · weekly & monthly rates
