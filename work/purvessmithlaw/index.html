Custody and Access

Protecting your relationship with your children.

Custody disputes are among the most emotionally charged legal matters a family can face. Michael Purves-Smith has deep expertise in contested custody, unilateral access denial, and parental alienation — bringing both strategic litigation skill and mediation experience to every case. The focus is on protecting your relationship with your children while achieving durable, enforceable outcomes.

  • Negotiated and fully litigated custody resolutions
  • Urgent motions and emergency relief where required

Practice Area II

Property Division

Equalization handled with precision.

Property equalization in Ontario requires meticulous analysis of all family assets — including businesses, corporations, farm holdings, pensions, and the matrimonial home. Purves-Smith Law provides strategic advice on equalization payments, occupation rent, and the sale of the matrimonial home, ensuring your financial interests are fully protected through negotiation or litigation.

  • Business, corporate, and farm asset equalization
  • Matrimonial home: sale, occupation rent, and buyout
  • Pension division and valuation advice

The Lawyer

Meet Michael Purves-Smith.

An Osgoode Hall graduate, Wellington Law Association member, and tough litigator focused on high-conflict, complex family matters across Guelph and the KW region.

MP

Principal Counsel

Michael D. Purves-Smith

B.A. · J.D., Osgoode Hall Law School

Michael Purves-Smith is an experienced family lawyer who focuses on high-conflict, complex matters, including custody, support, and property issues. A graduate of Osgoode Hall Law School and recipient of the Morden Prize in Evidence Law, Michael articled at a boutique litigation firm in Toronto before practising family law with a leading firm in Guelph.

Michael resides in Guelph, is an active member of the Wellington Law Association and the Law Society of Ontario, and performs as a Jazz musician in his spare time.
