№ 02 Who we are

A holistic practice for the whole person

Dallas Whole Life Counseling is a nationally recognized mental health practice, co-founded by Dr. Heather and James Robbins. Clients choose us because they want an experienced therapist who will listen actively and compassionately, and offer emotional support and heartfelt encouragement. Every patient is different, and we create custom treatment plans to deal with an individual's unique issues, emotions, and life experiences.
