Permanent Residence

A life-changing milestone, prepared meticulously.

Achieving permanent residence in Canada is a life-changing milestone. RND Law Office guides clients through every pathway — Express Entry, Provincial Nominee Programs, family sponsorship, and parent and grandparent sponsorship — with meticulous file preparation and strategic advice designed to maximize approval chances. The firm handles multifaceted and legally challenging cases to seek approvals for deserving applicants and ensure family reunification in Canada.

  • Express Entry and Provincial Nominee Program applications
  • Family sponsorship including parent and grandparent categories
  • Post-Graduation Work Permit applications bridging to permanent residence

Immigration Appeals

From procedural fairness to the Federal Court.

When an immigration matter is refused or escalated, RND Law is prepared to fight at the highest levels of the Canadian immigration system. The firm responds to procedural fairness letters, represents clients at admissibility hearings, argues appeals before the Immigration Appeal Division, and pursues judicial reviews at the Federal Court of Canada — providing robust, determined advocacy at every stage.

  • Procedural fairness letter responses and admissibility hearings
  • Immigration Appeal Division representation
  • Federal Court of Canada judicial review applications

Ravi Narayan ‘Ray’ Dulani is the CEO and founder of the firm, and brings in a wealth of excellent academic career and diverse experiences.
