A faith-based nonprofit — care for everyone

Our experience enables us to offer effective outpatient, individual counseling, marriage and relationship counseling, diversion programs, sports psychology, spiritual care and therapy for military members and veterans. № 03 Our mission & vision

Healing the whole person

A philosophy rooted in the belief that mental and emotional well-being are integral to overall health.

i. Our mission

“Everyone has the ability to lead a happy and fulfilling life.”

Therapy can be a powerful tool for achieving balance — and we are committed to educating, empowering and advocating for mental health and wellness.

ii. Our vision

The home of compassionate family mental health care

We promote psychological value and provide personal therapy to heal the whole person and improve peace of mind. Our team is bold and authentic — grit, determination, and positive self-regard for the human condition is part of our primary practice.
