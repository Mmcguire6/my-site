What We Do

A full-service firm for every legal need.

Real Estate Law

Family Law

Wills & Estates

Corporate & Commercial

Our Approach

From real estate roots to full-service counsel.

Kahane Law Office began with a focus on real estate — helping Calgarians close on the homes and properties that mattered most. Today, that same care extends across more than a dozen practice areas, in two offices, for individuals and businesses alike.

The principle hasn't changed: exceptional service, fair fees, and counsel that respects both your time and your money.
