№ 08 Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Word of Life Counseling Center is a non-profit, full service Christian counseling center founded in 1991 that provides state licensed Marriage and Family Therapy, a State Licensed Drug and Alcohol Outpatient Clinic, court approved anger management, life coaching, Health and weight coaching, and parenting classes.

God's method of salvation works but sometimes we need someone to help us discover the problem and remove the roadblocks that are stopping God's process of salvation and the move of the Holy Spirit within us from making us completely whole. The counselor's place in the body of Christ is to assist individuals and families to apply the word of God and the enablement of the Holy Spirit to resolve specific kinds of difficult and long-term psychological, emotional, and family problems.

At Word of Life Counseling Center we use an underlying overall methodology based directly on the Bible called Faith Therapy. It is used to integrate in-depth Bible knowledge, biblical principles, biblical models, Theophostic prayer ministry, and other counseling methods that agree with the Bible, alongside many years of experience dealing with the diagnosis and treatment of specific mental health disorders.

Here you will find thoroughly professional Christian counselors who are both state licensed and have been trained in in-depth Bible-based Faith Therapy. Our professional counselors are all Licensed in the State of Kansas — some at the clinical level, and two are licensed Clinical Addictions Counselors. Combined they have over 86 years of counseling experience, have counseled more than 6,053 clients for at least 39,083 hours, and nearly 89% of our drug and alcohol clients report they are still sober one year after treatment.
