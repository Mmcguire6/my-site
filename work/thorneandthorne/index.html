About the Firm

At the edge of the Athabasca River.

Our main office is located at the edge of the Athabasca River overlooking the main bridges which connect Fort McMurray. The building contains several meeting rooms including mirror boardrooms that can be expanded to become one of the largest boardrooms in Fort McMurray.

From these northern Alberta roots, Thorne & Thorne has grown into a multi-office practice serving clients across Alberta and British Columbia — with offices in Edmonton, Vancouver, and Spirit River extending our reach across western Canada.

Over the past decade, a growing number of our successful business clients have relocated part of their operations and residences to British Columbia. To accommodate the needs of these clients, we operate an office in the greater Vancouver area from which we conduct Alberta and British Columbia legal services.
