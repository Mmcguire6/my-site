Our Approach

The four T's of Nachbar Law.

Truthful. Trustworthy. Tough. Thorough. Four principles that have earned the firm hundreds of clients — and brought the long-term and repeat ones back again.

01

Principle I

Truthful

We tell you what's going on.

Our attorneys and staff will be truthful with you. We will always let you know what is going on in your case and will be truthful with you about your case.

02

Principle II

Trustworthy

Earned, one client at a time.

We have earned the trust of hundreds of clients over our many years of practice. We have many repeat and long-term clients. We have earned their trust and their repeat business.

03

Principle III

Tough

We don't make excuses.

We don't make excuses. We are tough litigators who can battle with the government or the other party. We thrive on outsmarting and outfighting the other side.

04

Principle IV

Thorough

We listen, then we prepare.

We will take the time to listen to you and to learn about your case. We won't rush you or cut you short. We will work to uncover every detail that can help us to be successful with your case in court.
