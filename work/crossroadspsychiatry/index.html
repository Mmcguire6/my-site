Dr. Kourtne Roberts

MD · Board-Certified Psychiatrist

I completed medical school at the University of Texas Medical Branch in Galveston in 2014 and my psychiatry residency there in 2018. I was most attracted to psychiatry because of the impossibility of separating the patient from their illness — care that requires a trained eye and a real conversation about their subjective experience within their own life.

In residency, I supplemented my training in a 60-bed inpatient unit covering addiction, acute adult, geriatric and adolescent care, a private practice clinic, and an adult inpatient chemical dependence rehabilitation center. I also served as resident faculty at the medical-student-run community clinic providing care exclusively to the indigent population of Galveston.

№ 08 Common questions

Good to know

Telemedicine is the term given to remote healthcare delivery — for example, via the internet and videochat apps. It allows doctors and patients to converse with one another remotely, wherever they happen to be in Texas.

As a medical doctor, Dr. Roberts's role is to diagnose a mental health condition, determine whether medications are safe and helpful for your condition, maximize the benefit of medications without doing harm, and manage psychotropic medications — all to help you lead a more fulfilling life.

A psychiatrist is needed when talk therapy is not enough to achieve the goal of stabilizing your condition and getting you to feel better. Many patients work with both — a therapist for talk therapy and a psychiatrist for medication management.

Patients across the state of Texas. The list of conditions Dr. Roberts has experience with is on the Specialties section above — in addition to "stress," "nerves," and whatever else life throws at people.
