№ 04 Supervision, workshops & community training

Beyond the therapy room

Investing in the wider community of practitioners, parents, and families across Iowa.

i. Clinical Supervision

For therapists in training

IDPH-approved supervisor for therapists in training and those working toward licensure in Iowa. My approach is rooted in cultural humility, critical awareness, and my own therapy experiences — I carefully choose how many supervisees I take so I can invest in each one's growth.

ii. Parenting Workshops

Understanding your child

Workshops designed to help you understand the underlying reasons for your child's behavior, respond in ways that diffuse challenging situations, and strengthen your relationship with your child — creating a more harmonious home environment.

iii. Child Protection & Juvenile Court Training

For professionals

Specialized lectures and workshops for professionals working with families involved in child protection proceedings and juvenile court systems — covering trauma's impact, family separation, family rights, and effective intervention strategies.

Yes. In addition to in-person therapy, we offer telehealth counseling sessions that allow you to meet with a licensed therapist from the comfort of your home. Our platform is HIPAA-compliant, ensuring your sessions are private and confidential.

Olive Branch Therapy is a preferred in-network provider for Blue Cross / Blue Shield (CareFirst), Tricare, Cigna, Medicaid (state insurance), Aetna, and United Health Care. Some providers are limited in which insurance they accept — feel free to call us and ask.
