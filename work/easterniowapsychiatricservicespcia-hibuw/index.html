№ 04 A specialty within a specialty

Children mental health services

EIPS specializes in helping children with their mental health. Our team understands the challenges faced by young patients and their families, and offers customized support — incorporating family involvement and age-appropriate care plans — to help children manage their mental health effectively.

  • i.

    Child Anxiety Disorders

    Specialized psychiatric evaluation and treatment for anxiety in children.

  • ii.

    Child Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity (ADHD)

    Medication management for ADHD, available from age five and older.

  • iii.

    Child Mood Disorders

    Evidence-based psychiatric care for childhood mood disorders.

  • iv.

    Conduct Disorder (CD)

    Targeted evaluation and treatment for conduct disorder.

  • v.

    Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD)

    Comprehensive psychiatric care for oppositional defiant disorder.

Early intervention helps young ones build lifelong resilience.

№ 09 Common questions

Good to know

Eastern Iowa Psychiatric Services PC offers comprehensive mental health services, including medication management, brief therapy sessions, and treatment for a wide range of conditions such as anxiety, depression, bipolar disorder, PTSD, ADHD, schizophrenia, and more. We also provide personalized care plans to ensure each patient receives the most effective mental health treatment in Cedar Rapids, IA for their unique needs.
