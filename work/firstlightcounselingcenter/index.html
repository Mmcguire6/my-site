№ 02 About Cole

I work with people, not disorders.

You are so much more than just a diagnosis or a disorder. I help people who are feeling stuck and want to move forward to create a better life. You don't go through life just to be plagued by anxiety, depression, panic attacks, or relationship problems — and you don't have to.

My goal in counseling is to help you, your marriage, or your family flourish. I'm not concerned with diagnosing you as much as I am with helping you achieve your goals of a better life. My passion is to help give you the tools you need so that you won't need me anymore.

“I've been described as a consummate optimist — and while I won't make light of your struggles or feed you empty platitudes, I will help you find hope and the small joys in life.”

Est.Omaha marriage & family therapist
