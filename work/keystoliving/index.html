№ 02 Who we are

Skilled at helping, and we care

Keys to Living Counseling Center was founded in 1980 by local professionals and pastors. The need was for professional counseling services that not only respected the faith and values of Christian clients but utilized them as integral to the counseling process. Understanding this worldview is central to the effectiveness of treatment — and based on the absence of this kind of professional counseling service, Keys to Living was founded.

№ 07 Rates & insurance

Professional counseling at minimal expense

i. Coverage

  • Covered by most insuranceAll of our therapy services are typically covered through medical insurance. You'll pay only your copay if you've met your deductible.
  • Title 19 (Medicaid)If you qualify for Title 19 Medicaid (state insurance), your counseling will be fully covered.
  • Sliding fee availableIf you don't have insurance — or yours doesn't cover mental-health benefits — we offer a sliding fee based on income to make counseling affordable.
