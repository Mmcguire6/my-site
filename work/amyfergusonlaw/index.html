The Attorney

Meet Amy E. Bailey.

Founder of Ferguson Law Office, University of Dayton Law alumna, and a former Magistrate who has spent her entire career serving the Dayton community.

AB

Founding Attorney

Amy E. Bailey

B.A. · J.D. · Visiting Magistrate

Attorney Amy E. Bailey (formerly Ferguson) founded Ferguson Law Office in 2012 after earning her J.D. from the University of Dayton School of Law. In 2019, she accepted the position of Magistrate and Court Administrator at Fairborn Municipal Court, and in 2022 recommitted herself to the full-time operation of her law practice while continuing to serve as a Visiting Magistrate. She is dedicated to providing exceptional legal representation and ensuring client satisfaction in every case.
