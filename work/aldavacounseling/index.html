№ 04 What sets the practice apart

Bilingual & culturally rooted care

Therapy is hard work. Therapy in a second language — for many — is harder still. The practice is built around that truth.

  1. i.

    Therapy in Español and English

    Sessions conducted in Spanish or English. Native-language access is central to the mission of the practice.

  2. ii.

    Deep Latino/a cultural understanding

    A specialised focus on immigrant acculturative stress and the lived experience of the Latino/a community.

  3. iii.

    Breadth of evidence-based trauma methods

    EMDR, ART, CPT, ERP, I-CBT, and Theraplay-Based Strategies — offered by a single experienced clinician.

For the people, families & generations the practice was built around.
