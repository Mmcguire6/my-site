John W. Molony

John W. Molony is a Charleston native who graduated from Porter-Gaud School and earned his Bachelor of Arts degree from Clemson University, double majoring in English and History. While an undergraduate at Clemson, he studied at Universite Catholique de l'Ouest in Angers, France. Immediately after college, Attorney Molony earned his Juris Doctor from Mercer University, Walter F. George School of Law, where he was a member of Phi Delta Phi legal fraternity. As owner and sole managing member of John W. Molony Law Firm, LLC, Mr. Molony personally handles every aspect of each case.
