i. Class Actions & Consumer Protection

Class Actions & Consumer Protection

Holding corporations accountable, one class at a time.

Class actions are at the heart of Michael Fuller's practice. When corporations cheat consumers — through overcharges, data breaches, unlawful practices, or deceptive products — he organizes affected individuals into a unified force capable of fighting back. His results include a $260 million CenturyLink class action, an Equifax data breach class action, and a jury verdict against Wells Fargo covered by NBC News.

  • Major verdicts and settlements against national banks and corporations
  • Consumer protection, UTPA, and debt collector enforcement
  • Data breach, overcharge, mortgage abuse, and whistleblower class actions

The Lawyer

Meet Michael Fuller.

Led by Michael Fuller.

Principal Counsel

Michael Fuller

I grew up in a trailer park and was the first in my family to go to college. As far back as I can remember, I always wanted to be a lawyer — I watched street-smart attorneys fight for underdogs regardless of their ability to pay. I was held back in the first grade and expelled from middle school. I come from humble beginnings, raised in a trailer park by a single mom, and now I dedicate about 20% of my practice to pro bono.

In 2019 I was named one of the “Top 40 Under 40” lawyers in America. I am ranked among the top 2.5% of my peers by Super Lawyers magazine. My Martindale-Hubbell AV rating is Preeminent. I serve as Oregon State Bar consumer section past chair, Oregon Trial Lawyers Association consumer section chair, and National Association of Consumer Advocates Oregon Chapter chair.
