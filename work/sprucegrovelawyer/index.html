N. Loretta Ann Edlund

B.A. (Distinction) · LL.B. · Commissioner for Oaths · Notary Public

Biography

Loretta graduated from the University of Alberta in 1985 with her LL.B. and holds a B.A. degree in Psychology (with Distinction) from U of A (1979–1982). She was admitted to the Bar with the Law Society of Alberta in 1986 by the late Honourable Associate Chief Justice Tevy Miller.

Loretta has more than 35 years of experience practising law in Alberta — with more than 20 of those years in the Parkland County area — guiding people through real estate transactions, family and criminal matters, estate planning, probate, and corporate law.
