№ 04 A specialty within our practice

Ask about SPRAVATO® REMS — a treatment option for treatment-resistant depression.

For some adults with treatment-resistant depression, SPRAVATO® (esketamine) may be an option when other antidepressants have not provided sufficient relief. SPRAVATO® is FDA-approved and is administered in our office under medical supervision as part of the SPRAVATO® Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy (REMS) program.

  • i. FDA-approved for adults with treatment-resistant depression.
  • ii. Administered in-office under medical supervision, with monitoring during and after treatment.
  • iii. Our practice is REMS-certified to safely evaluate, administer, and monitor eligible patients.
