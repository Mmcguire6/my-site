Our Approach

Personal attention, honest fees, real community.

Three principles have shaped how MacMillan Tucker works with every client of the Fraser Valley — the same principles that have kept the firm going.

01

Personal Attention

Your Case Matters

Large or small, every file gets a lawyer who knows it.

Your lawyer will take a personal interest in your case and your situation and give you specialized attention. Cases can take a long time, and it's important to have a knowledgeable lawyer who will check in with you, see how you're doing, answer your questions, and advise you of any developments in your case.

02

Honest Fees

Reasonable & Competitive

Efficient service, fair fees, contingency for injury.

We will give you efficient and effective service and strive to keep your legal fees reasonable and in proportion to the matter. For all personal injury cases, we work on contingency so our clients only pay when their case is successfully resolved.
