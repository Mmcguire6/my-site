№ 02 About Saving Hope

A private practice for the Corpus Christi community

Saving Hope Counseling, PLLC is a private practice serving the Corpus Christi community. It was founded by Gabriela V. Cantu, MS, LPC-S, in 2019 and is designed to be a stable pathway to cultivate growth for individuals, families, couples and children. Our Licensed Professional Counselors, Student Interns and LPC Associates are well-trained in effective techniques, passionate about their craft, and continue to enhance their skills.
