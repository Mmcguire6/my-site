The Attorney

Meet Attorney Folk.

Principal counsel — sole practitioner since 1996, Akron native, and the only handshake on every file.

Attorney at Law

Alexander R. Folk

J.D., University of Akron School of Law

Alexander R. Folk graduated from Kent State University with majors in Political Science and Sociology in 1993, then earned his Juris Doctorate from the University of Akron School of Law in 1996. The Ohio Supreme Court admitted him to practice law in Ohio in 1996. His majors in Political Science, Sociology and Journalism from Kent State University, along with his law degree from the University of Akron School of Law, provide him with the education, qualifications and unique insight into complex organizations and relationships to better serve his clients.
