Meet the Lawyer

David W. McMath

Scotland-born, Quebec-raised, Osgoode Hall-trained — David has practised law in Fredericton since 1992, bringing decades of quiet expertise to every client engagement.

DM

Principal Counsel

B.A. · LL.B.

David W. McMath

David was born in Scotland and raised in Beaconsfield, Quebec. He graduated from Queen's University with a B.A. in 1983, and received his LL.B. from Osgoode Hall Law School at York University in 1986. He practised law in Oshawa, Ontario until 1992 when he relocated to Fredericton and was admitted to the New Brunswick Bar.

David has a broad range of experience in areas such as business law, real estate, wills, charities, and trusts. He has appeared before the New Brunswick Court of Queen's Bench and the New Brunswick Court of Appeal, as well as various administrative tribunals.
