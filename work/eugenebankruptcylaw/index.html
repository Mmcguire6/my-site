Most Common Relief

CHAPTER 7

Chapter 7 Bankruptcy

The fastest route to a fresh start.

Chapter 7 bankruptcy is the fastest route to a fresh financial start — eliminating unsecured debt including credit cards, medical bills, and personal loans. As a Lane County bankruptcy attorney, Tom Butcher has helped hundreds of families struggling with crippling debt discharge what they owe and reclaim their financial lives. Most Chapter 7 filers do not lose any property, and the discharge typically concludes in three to six months.

  • Discharges credit card and medical debt completely
  • Stops wage garnishments, creditor calls, and lawsuits immediately upon filing
  • Most cases completed in 3–6 months with no asset loss
Learn More
Save Your Home

CHAPTER 13

Chapter 13 Bankruptcy

Stop foreclosure. Keep your home.

Chapter 13 bankruptcy creates a structured three-to-five-year repayment plan that allows you to catch up on mortgage arrears, stop a foreclosure sale, and keep your home. Individuals and married couples who have regular income but need time to reorganize benefit enormously from Chapter 13's protections, including the ability to stop foreclosure and address secured and unsecured debts in an orderly, court-supervised plan.

  • Stops foreclosure and allows you to keep your home
  • Structured repayment plan tailored to your income
  • Can address secured and unsecured debt in a single court-approved plan
