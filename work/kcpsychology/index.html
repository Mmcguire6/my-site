№ 03 Our philosophy, treatment & assessment

Best methods. Better results.

Three principles guide everything we offer — from a first session to the most complex forensic evaluation.

i. Our Philosophy

Research-grounded care

We provide therapy and evaluation services with the same high level of quality of care one would expect from any other healthcare specialty — grounded in research and established standards of best practices.

ii. Our Treatment

Measurable, collaborative goals

Every treatment begins with a diagnostic evaluation, followed by a discussion of the best approach to achieve the best outcome in the least amount of time. Patients collaborate to establish measurable goals so progress is objectively visible.

iii. Our Assessments

Psychometrically-sound instruments

Only psychometrically sound instruments are used — with appropriate cultural and developmental considerations in line with the standards of practice and specialty guidelines for the particular type of evaluation.

№ 05 A specialty within a specialty

Specialized Psychological Evaluations

We conduct psychological assessments across a number of areas using only psychometrically sound instruments, with appropriate cultural and developmental considerations. Evaluations are conducted by psychologists with extensive training and experience — including years of instructing and supervising trainees in psychological assessment. Most assessments are completed within one evaluation period, and results can generally be expected within a few weeks.

Psychometrically sound instruments with cultural and developmental considerations.

i.

Diagnostic Clarification

Clarify complex or previously unresolved diagnoses using psychometrically sound instruments.

ii.

Parenting Capacity

Evaluation of parenting capacity conducted per established clinical and forensic guidelines.

iii.

Guardianship Evaluation

Assessment supporting legal guardianship proceedings.

iv.

Adoption Evaluation

Psychological evaluation conducted in support of adoption processes.

v.

Policing Evaluation

Evaluations conducted for law-enforcement purposes.

vi.

Neurodevelopmental & Learning Disorder Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation with cultural and developmental considerations per specialty guidelines.
