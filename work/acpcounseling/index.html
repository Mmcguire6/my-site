№ 02 Who we are

Welcome to ACP

Welcome to Associated Counseling Professionals, a team of experienced and compassionate therapists who are ready to help you with your mental health and wellness needs. Whether you are struggling with anxiety, depression, trauma, relationship issues, life transitions, or any other challenge, we are here to support you and guide you towards positive change.

At ACP, we offer a variety of services for individuals, couples, families, and groups. We work with clients of all ages, backgrounds, and identities. We use evidence-based approaches such as cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT), dialectical behavior therapy (DBT), and eye movement desensitization and reprocessing (EMDR). We also incorporate mindfulness, hypnosis, and other holistic techniques to enhance your well-being.
