№ 02 About · Sylvia Boerger, LPC

We listen, act, and transform

I value the importance of promoting self-care among individuals, couples, and families — those who experience, or are at risk of developing any symptoms of depression, anxiety, and illness related to emotional distress. I see the counseling process as an alliance between client and therapist, and consider the clients as the experts on their lives.

From a young age, I learned to advocate for myself. Coming from a marginalized family myself — moving to a different country, learning a new language, and having an economic disadvantage — adapting was not easy. But through hard work and dedication, I was able to succeed. Now, it's my time to help others reach their successful story.
