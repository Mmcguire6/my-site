Estate Planning

Plans that protect what matters most.

Collier Law near Salem is experienced in all aspects of estate planning. Our practice focuses exclusively on estate planning, trust administration, and probate. A basic estate plan is a set of documents that includes a will, possibly a revocable living trust, and ancillary documents such as a Durable Power of Attorney, Advance Directive, HIPAA Release, and more — legal tools that help individuals and couples plan for and deal with illness, incapacity, and death.

  • Wills, revocable and irrevocable trusts tailored to your family
  • Durable Powers of Attorney and Advance Health Care Directives
  • Complimentary initial estate planning consultation
