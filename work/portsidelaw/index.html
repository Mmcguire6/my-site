What We Do

Twelve services, one family law focus.

From the formal mechanics of separation to mediation, collaborative law, and the urgent orders that protect families — the full breadth of family law, in one firm.

i.

Through separation — the mechanics of dissolving a relationship

Divorce

A plan for separation from the marriage — dividing property, assets, debts, and establishing parenting arrangements.

Uncontested Divorces

When both parties agree and a separation agreement has already been filed with the court — a streamlined path forward.

Property Division

Determining what's included or excluded as family property — matrimonial homes, vehicles, registered and non-registered investment accounts, and pensions.

Child & Spousal Support

Family dissolution often leads to uneven economic situations — we work quickly to address these differences and arrive at a fair result.

Parenting Arrangements

Despite a relationship ending, separating parents typically want the best for their children — protecting children, ensuring a healthy relationship between parents.

ii.

Out-of-court resolution — alternatives to litigation

Collaborative Law

An out-of-court process that helps separating couples reach lasting agreements — with a team of Collaborative lawyers, divorce coaches, and financial specialists.

Mediation

A cost-effective way to resolve separation, divorce, and parenting issues without going to court — our qualified family law mediators help both parties communicate effectively.

Legal Coaching & Unbundled Services

For matters where a full retainer isn't financially viable — targeted help on specific tasks while you handle the rest, with the lawyer's guidance.

iii.

Urgent matters — when time and safety matter most

Emergency Orders

Court orders of urgent nature — we respond equally as quickly to ensure that the fallout of those changes is minimised.

Child Protection

When the Ministry becomes involved due to protection concerns — ensuring clients are properly supported and challenging the Ministry when necessary.

iv.

Family beginnings — planning ahead and growing the family

Prenuptial & Cohabitation Agreements

Agreements that spouses enter into to protect their property in the event of the dissolution of their relationship.

Adoptions

Adding a family member under the age of 18 is an exciting and happy experience — we handle the paperwork and effort to formalise it properly.
