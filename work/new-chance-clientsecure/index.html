№ 04 Therapeutic approaches

A broad, evidence-based toolkit

No single method fits every person. These are the core modalities we draw from — chosen together, in service of your goals.

i.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Restructuring unhelpful thought patterns.

ii.

Eye Movement Desensitization & Reprocessing (EMDR)

Trauma processing through bilateral stimulation.

iii.

Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)

Skills for emotional regulation and distress tolerance.

iv.

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)

Values-based action and psychological flexibility.

v.

Internal Family Systems (IFS)

Working with inner parts toward self-leadership.

vi.

Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT)

Integrating mindfulness with cognitive approaches.

vii.

Solution Focused Brief Therapy (SFBT)

Goal-oriented, strength-based brief work.

viii.

Motivational Interviewing

Collaborative exploration of ambivalence toward change.
