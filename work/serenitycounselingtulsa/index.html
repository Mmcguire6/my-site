№ 04 Treatment style

A faith-integrated, whole-person approach

Two main treatment modalities — EMDR and CBT — woven together with biblical counseling and DBT-informed coping skills.

Ask about the approach →
i.

EMDR Therapy

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing focuses on healing the symptoms and emotional distress that come from disturbing life experiences.

ii.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Focuses on changing dysfunctional thinking, behavior, and emotional responses — decreasing nervousness, negative self-talk, and self-defeating patterns.

iii.

Christian Counseling

As an ORU-trained Christian counselor, biblical counseling is available to those who desire it; the whole-person model incorporates mind, body, and spirit.

iv.

DBT-Informed Care

Dialectical Behavior Therapy principles inform coping skills, emotional regulation, and boundary-building work.
