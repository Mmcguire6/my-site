№ 02 Who we are

Guiding you toward healing, connection & growth

At New Beginnings Counseling, our therapists are available to meet you where you are in your life and work with you on achieving milestones while working toward your ultimate goals — so you are living a healthy, successful, and most importantly, happy and fulfilled life.

i. Our Mission

Meet you where you are. Work with you on achieving milestones while moving toward your ultimate goals — a healthy, successful, happy and fulfilled life.

ii. Our Vision

Normalize the need for mental-health therapy by removing the stigma — to enhance self-care and self-acceptance.

Stigma-freetherapy for every stage of life

№ 04 Therapeutic approach

Empowering change through proven therapeutic approaches

Six evidence-based modalities — chosen and combined to fit you, not the other way around.

i.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Restructuring thought patterns for lasting change.

ii.

EMDR Therapy

Evidence-based trauma reprocessing.

iii.

Dialectical Behavioral Therapy (DBT)

Skills-based emotional regulation.

iv.

Trauma-Focused CBT (TF-CBT)

Specialized trauma treatment for all ages.

v.

Solution-Focused Therapy

Goal-oriented, strength-based approach.

vi.

Sand Tray Therapy

An expressive, non-verbal modality.

№ 05 Getting started

Your first steps

i.

Reach out

ii.

Tell us about you

Share your therapist preference (male, female, no preference), appointment style (in-person or telehealth), and preferred time.

Begin your work
