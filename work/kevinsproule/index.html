About Kevin

From a family farm in Lafleche to a Red Deer courtroom.

"I was born and raised on the family farm near Lafleche, Saskatchewan. Dad was a farmer and community leader, mom was a teacher and farm wife extraordinaire. Five siblings."

Started a general small-town practice in Stettler, Alberta in 1982 — doing a bit of everything. Moved to Red Deer in the early 1990s, focusing more and more on criminal defence ever since.
