№ 02 About the practice

A decade of practice, grounded in research

I have been providing psychological services for over a decade and have practiced in Lubbock since 2018. I work with adults and couples who are dealing with anxiety, depression, work stress, relationship difficulties, and major life transitions, as well as those seeking deeper personal and professional growth.

In West Texas, where affirming and progressive mental health care can be hard to find, I intentionally offer an inclusive, values-affirming space for LGBTQIA+ and gender-diverse individuals. My approach integrates evidence-based therapies such as CBT and ACT with depth-oriented and trauma-informed work — both structured and emotionally meaningful.

№ 05 Meet Dr. Ingram

Dr. Paul Ingram, Ph.D.

Licensed Psychologist · Research & Clinical Services

Dr. Ingram completed his undergraduate degree at the University of North Carolina at Asheville with a B.S. in psychology before earning his M.A. in Clinical Psychology from Western Carolina University. After his Masters, he got licensed and practiced for three years, serving rural and under-served populations in Appalachia.

He returned to school and earned his Ph.D. in Counseling Psychology from the University of Kansas. Outside of practice, he is an avid board gamer, outdoor adventurer, and beer brewer.
