Managing Partner

Tor Friedman

J.D. · B.A. · Florida Legal Elite

Tor Friedman is the managing partner and a lead attorney for Friedman & Abrahamsen Law. He has served as Assistant State Attorney in Leon County Florida and worked in the Juvenile, Traffic, Misdemeanor, and Felony Divisions — where he tried and won litigation in all divisions and has handled thousands of cases. Voted Florida Legal Elite Attorney and Florida Super Lawyer, Tor was also selected to be an adjunct professor at Florida State's College of Law to teach trial practice to future FSU lawyers.

Partner & Co-Lead Attorney

Eric Abrahamsen

J.D. (cum laude) · County Court Supervisor

Eric Abrahamsen is a cum laude graduate of the Florida State College of Law. He immediately went to work for the State Attorney's Office in Leon County Florida, rising from Misdemeanor/Traffic to felony prosecutor and ultimately supervisor of the county court division. Eric spent over 4 years at the State Attorney's Office with numerous jury trials including multiple mandatory life offenses, sexual offenses, armed robbery, kidnapping, arson, and driving under the influence (DUI) offenses.
