The Attorney

Meet Chris Reid.

Cumberland School of Law graduate. Formerly at the Heritage Foundation and Health & Judiciary Policy Analyst for the Governor of Alabama. Birmingham Power 50 Inaugural Class. Five-time host of NBC's The Attorneys.

CR

Founding Attorney

Christopher Calvin Reid

B.A. · J.D. · Birmingham & Dothan

Chris Reid graduated from Samford University in 2006 with a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science and then received his law degree from Cumberland School of Law in May 2010. He has held policy positions at the Alabama Policy Institute and the Heritage Foundation in Washington D.C., interned for House Republican Whip Roy Blunt, and upon graduating law school served as Health and Judiciary Policy Analyst for the Governor of Alabama. In 2014, Mr. Reid moved to his downtown Birmingham office and began specializing in domestic relations, nonviolent criminal defense, personal injury, wills trusts and estates, and business law, growing the firm from a one-man operation to a ten-member staff.
