№ 02 About Beginning Change

Built on availability & the dignity of being heard

Beginning Change Counseling is the vision of Chris Medley. After a decade and a half of working in the University system, Chris saw the growing need for support in the Lubbock community. The goal is to provide autonomy to the client and help the client take charge of their life — because no one should have to feel helpless or voiceless.
