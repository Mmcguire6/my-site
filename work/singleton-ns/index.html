Drug Offences

In Canada there are many criminal laws involving drugs making it an offence to possess, sell, grow or manufacture a number of controlled drugs and substances. It is extremely important if you are charged with a drug offence to retain the services of an experienced criminal lawyer who knows how to defend against these charges.

Possession Trafficking CDSA Grow Op

III.

Sexual Offences

Being charged with any sexual offence is extremely serious and can have enormous consequences for the person charged. Some of these offences can result in a maximum sentence of life in prison. If the police contact you asking questions about any sex-related offence you should immediately contact a lawyer.

Sexual Assault Charter Rights Sex Offender Registry Criminal Code

IV.

Motor Vehicle Offences

The Criminal Code has a number of offences which relate to the operation of a motor vehicle including Dangerous Driving, Driving while a Licence is Suspended, and Hit and Run charges. A charge of Dangerous Driving is very serious and could result in a criminal record, a driving suspension and possibly a period of time in jail.

Dangerous Driving Suspended Licence Hit and Run Criminal Code
Learn More
V.

Assault & Violent Crimes

The Criminal Code defines Assault as a crime of violence involving the intentional application of force without consent to another person. A skilled defence lawyer knows how to exploit the weaknesses in police identification evidence to the advantage of his client.

Assault Domestic Assault Aggravated Assault Violent Crime
Learn More
VI.

Weapon Offences

Weapons charges are very serious offences under the Criminal Code and are dealt with harshly by the courts. The sentences imposed typically range from one to ten years based on the seriousness of the offence. There are mandatory minimum terms of imprisonment of at least one year for some weapons offences.
