DUI / OVI Defense

Protecting your driving privileges & your record.

DUI/OVI defense focuses on protecting the rights of individuals charged with operating a vehicle under the influence of alcohol or drugs. In Ohio, even a first-time offense can lead to serious consequences, including license suspension, fines, increased insurance rates, and possible jail time.

  • Challenge traffic stop legality and procedure
  • Contest breath test and blood test accuracy
  • Pursue charge reduction, dismissal, or acquittal
