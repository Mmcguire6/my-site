IV

Wills & Living Trusts

A Will provides instructions for administration of your estate — the beneficiaries of your assets, guardianship for minor children, and care for pets. A revocable living trust allows you to maintain control of your assets while preparing your estate to avoid the probate process.

Wills Revocable Trusts Guardianship
Learn More
V

Oregon Estate Tax Planning

Oregon is one of the few states with a state-level estate tax — estates valued over $1 million may be subject to it. With proper planning, including credit shelter trusts for married couples, the Oregon estate tax can be significantly limited and your beneficiaries protected.

$1M Threshold Credit Shelter Trust Tax Planning
Learn More
VI

Elder Law

As qualified Elder Law attorneys, we help individuals and families plan ahead so their assets are sufficient to cover the costs of care throughout life. Guardianship, conservatorship, and long-term care planning for the unexpected moments that come with aging family members.

Guardianship Conservatorship Long-Term Care
