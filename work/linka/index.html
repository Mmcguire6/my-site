The Lawyer

Meet Rodger W. Linka

Principal counsel of the firm — called to the Saskatchewan Bar in 1978, and the steady hand on every file ever since.

RL

Principal Counsel

Rodger W. Linka

B.A. · LL.B. · C.Arb.

Rodger W. Linka has been practising law in Saskatchewan since 1978. His early career began in general practice with an emphasis in criminal prosecutions and defence, and over the decades he has built a broad practice serving individuals, families, and businesses across Regina. Holding the C.Arb. designation, Rodger is also a Certified Arbitrator, offering clients sophisticated dispute resolution services outside the courts.
