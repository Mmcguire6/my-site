About Dennis

Meticulous. Passionate. Relentless.

Dennis is a criminal defense lawyer who values being accessible to his clients — a personable and down-to-earth representative who zealously and passionately advocates for his clients. He fastidiously examines each matter, enjoying the challenges and opportunities each case uniquely presents.

He is animated by the words of Voltaire wherein “no problem can withstand the assault of sustained thinking.”

Dennis obtained his 4-year Bachelor of Arts in Criminal Justice and Criminology with 5 and a half years of credits. During his undergrad he assisted in creating two courses for the university — “International Law, Human Rights and Oppression” and “The United Nations” — both of which received accreditation and are still taught today.

After teaching abroad and obtaining certificates in foreign languages, he went on to begin studies in Forensic Science before opting to apply to law school. While in law school, he was employed as a research assistant and worked in Appeals and Advisory with the federal government.
