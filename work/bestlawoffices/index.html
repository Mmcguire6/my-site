Principal Lawyer · Amy P. Best Professional Corporation

Amy P. Best, B.Sc

Born and raised in Sudbury, Amy carries forward more than four decades of family legal tradition in the community she calls home.

Amy obtained her B.Sc (Honours) in Biomedical Sciences from the University of Waterloo, where she graduated on the Dean's List. She attended law school in Fredericton, New Brunswick, graduating on the Dean's List from the University of New Brunswick in 2011. Amy articled and worked for a large full-service firm in London, Ontario from 2011–2013, and was called to the Ontario bar in 2012.

In January 2014, Amy joined her father Peter's practice, and in 2016 she and Peter began practising through her firm, Amy P. Best Professional Corporation (Best Law Offices). Peter retired from the practice of law in October 2023, and Amy now leads the firm.

Our Story

From Espanola to Sudbury — a family in law.

D. Peter Best was born in 1948 and raised in Espanola, Ontario. Called to the bar in 1975, he began his Sudbury practice in 1977 — building deep ties to clients across the Sudbury basin, the North Shore, and Manitoulin Island over the course of nearly five decades.

When Amy joined the practice in 2014, the firm became what it is today: a generational practice where institutional knowledge and experience work side by side. Peter retired in October 2023; his legacy continues in every file Amy carries forward.
