Owner · Lawyer

Neil C. Johnston, Jr.

Your neighbor, your fellow outdoorsman, your trusted legal advisor.

Meet Neil C. Johnston, Jr., a distinguished attorney deeply rooted in the heart of Alabama. Neil is not just an advocate; he's your neighbor, your fellow outdoorsman, and your trusted legal advisor. Born and raised in Mobile, he has a lifelong affinity for the Eastern Shore of Mobile Bay where he spent his formative summers — and come fall, you'll find him relishing the natural beauty of the Tombigbee River in Washington County.

Neil graduated summa cum laude from the University of Alabama in 2009 and earned his Juris Doctorate from the University of Alabama School of Law in 2012, passing the Alabama State Bar that same year. Growing up surrounded by a family of lawyers — including Presiding Circuit Court Judge Joseph M. Hocklander (grandfather) — the practice of law is something Neil approaches with both serious professional rigor and deep personal heritage.
