Featured Practice

Where the firm's real specialism lives.

Two areas of practice define Park Place Law — out-of-court alternatives that protect families, and the deep family-law foundation that backs every file.

Our Speciality

Featured Practice 01

Mediation & Collaborative Divorce

Resolving family disputes outside the courtroom.

Park Place Law offers certified mediation and a certified collaborative divorce practice that keeps families out of court. Collaborative divorce is a respectful, no-court approach to resolving family disputes — supporting both sides through the stress and uncertainty of separation while working toward a fair and equitable outcome through effective problem-solving, not escalation. Kirsten Madsen has appeared as counsel at hundreds of mediations and arbitrations and has served as a facilitator for the Parenting After Separation course provided by the Ministry of the Attorney General for British Columbia.

  • Certified Family Law Mediator on staff
  • Certified Collaborative Family Lawyer
  • Parenting After Separation facilitator — BC Ministry of Attorney General
