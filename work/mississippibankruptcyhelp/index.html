Chapter 7 Bankruptcy

A consumer Chapter 7 bankruptcy typically takes about six months to completely close. Filing wipes out qualifying unsecured debts and provides a clean financial slate — the liquidation chapter for individuals who need a true fresh start.

Repossession

Repossession is when a creditor comes to your house and takes property from you — most often vehicles. If you fall behind in payments, the repo man may take the vehicle from your driveway, parking lot, or while you're at church. Bankruptcy can stop it.
