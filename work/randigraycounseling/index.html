№ 04 A specialty within the practice

Our trauma therapy modalities

We use specialized, evidence-based modalities to help process trauma, reduce symptoms, manage emotional distress, and build healthy coping.

i.

Brainspotting

A focused approach that uses fixed eye positions to access and process unresolved trauma stored in the brain and body.

ii.

Somatic Experiencing

A body-based modality that gently releases the physiological imprint of trauma, restoring nervous-system regulation.

iii.

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization & Reprocessing — a research-supported method to reduce the distress of traumatic memories.

We've all had overwhelming events happen in our life. Our trauma work helps individuals process the trauma, reduce symptoms, manage emotional distress, and develop healthy coping mechanisms.
