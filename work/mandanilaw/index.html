Rashesh Mandani

J.D. · B.A. (Hons.), International Economics and Finance

Mandani Law is built on strong foundations. Rashesh Mandani is a lawyer with first class experience — he was an in-house lawyer for the City of Hamilton from 2012 to 2016, managing the legal matters of the $500 million Hamilton Waterfront redevelopment projects. He articled at the leading Bay Street firm Cassels Brock & Blackwell LLP, where he worked on a $9 billion merger of two international companies, commercial real estate transactions, court appearances, and several major product liability class actions. Prior to law, Rashesh founded and operated a successful IT business for over six years before selling it to pursue legal studies full time.
