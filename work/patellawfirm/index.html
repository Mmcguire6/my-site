Practice · I

DUI Defense

From the stop, to the test, to the verdict.

DUI charges carry life-altering consequences — licence suspension, heavy fines, and potential jail time. Sneh Patel combines his prosecutorial background with deep knowledge of Georgia DUI law to scrutinise every variable: field sobriety testing procedures, breathalyser calibration, traffic stop legality, and constitutional protections. Whether the charge is alcohol- or drug-related, Sneh will look for every available pathway to protect your rights and your future.

  • Former ADA insight into how prosecutors construct DUI cases
  • Full review of stop legality and test procedure compliance
  • Track record of charge reductions and dismissed cases
