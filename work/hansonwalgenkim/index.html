YW

Attorney · Founding Partner

Young Walgenkim

J.D., Magna Cum Laude · LL.M.

Young Walgenkim has been passionate about fighting for the rights of consumers since childhood. Growing up as a son of Korean immigrants, Young witnessed his parents repeatedly fall victim to scams and unscrupulous businesses. During law school he worked at the Financial Fraud/Consumer Protection section of the Oregon Department of Justice, and since starting his law practice in 2012 he has been suing car dealers all across the state for numerous violations of state and federal law.
