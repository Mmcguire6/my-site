07 / 10 — Our Approach Our Approach · Home-Grown Counsel

Our Approach

Home-grown counsel, candid advice.

Four commitments that have shaped how Skidmore & Associates works with every client — from a first conversation to a final closing or verdict.

01

Mentored Practice

Home-Grown Values

A culture, not a credential.

At Skidmore & Associates we bring a "home-grown" element to the practice of law. Each attorney entered the firm at the staff level before becoming licensed to practise law — the mentoring process began prior to each attorney's graduation from law school and continued thereafter.

02

Counsel

Candid Legal Advice

Good, bad, or indifferent.

We represent others the way we would like to be represented. At Skidmore & Associates we provide candid legal advice to clients based upon the facts presented and legal precedent — enabling cost-effective and informative decisions as to their legal options.

03

Standard

Quality People, Quality Law

Personal and professional balance.

We believe that quality legal services can only be provided by quality people. The attorneys and staff aspire to the highest degree of morality in their personal and professional lives — a careful balance is emphasised and one is not practised to the exclusion of the other.

04

Relationships

Long-Term Relationships

Decades of continued service.

We have been able to develop long-term relationships with our clients by providing intelligent and practical solutions to their legal and business concerns — built on decades of service to individuals and commercial clients across Northeastern Ohio.
