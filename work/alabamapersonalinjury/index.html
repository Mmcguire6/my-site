Practice Area I

Car & Truck Accidents

Devastating injuries demand a relentless response.

We share the road with 18-wheelers, big rigs, tractor-trailers, commercial trucks and semi-trucks each and every day. When involved in an 18-wheeler accident, the resulting injuries can be devastating and even fatal. According to the National Center for Statistics and Analysis, each year approximately 5,000 people die in 18-wheeler accidents, and an estimated 140,000 are seriously injured. Lewis & Feldman investigates every accident thoroughly, pursues the trucking company and driver alike, and fights to recover full compensation.

  • Automobile, truck, and motorcycle wreck representation across Alabama
  • Investigation of driver negligence and trucking company liability
  • Maximum compensation for catastrophic and fatal injuries
