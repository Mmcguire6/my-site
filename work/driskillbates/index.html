№ 08 Frequently asked

Good to know

For more on whether an evaluation is the right option for you, please call our office.

Driskill & Bates does not currently provide therapy or counseling services. Following your evaluation we can facilitate treatment planning through your physician or counselor and make additional recommendations for support.

As psychologists, we do not prescribe medication. Following your evaluation we can facilitate treatment planning through your physician and make additional recommendations for support.

Self-pay options are available for evaluations. Please call our office at 806-794-3393 to discuss insurance and payment expectations before your appointment is scheduled.

Please cancel your appointment 48 hours in advance. Appointments missed without calling or cancelled late might not be eligible to be rescheduled or subject to a cancellation fee.

Please do not bring your child to our office if he/she is sick or has been sick in the last 3 days. We do not want to expose our staff or other clients. In addition, evaluations completed when a child is not feeling well are likely to be invalid. Please call to reschedule as you will be sent home if you arrive with a sick child.

Office notices

i.

Sick Child Notice

Please do not bring your child if he/she is sick or has been sick in the last 3 days. Evaluations completed when a child is unwell are likely to be invalid.

Weather Notice
