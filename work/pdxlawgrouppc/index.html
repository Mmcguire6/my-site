07 / 11 — The Approach

Our Approach

How David actually works the file.

Three commitments that have shaped PDX Law Group since 2012 — the principles that turn a "free consult" into a finished file you can stand behind.

01

Principle One

Direct Access

— the lawyer you meet is the lawyer who runs your file.

You work directly with David Richardson from the first call through resolution. No handoffs, no being passed around, no wondering who is actually handling your file.

02

Principle Two

Honest Valuation

— the full picture, not the convenient one.

David looks at the full picture — medical expenses, lost income, pain and suffering, and other damages supported by the facts — so you make decisions with clear information, not pressure.

03

Principle Three

Ready to Fight

— settle when it's fair, try when it isn't.

Most cases resolve through negotiation. When the other side won't be reasonable, David will fight for the result you deserve — whether that means a settlement or taking the case to court.
