What We Do

Your Regina Real Estate, Covered.

DBK Law focuses exclusively on real estate and conveyancing. Every transaction is handled personally by Bernard Duchin — your specialist from contract to keys.

i.

Buying a Home

A lawyer protects your interests under the contract and assists you in transferring Title to the property. Your lawyer also helps you register a new mortgage, remove any unacceptable encumbrances on the Certificate of Title, advance mortgage funds when appropriate, provide sale proceeds to the seller and ensure the purchaser obtains possession of the house on time.

Purchase Title Transfer Mortgage Possession

ii.

Selling a Home

DBK Law assists sellers with removal of Writs of Execution and/or Judgments, discharging instruments against Title, creating the Transfer and Statement of Adjustments, and ensuring sale proceeds are properly distributed. Interim financing arrangements can be coordinated where possession dates overlap.

Sale Title Discharge Adjustments Interim Financing

iii.

Refinancing

This term generally describes the process of rearranging your financial affairs. By refinancing, you may be able to complete a few projects around the house, consolidate loans, maximize your RRSP or finally buy that new car. By refinancing a number of smaller debts and consolidating them into one larger loan, it typically makes it easier to budget and pay all your bills. DBK Law handles the full legal process efficiently.

Mortgage Refinance Consolidation Legal Costs

iv.

Role of the Lawyer

Reviewing and explaining all of the documents involved is perhaps the most visible role we play with our clients. There are many legal and financial documents that routinely need to be explained or reviewed so that you have a better understanding of how the process works. We have received many referrals simply because we have taken the time to answer questions, properly explain things and put clients at ease.
