Our Approach

Counsel that's personal, advocacy that's relentless.

Three principles have shaped this practice since 1990 — the same three principles that drive every file today.

01

Pillar One

Personalized Counsel

Heard before advised.

We are committed to providing our clients with personalized legal services. Our many years of experience enable us to apprise our clients of their options at every stage of a case.

02

Pillar Two

Trial-Ready Advocacy

Settle when we should. Try when we must.

While we are experienced trial lawyers, we make every effort to resolve our cases prior to trial. Should settlement efforts fail, we try our cases zealously and aggressively, but in a professional manner.

03

Pillar Three

Community-Rooted

Born here. Practising here.

Born and raised in Greene County, Michael Sheets has served the Dayton community since 1990. He is a member of the Dayton and Greene County Bar Associations and is licensed to practice in all Ohio state courts and U.S. Federal Courts.
