What We Do

A full range of legal services.

From the courtroom to the closing table — Axon Law represents Edmontonians across the legal matters that shape your life and business.

Criminal Defence

Strong, prepared representation across criminal matters — from impaired driving and assault charges through to serious indictable offences.

Immigration

Permanent residency, work permits, study permits, citizenship, and family sponsorship — guidance through Canada's evolving immigration landscape.

Corporate & Tax Law

Incorporations, shareholder agreements, mergers and acquisitions, and tax planning for Alberta entrepreneurs and growing businesses.

Family Law

Divorce, separation, custody, child and spousal support, and matrimonial property — handled with discretion through difficult transitions.

Wills, POA & PHD

Wills, enduring powers of attorney, and personal directives — protect your family and ensure your wishes are followed.

Residential Real Estate

Purchases, sales, refinances, and conveyancing — closings handled cleanly so the keys land in the right hands on time.

Civil Litigation

Disputes between individuals, businesses, and institutions — from breach of contract claims to commercial litigation in Alberta courts.

Personal Injury

Motor vehicle accidents, slip and fall, and serious injury claims — fighting for fair compensation while you focus on recovery.

Notarization & Authentication

Notary public services, document authentication, and commissioning of oaths for use in Alberta and abroad.
