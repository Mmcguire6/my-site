Lawyers

A South Calgary firm, built on a partnership.

Sandy Cameron founded the firm in Southern Alberta in 1978; Geoff Horne joined upon being called to the bar in 2003 and carries the practice forward today.

Founding Partner · Retired

A.B. (Sandy) Cameron

Practising law in Southern Alberta since 1978, with a focus on real estate conveyancing, new home construction, and condominium matters. Founding partner of the firm; retired from full-time practice at the end of 2006.

B. Comm., Carleton University (1964) · LL.B. (Cum Laude), University of Ottawa (1969) · Called to Bar, Alberta (1978)

Partner

Geoffrey Horne

Came to law after a career in professional and high-performance sports — Strength & Conditioning Coordinator for the Toronto Blue Jays (1993–1997, including the 1993 World Series team) and Athletic Therapist for Canada's National Men's Hockey Team (1997–1999). Practice focused on residential real estate conveyancing, condominium governance, wills & estates, and small business law.

B.Sc. (Zool.), B.P.E., M.P.E., LL.B. — University of Calgary · Called to Bar, Alberta (June 2003)
