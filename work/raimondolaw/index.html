Gang Charges

California law punishes gang members — and those who associate with gang members — much more harshly than people who have no gang ties. Penal Code 186.22 PC can add years to any underlying felony sentence.

3 Strikes Cases

California's Three Strikes law imposes mandatory 25-years-to-life sentences for defendants with two prior serious or violent felony convictions. The stakes couldn't be higher — you need a proven trial lawyer in your corner.

Expungements

Expungement is a legal process by which a conviction or plea of either Guilty or No Contest in a criminal case is set aside and the case is dismissed. It relieves the defendant of many of the disabilities associated with a criminal conviction.
