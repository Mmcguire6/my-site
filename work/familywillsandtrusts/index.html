Minor-Child Protection Plan

Minor-Child Protection Plan

For parents who must consider the unexpected.

Our exclusive Minor-Child Protection Plan is designed for parents who must consider what would happen if they did not make it home from work, a trip to the grocery store, or a date night. People forget, may become unwilling, physically unable, or easily yield to "undesirable" candidates with closer family ties. This plan ensures your children will always be well cared for and loved by familiar people they know and that you trust.

  • Legally appointed guardians with real legal authority to provide care immediately in a crisis
  • Protection from opportunistic third-parties and undesirable candidates with closer family ties
  • Especially vital for parents who have relocated to the Greater Lansing Area without nearby family
