№ 02 Hear our story

A 'boutique' private practice — we are all family here

Welcome to Healing Grace Counseling Center! Healing Grace has been family owned and operated since 1995. The entrepreneurial spirit along with passion for helping others has created a 'boutique' private practice setting — we are all family here, and we hope you feel that when you walk in our doors. Our therapists are focused on promoting the growth of each patient, meeting patients where they are and recognizing no one is defined by a diagnosis.
