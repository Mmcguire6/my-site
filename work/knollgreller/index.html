The Lawyer

Meet Jason Greller.

JG

Principal Counsel · Real Estate Attorney

Jason A. Greller

Originally trained as a litigator, Jason knows that the best way to avoid problems is to start with a well thought out and carefully crafted document. Jason’s clients benefit from his 25+ years of practice, his comprehensive knowledge of real estate law and his experience assisting clients in thousands of transactions.

Jason currently serves on the Board of Directors of the Real Property, Probate & Trust Law Section of the Wisconsin State Bar, is a frequent lecturer on residential real estate transactions, and served as faculty for the University of Wisconsin Law School Lawyering Skills Course for Residential Real Estate Transactions. As a member of the Wisconsin Realtors Association Real Estate Forms Committee, Jason lends his expertise to the WRA regarding Wisconsin State approved forms used in real estate transactions.
