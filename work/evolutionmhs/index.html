№ 05 Neurofeedback

Become a healthier you with neurofeedback

A non-invasive, drug-free therapy that helps your brain learn to function more efficiently. Using real-time feedback from brainwave activity (EEG), neurofeedback teaches self-regulation — improving focus, mood, sleep, and more.

  • i. Evidence-based, supported by neuroscience research
  • ii. Long-term improvement without medication
  • iii. Individualized treatment based on brain activity
  • iv. Complementary to medication & psychotherapy
Give neurofeedback a try →

How it works

i.
Initial AssessmentA brain map (qEEG) identifies areas of dysregulation.
ii.
Training SessionsSensors are placed on the scalp to measure brainwaves while engaging in specific activities.
iii.
Real-Time FeedbackVisual or auditory cues signal the brain to self-correct.
iv.
Repetition & ReinforcementThe brain gradually learns more balanced, flexible functioning.
