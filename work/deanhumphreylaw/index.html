The Attorney

Meet Dean Humphrey.

A North Carolina lawyer who chose Wilmington, built his own firm, and answers his own phone.

DH

Founding Attorney

Dean H. Humphrey

Born in Burlington and raised in Mocksville, North Carolina, Dean attended UNC for college before earning his law degree from Wake Forest University School of Law in 1995. After almost two years in practice in Mocksville, he chose Wilmington as his home in the summer of 1997 and has never looked back. In early 2003 he launched his own firm, built on a simple promise: treat every client with respect and give every case the individual attention it deserves.
