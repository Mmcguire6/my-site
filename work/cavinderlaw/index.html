Founding Attorney

Jason Alan Cavinder

Jason was born and raised in the small town of Sidney, Ohio. Though not from a family of legal professionals, he aspired from a young age to become an attorney — a dream that evolved from a passion for persuasion into a genuine commitment to help people. After graduating Cum Laude from Wright State University with a B.A. in Political Science, he earned his Juris Doctor from the University of Toledo College of Law in 2006, where he competed successfully on the national Moot Court circuit. He then joined the Montgomery County Prosecutor's Office, spending over four years gaining the trial experience that defines his practice — and his advantage — today.
