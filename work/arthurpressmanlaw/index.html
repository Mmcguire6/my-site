Featured Defense

The work that defines the practice.

Two intersecting specialties — one built from 35 years in Western New York courts, the other from serving a community of Canadian and out-of-state drivers.

Our Flagship

DWI Defense

Driving While Intoxicated

Evidence challenged. Charges dismissed. Licenses restored.

Driving While Intoxicated charges carry devastating consequences — license suspension, ignition interlock requirements, steep fines, and the permanent mark of a criminal record. Arthur Pressman has spent over 35 years mastering New York DWI law, aggressively challenging breath test evidence, contesting roadblock legality, and winning trials across Western New York courts. From a .23 BAC dismissed at trial to Canadian visitors charged after Bills games — he has seen it all and knows how to win.

  • DWI charges dismissed and DWAI non-criminal reductions secured at trial
  • Breath test and Breathalyzer evidence successfully challenged and suppressed
  • Refusal hearings won — client licenses restored by court order

Canadian & Out-of-State Drivers

Cross-Border Defense

Don't plead guilty. Don't travel back. Don't risk your license.

Out-of-state and Canadian drivers charged with a traffic violation in New York? We can help. Even when driving out of your state or country, you could face penalties back home upon conviction — demerit points, license suspension, insurance hikes under Ontario or Quebec rules. Arthur Pressman specializes in representing Canadian and out-of-state drivers in Western New York courts, often handling the entire case so you never have to travel back. Don't plead guilty.

  • Ontario, Quebec, and U.S. out-of-state drivers represented routinely
  • Tickets reduced to non-moving violations — no points transferred home
  • Court appearances handled remotely — you stay home, we go to court
