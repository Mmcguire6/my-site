№ 03 Services

Individual therapy & trauma-focused care

Two doorways into the same work — meeting teenagers and adults wherever they are.

i.

Teenagers

Individual therapy is helpful through the teenage years to address unique concerns at this stage of life to support them in becoming healthy adults. While parent-caregiver feedback is requested, sessions occur between the teenager and therapist.

ii.

Adults

Individual therapy is a collaborative process between the therapist and client that aims to facilitate healthy change and improve quality of life.

Clinical approach

i.

Eye Movement Desensitization Reprocessing (EMDR)

Originally developed to process trauma by identifying memories and reprocessing them — decreasing triggers and overwhelming feelings, with research-supported benefit for depression and anxiety.

ii.

Brainspotting (BSP)

A trauma therapy that helps identify access points in the brain where trauma memories are stored, to reprocess and decrease activation of those memories.

iii.

Emotionally Focused Individual Trauma Therapy (EFIT)

A trauma therapy that helps process emotions based on past traumatic experiences — especially helpful for childhood trauma.

iv.

Internal Family Systems (IFS)

Focused on helping our inner child heal from trauma so we can release negative beliefs and patterns of behavior.

v.

Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (TF-CBT)

Aimed at reducing emotional symptoms and unhealthy behaviors that result from trauma.

vi.

Circle of Security – Parenting (COS-P)

A parenting program that helps parents identify the meaning behind their child's behavior and empower different parenting choices.

vii.

Reiki

A spiritual Japanese healing technique — energy work that supports relaxation, natural healing processes, and the restoration of physical and emotional well-being.

viii.

EMDR & Attachment

When EMDR and attachment work are paired, it can have a lasting effect on how we respond to those in relationship with us.
