Why Goad & Goad

Three things, for over fifty years.

Cambridge has changed enormously since Gary Goad first opened his practice in Preston in 1969. The firm's commitments — community, breadth, and reliable counsel — have not.

01

Community Roots

Over Fifty Years of Cambridge Counsel

Gary Goad first opened his practice in Cambridge (Preston) in 1969, and the firm has been a fixture of the Waterloo Region's legal landscape ever since. When Tim Goad joined in 1980 — and Jim Renick and Steve Matheson followed — the firm deepened its commitment to the community it calls home. Clients return generation after generation because this firm has grown with Cambridge.

50+ Years in Practice
