A different kind of counseling

No labels. No diagnoses. No pathology — just you, and everything you already have to grow.

Jane practices counseling — not psychotherapy or psychiatry. She will not assess, label, analyze, categorize, diagnose, or medicate you. There is nothing "wrong" with you to be fixed.

Instead, her work is rooted in the Fendelman Metanoia Counseling Method for Transformation — helping you break self-sabotaging patterns, repair relationships, and step fully into your own power.

The result is a warm, judgment-free space where real change becomes not just possible, but expected.

The signature method

The Metanoia Method

Metanoia is a total reversal in thinking — a genuine spiritual awakening. Rather than managing symptoms, Jane helps you transform the inner story quietly shaping your life, so the change holds long after the work is done.

About Jane

Three decades of helping people heal

Jane Fendelman, MC, is a counselor, author, and speaker who has served more than 40,000 people. She earned a Bachelor of Science in Education from Southwest Missouri State University in 1982 and a Master of Counseling degree from the University of Phoenix in 1994.

An individual, marriage/couples, and child-family counselor, Jane is also a twice-certified Hospice Worker, a Grief Counselor, holds a Critical Incidence Debriefing Certification, and is an Omni-Denominational Minister. She works with the LGBTQ+ community and welcomes people of every background and belief.
