Our Approach

Four steps. Maximum result.

Everyone at our firm works as a team to help clients, and Attorney Edmonston personally handles every case. Here is how that process unfolds — from the first client conference to the maximum settlement.

01

Intake

Thorough Case Investigation

— the extensive client interview.

We start each case with an extensive client interview. The conference includes obtaining background information and assessing liability issues, injuries, and damages — reviewing medical itemizations, traffic collision reports, and employment records.

02

Strategy

Trial-Ready from Day One

We place a high emphasis on our trial skills. Attorney Edmonston prepares every case as if it will go to trial. This disciplined approach forces insurance companies to offer maximum value at every stage.
