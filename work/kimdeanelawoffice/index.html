Kim T. Deane M.A., LL.B.

Kim Tigre Deane is a graduate of the University of Windsor, holding an Honours Bachelor of Arts in Sociology and Criminology alongside her Bachelor of Laws and Master of Arts in Sociology — all earned concurrently. Called to the bar in 1997, Kim established her Tecumseh practice in 2009 after years of serving the Windsor-Essex community. Her approach to practice is to be direct, with an attention to detail, ensuring that complicated legal concepts are simplified and that her clients' best interests are always protected.
