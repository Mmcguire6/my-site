Our Approach

Three reasons to choose us over a big firm.

When you retain Leebody Yuan Law, you choose a small, well-run practice committed to one thing — getting the best result for your file, with no marketing budget or business overhead inflating the bill.

01

Principle One

Personal Responsibility

No sub-delegation. Ever.

When you choose our firm to represent you, Mr. Yuan will be personally responsible for your file. Our firm never sub-delegates, and you will never have an inexperienced junior associate learning the business on your dime.

02

Principle Two

Undivided Attention

One file at a time.

Our firm treats our clients equally despite the gravity of the offence. We know that if you are paying for a lawyer to professionally handle your file, you want the best results. This means we will handle one file at a time, and you will receive our fullest attention when we are working on your file.

03

Principle Three

Lower Fees

Quality without the big-firm markup.

We are a well-run company without a large business overhead, a costly marketing budget, or an expensive employee pay-roll — so our fees remain lower than a big firm without sacrificing quality.
