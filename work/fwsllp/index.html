Our Story

Forty years of east-central Alberta.

Our firm started in 1983 as Farnham Schaffter, a partnership between Scott Farnham and Jacqueline Schaffter. Scott Farnham continues to practice with the firm, while Jacqueline Schaffter is now a Justice of the Alberta Court of Justice.

We have grown over the years. We now have seven lawyers and 12 support staff. We believe our size is a significant benefit to our clients, as each lawyer is more able to focus on his or her preferred areas of practice, and we are able to provide continuity of service when one of us is away.

Our clients and colleagues refer their friends to us with confidence, knowing that they will be well looked after. We are a collegial and friendly place to work.
