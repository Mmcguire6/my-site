№ 02 Welcome

A neurodiversity-affirming practice for evaluation

Frye Psychological Services is a neurodiversity-affirming practice that provides comprehensive neuropsychological evaluations for children and adults — including adults being diagnosed for the first time. Our evaluations use clinical interviews, standardized questionnaires, and objective neurocognitive assessments to give a comprehensive view of an individual's neurocognitive profile alongside their social-emotional and behavioral functioning.

№ 04 PSYPACT & special programs

Access pathways built around how families really live

i.

PSYPACT

Targeted diagnostic evaluations for autism spectrum disorder are available via telehealth for adults in 36 states through PSYPACT participation.

ii.

Empowerment Scholarship Account (ESA) Approved Vendor

Frye Psychological Services is an approved vendor for ESA Program recipients through ClassWallet. Evaluations are an approved expense for ESA students with disability contracts (not covered under the universal ESA program).

iii.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

Under IDEA, parents have the right to obtain an IEE conducted by a qualified evaluator outside the public education agency, potentially at public expense, if they disagree with the school's evaluation.
