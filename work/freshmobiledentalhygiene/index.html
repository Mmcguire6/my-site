Services

The complete hygiene appointment.

Everything you'd expect from a hygiene visit at the dentist — delivered to you, by a registered hygienist.

Complete oral exam

A clinical check for anything abnormal in the mouth, teeth or tissues, plus an oral cancer screen. Any decay noted is communicated to you, with a referral form to take to your dentist.

Periodontal exam

Measuring the bone support around the teeth and assessing recession of the gum tissue. The measurements become a baseline for future visits, so we can ensure the periodontium is healthy or improving.

Scaling (debridement)

Removal of biofilm (plaque) and calculus (tartar) — deep cleaning above and beneath the gumline to help prevent periodontal disease and tooth decay.

Polishing

Removal of stain and biofilm (plaque) on the teeth — that fresh-from-the-hygienist finish.

Fluoride treatment

Fluoride to help prevent tooth decay. There are proven benefits for clients who are prone to cavities or have active cavities.

Sealants

Recommended for teeth with deep grooves at increased risk of cavities. A small amount of plastic resin is placed in the groove and cured with a light.

Oral education

Dental-hygiene education for you or a group — denture care, self-oral screening, pediatric or geriatric sessions, or whatever you need.

Treatment in your environment

We provide treatment in your environment — where it's comfortable and familiar to you. Most of Alberta covered.
