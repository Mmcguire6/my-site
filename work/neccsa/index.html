№ 02 Our mission

High-quality care, made accessible

At Northeast Counseling Center, our mission is to provide high-quality, compassionate care to individuals, couples, and families struggling with mental health issues. We believe that therapy should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or financial situation. We take a client-centered approach — our therapists are trained in a variety of evidence-based techniques and tailor their work to each person's goals.

№ 09 Common questions

Good to know

We're currently in network with Aetna, Blue Cross / Blue Shield of Texas and Anthem plans, Curative, Cigna, Community First Medicaid, United Healthcare, Optum and UMR, Traditional Medicare and AARP UHC Medicare plans, and Superior Medicaid. We do NOT accept BCBS Magellan plans under BCBS, Molina, Tricare West, Wellpoint, or Aetna CVS plans. Not all therapists accept all plans — please contact your own medical/mental health plan and verify that your specific therapist request is an in-network provider before calling NECC.

Our 2026 private-pay rates with Licensed Therapists are: Initial Intake (60 min) $165; Individual Follow-up (60 min) $120; Individual Follow-up (45 min) $105; Family Therapy or Couples Counseling (60+ min) $150; Brainspotting (60–90 min) $150–$200. Affordable associate-therapist rates (LPC-A, LMFT-A, LMSW, effective May 2023): Initial Intake (60 min) $75; Individual Follow-up (60 min) $75.

Yes, we accept Lyra employee assistance programs. Please get EAP approval and authorization codes prior to calling and scheduling. Some companies we see include H-E-B, Starbucks, Dell, AT&T, Apple, Capital Group, Walmart, Security Service, Frost Bank and META.

Yes, we offer telehealth therapy. We have morning appointments and some afterschool appointments available.
