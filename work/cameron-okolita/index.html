Flagship Solutions

Two paths out of debt — both federally protected.

For most Albertans, the right debt relief solution is either a Consumer Proposal or a Personal Bankruptcy — both administered by federally licensed professionals, both designed to stop creditor pressure, and both engineered to give you a genuine fresh start.

Most Popular

01 · Consumer Proposals

Consumer Proposal

Canada's best alternative to bankruptcy.

A Consumer Proposal allows you to pay a reduced portion of your debt, eliminate the interest you need to pay, and still allows you to maintain control over your assets and your life.

  • Avoid personal bankruptcy and keep your assets
  • Collector calls and lawsuits stop immediately upon filing
  • Eliminate interest and restructure into manageable monthly payments
Learn More
Fresh Start

02 · Personal Bankruptcy

Personal Bankruptcy

When a fresh start is the right answer.

Bankruptcy is a legal process in which you surrender certain of your assets to a Licensed Insolvency Trustee for disposition and distribution to your creditors in exchange for financial relief and a discharge from your debts. Immediate protection, federally supervised process, and a genuine path forward.

  • Immediate legal stay of proceedings protects you from creditors
  • Federally licensed professionals administer the entire process
  • Path to discharge and the beginning of credit rebuilding
