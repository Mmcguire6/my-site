The Lawyer

Meet Dennis L. Pollock.

Principal lawyer of Pollock & Company — called to the Alberta Bar in 1973, and practicing law in the city of Lethbridge since that time.

DP

Principal Lawyer

Dennis L. Pollock

B.Sc. · B.Ed. · LL.B.

Dennis L. Pollock graduated from the University of Alberta in 1972 with a Bachelor of Science, Bachelor of Education, and Bachelor of Law Degree. He was admitted to the Bar a year later, and has practiced law in the city of Lethbridge since that time.

His extensive involvement within the local community includes Past President of the Lethbridge Symphony Association, Past President of the Lethbridge Bar Association, Venture Leader with Scouts Canada, Vice President of the Waterton Park Community Association, Councilor for the Improvement District No. 4 in the Province of Alberta, Community Service Projects and Church activity.
