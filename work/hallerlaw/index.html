Meet Jack Haller.

Principal Counsel

Jack Haller

Mr. Haller has been a lawyer in Moncton since 2004. His objective is to help clients resolve their legal issues, whether through negotiated settlements or through Family Court. He has appeared in the Court of Queen's Bench of New Brunswick on numerous occasions and is currently a volunteer lawyer with the family law clinic at Support to Single Parents.

“Each client has unique circumstances. We provide legal services in a compassionate, patient and professional manner.”

Jack is a member of the Canadian Bar Association and the Law Society of New Brunswick, a Director of Support to Single Parents, and Past President of the Multicultural Association of the Greater Moncton Area. He has been a speaker on family law for the Law Society of New Brunswick. Jack enjoys spending his leisure time with his wife and two children, Samuel and Joshua.
