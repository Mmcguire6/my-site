Services

Thirteen practice areas, one Grande Prairie firm.

From the residential closing on a starter home to the agricultural land deal that's been three generations in the making — the legal work Peace Country actually needs, handled by lawyers cross-trained across the file.

i.

Real estate — the largest matters most clients ever sign

Residential Real Estate

Buying or selling a house, condominium, or rental property — conveyancing, financing, and the title work that gets keys into the right hands.

Corporate Real Estate

Commercial purchase, sale, and leasing — from retail space to industrial properties, with the diligence that protects your investment.

Agricultural Real Estate

Farmland purchase and sale, lease arrangements, and inter-generational transfers — the legal work that shapes Peace Country agriculture.

ii.

Business & estates — corporate counsel and family planning

Business Law

Incorporation, partnership and shareholder agreements, contracts, and ongoing corporate counsel — for the businesses that build the region.

Commercial Financing

Lending agreements, security registration, and the loan documentation that keeps a project moving from application through close.

Estate Planning

Wills, enduring powers of attorney, personal directives, and trust planning — with attention to tax and the realities of family transfers.

Estate Administration

Grants of probate, executor support, asset gathering, and distribution — through the most time-sensitive matters families face.

Adult Guardianship & Trusteeship

Applications to be appointed Guardian or Trustee for an adult who has lost capacity — representing families through Alberta's process.

iii.

Family, civil & criminal — representation when matters get contested

Family & Divorce

Separation, divorce, parenting and child support, spousal support, and property division — with discretion and steady representation.

Civil Litigation

Contract disputes, debt collections, commercial disagreements, and the courtroom advocacy that comes with them — Provincial and Court of King's Bench.

Mediation & Arbitration

Out-of-court resolution with certified mediators — for separating families, contract disputes, and matters that work better with conversation than confrontation.

Criminal Law

Representation for charges from impaired driving and assault to drug offences — protecting your record, your family, and your future.

Immigration

Permanent residency, work permits, family sponsorship, and the documentation that brings families together — or keeps them here.
