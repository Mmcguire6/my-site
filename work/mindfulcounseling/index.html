№ 02 Who we are

A warm, nonjudgmental space — a holistic "custom-fit"

Mindful Counseling is a place where healing and awareness begins with a therapist who is truly present and listening deeply to the experience of the client. Clients will experience a warm, nonjudgmental environment from a therapist that uses an eclectic approach to therapy, pulling from many methods that are uniquely appropriate to the client — thereby creating a holistic "custom-fit." As a client, you can learn ways to cultivate a rich and meaningful existence, and strategies on how to relate to those around you in a more positive way.

№ 08 Practice information

Good to know

Yes — in-network only. We're credentialed with Aetna (including Meritain), Blue Cross/Blue Shield (Commercial, Federal and Marketplace), New Mexico Medicaid (All Four Turquoise Plans and Exempt), Medicare (including BCBS Advantage & Presbyterian Senior Care), Molina Healthcare, Presbyterian Health Plan, and United Health Care (including AARP, GEHA, Oxford and UMR). We don't bill out-of-network policies.

Our Private Pay rate is $105 per session, collected at the time of service. Mindful Counseling's payment app, Ivy Pay, accepts all major credit cards.

Our providers primarily see adults, ages 18+. We also see a limited number of adolescents, ages 10+, on a case-by-case basis — and the adolescent must be willing to participate in the counseling process.

Our providers do not specialize in counseling services that are required by a 3rd party, including court-ordered services or counseling for clients currently experiencing legal issues. Our practice does not accept 3rd party referrals.
