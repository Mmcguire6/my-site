№ 02 Who we are

A connected network of independently licensed providers

Counseling Connections & Associates (CCA) provides high-quality mental health counseling and psychiatric services in Omaha, Nebraska. Our practice connects individuals, couples, families, children, and adolescents with experienced, independently licensed mental health and medical providers offering evidence-based treatment across a wide range of specialties.

Founded in 2011 by Dr. Kristi Tackett-Newburg, CCA was created to increase access to comprehensive mental health services in the Omaha community — a diverse network of licensed psychologists, therapists, counselors, and psychiatric providers, each maintaining independent clinical judgment while delivering compassionate, individualized care.
