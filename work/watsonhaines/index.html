Our Lawyers

Two founding partners. Three decades of Kamloops practice.

Gerald K. Watson and Derek Haines opened Watson & Haines on September 3, 2003. Both were called to the British Columbia Bar in 1996 — and both still personally handle every file the firm takes on.

GW

Founding Partner · Lawyer

Gerald K. Watson

LL.B. · Called to the BC Bar, 1996

Gerald Watson was born in Thunder Bay, Ontario and moved to Kamloops in 1977. He attended UBC Law and was called to the bar in 1996. Gerald is an active member of the community and has been a school board trustee for SD73 for 11 years, is a past President of the Rotary Club of Kamloops (17 year member) and a past President of Big Brothers Big Sisters of Kamloops and Region (17 year Director). Gerald has a solicitor's practice and works mainly in Residential and Commercial Real Estate Transactions, Wills and Estates, and Corporate and Trust Law.

DH

Founding Partner · Lawyer

Derek Haines

B.B.A. · LL.B. · Called to the BC Bar, 1996

Derek Haines was born and raised in Kamloops. He graduated from Simon Fraser University with a Bachelor of Business Administration Degree in 1991, attended law school at the University of British Columbia where he graduated with a Bachelor of Laws in 1995, and was called to the bar as a lawyer in 1996. Derek has continued to work as a lawyer in Kamloops ever since and is an active community volunteer with groups including Ducks Unlimited, Operation Rednose, and KIBIHT.
