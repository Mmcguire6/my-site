№ 02 Who we are

An evaluation-focused practice for youth & families

At Bridging the Gap Psychological Services, we are dedicated to providing precise and compassionate psychological evaluations to support youth and families. Founded by Dr. Aaron White, who brings a robust foundation in autism diagnostics, pediatric psychological evaluations, and behavioral interventions, our practice is committed to delivering timely and comprehensive assessments. By collaborating with a network of community applied behavioral analytic providers, therapists, pediatricians, and other professionals, we ensure that families can access essential services quickly — avoiding lengthy waitlists.

№ 04 Minimizing waitlists

The reason for our name

In the Omaha Metro, families wait eight months — sometimes more than a year — for an autism evaluation. Early intervention can't wait that long.

For the past three years, Dr. White has successfully maintained shortened wait times. We are committed to continuing this trend — scheduling evaluations as quickly as possible upon initial contact, and helping families move into the support services they need.

8 mos–1 yr

Typical Omaha-area wait. Ours is far shorter.

i.

Shortened Wait Times

Omaha Metro waitlists for autism evaluations can exceed eight months to a year. Dr. White has successfully maintained shortened wait times for over three years.
