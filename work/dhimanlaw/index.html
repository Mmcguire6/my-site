The Lawyer

Meet Inderjit Dhiman.

Founder, principal counsel, and the lawyer who will handle your file from first call to final signature.

Founding Lawyer · Principal Counsel

Inderjit Dhiman

B.A. · LL.B. · Barrister, Solicitor & Notary Public

Inderjit Dhiman is the founder and principal lawyer of Dhiman Law Professional Corporation. She is a dual-qualified lawyer entitled to practise law in India and Canada, and as a member in good standing with the Law Society of Ontario, she provides legal services across the province. A proud Indian-Canadian, Inderjit believes that cultural diversity is a collective strength, and she is dedicated to ensuring that every client — regardless of background — has access to quality, affordable legal representation.
