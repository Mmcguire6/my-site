About the park

A community park, run by the Kinsmen.

St. Albert Kinsmen RV Park is a 93-stall, full-service premier RV park operated by the Kinsmen Club of St. Albert — a registered non-profit and member of Kin Canada.

Open since its first full season in the summer of 2010, the park was built with community funding from the Kinsmen Club and a CAMRIF grant from the governments of Canada, Alberta and the City of St. Albert. It's part of the St. Albert Kinsmen Banquet Centre, RV & Community Park.

The setting

Quiet nature, minutes from the city.

The park sits among walking and cycling trails with bird- and nature-viewing areas nearby, with access to Red Willow Park and Lois Hole Centennial Provincial Park.

It's about 15 minutes to West Edmonton Mall and close to Edmonton and the wider Capital Region — quiet when you want it, the city when you need it. The Kinsmen Banquet Centre is a short walk to the east.
