№ 02 About High Desert Healing

A trusted home for mental health & wellness

High Desert Healing (HDH) is a private outpatient mental health and wellness clinic founded in 2016 by Lauren Gomez, LCSW, a licensed clinical social worker with over 20 years of experience in the mental health field. Lauren established HDH with a vision to create a safe, inclusive, and healing space for individuals and families navigating the challenges of mental health disorders — a trusted provider of high-quality, evidence-based care in the Albuquerque community and beyond.

№ 04 A specialty within a specialty

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation

An FDA-approved, non-invasive treatment that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain — primarily indicated for individuals experiencing treatment-resistant depression, particularly when medication or psychotherapy have not been effective.

  • i.
    FDA-approved & non-invasiveUses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells — no anesthesia, no medication required.
  • ii.
    For treatment-resistant depressionPrimarily indicated when medication or therapy has not provided adequate relief.

    Covered by most insurances
