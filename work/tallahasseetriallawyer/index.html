Our Approach

Personalized representation. No red tape.

Four commitments that have shaped how Thompson, Crawford, Brown & Smiley has worked with every client — from the first call through to closing argument.

01

Pillar I

Direct Attorney Access

No cutting through red tape.

There's no cutting through 'red tape' to talk to a seasoned attorney. When you call our office you will speak directly with the attorney who will be handling your legal matter.

02

Pillar II

Complimentary Consultation

No cost to lay the groundwork.

We want to help you lay the groundwork for your circumstances — that is why our initial consultation is complimentary to you. We look forward to being your legal team.

03

Pillar III

Personalized Representation

Your objectives, understood first.

Our clients are entitled to personalized attention by the attorney handling their matter and the right to select their attorney. Your objectives are defined and understood before representation begins.

04

Pillar IV

Full Transparency

Status, time, and fees — always.

You have the right to be advised of the status of our work, a detailed accounting of time spent, and an estimate of legal fees and costs at every stage of representation.
