Our Approach

Three commitments. Every client. Every case.

Most clients come to Sessions & Fleischman in an overwhelming situation. They need a lawyer who will think ahead, prepare thoroughly, and bring the experience needed to deliver results. Here is how we work.

01

Independent Investigation

We do not trust the police report alone.

Police reports do not always accurately reflect what happened. We conduct our own investigation — interviewing witnesses, hiring accident reconstruction experts, obtaining video evidence, and identifying every party responsible for your injuries and losses.

02

Negotiate on Your Behalf

Insurance companies do not have your interests at heart.

Insurance companies use manipulative, deceptive, and unscrupulous tactics to minimize what they pay. We calculate every economic and non-economic loss you are legally entitled to receive, and we strongly negotiate on your behalf to ensure you receive the compensation you deserve.

03

Trial-Ready from Day One

When the other side knows you'll go, the deal changes.

Ben Sessions has personally tried more than 60 jury trials across every level of Georgia's court system. We prepare every case as though it will be decided by a jury — because when insurance companies and prosecutors know your lawyer is genuinely willing to go to trial, they negotiate differently.
