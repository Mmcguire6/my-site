The Lawyer

Meet Peter C. Ghiz.

Barrister & Solicitor — three Dalhousie degrees, two bar admissions, and 25 years of independent practice on the Island.

PCG

Barrister & Solicitor · Principal

Peter C. Ghiz

B. Comm. · M.B.A. · L.L.B.

Peter graduated Dalhousie University in 1977 (B. Comm.), 1979 (M.B.A.) and 1988 (L.L.B.). He became a member of the Law Society of Prince Edward Island in 1989 and the Nova Scotia Barristers' Society in 1994. He is a member of the Canadian Bar Association, a past board member of the Community Legal Information Association and Hospice PEI, and currently sits on the Real Property and the Unauthorized Practice Committees of the Law Society of Prince Edward Island.
