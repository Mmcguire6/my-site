Biography

A career built from the inside out

From prosecutor's desk to the defense table.

After a year in private practice, Al Messer was recruited by the Buncombe County District Attorney's Office as a prosecutor, where he learned how prosecutors approach cases from the inside out. Knowing how the other side thinks allows him to see cases from both sides — identifying strengths and weaknesses in the prosecution's strategy — information that allows him to provide skilled defense representation to his clients.

He has successfully represented clients in hundreds of criminal trials, in cases ranging from traffic violations to homicide, in both state and federal court throughout Western North Carolina.
