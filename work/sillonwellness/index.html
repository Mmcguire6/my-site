№ 04 Why Sillon is different

Doesn't every therapist offer this?

Unfortunately, no. Not all therapists use evidence-based treatments — which means your treatment may or may not be effective. Here's what sets us apart.

  • i.

    Evidence-Based Treatment Only

    Every therapy offered is research-backed and matched to your specific difficulty — not a one-size-fits-all approach.

  • ii.

    Therapist Matching

    Our team reviews your individual requests and identifies a counselor who specializes in the challenges you identify as most important.

  • iii.

    PSYPACT Affiliated

    Our psychologists are affiliated with PSYPACT, enabling continuous client care across 24 states during life transitions.

  • iv.

    Military & Veterans Care

    Preferred Tricare and TRIWest provider with specialized support for active duty service members and veterans.

  • v.

    Teletherapy Available

    Access virtual therapy from home — convenient, quality care wherever you are in Texas.
