Practice Area II

Expungements

Clear your name — a fresh start.

Expungement is a legal process that forever clears or seals the records of certain misdemeanor and felony convictions. If you are convicted of a criminal offense, it becomes part of a permanent record seen by potential employers, schools, banking institutions, or by any agency requesting a background check. Criminal defense lawyer David Knoll works tirelessly to get records expunged whenever possible.

  • Misdemeanor and eligible felony expungements
  • Removes conviction from employment background checks
  • David Knoll manages the entire process on your behalf
