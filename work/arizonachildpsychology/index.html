№ 02 Who we are

A multidisciplinary practice, exclusively for young people

We are a multidisciplinary psychology and counseling practice comprised of licensed psychologists, certified school psychologists, professional counselors, and clinical social workers. Because we staff cases across disciplinary perspectives, our clients benefit from the comprehensiveness of our team approach — informed by evidence-based practices, with a focus on tangible, concrete solutions.
