The Lawyer

Meet Peter Shipanoff.

St. Albert raised, U of A trained — with a philosophical grounding and an extensive courtroom record.

Barrister & Solicitor

Peter Shipanoff

B.A. (Hons), First-Class · University of Alberta

Raised in St. Albert, Alberta, Peter obtained a Bachelor of Arts (Honors) from the University of Alberta with first-class Honours standing, where he studied legal philosophy under the tutelage of the eminent Dr. R.A. Shiner. He concentrated his law studies on criminal law and criminal litigation, complemented by practical experience as an active member in Student Legal Services.

Following his legal studies, Peter has dedicated himself to the practice of criminal law and has been counsel in many reported and precedent-setting decisions, successfully representing clients in every level of the court system across Alberta, British Columbia, Saskatchewan, the Yukon, and the Northwest Territories.
