№ 04 What is ADHD · symptoms

Major symptoms of ADHD

  • Trying to pay attention, but your mind drifts and you miss what was said
  • Starting tasks but not finishing them
  • Forgetfulness in daily activities — chores, returning texts, paying bills
  • Poor organizational skills
  • Avoidance of tasks requiring sustained mental effort
  • Often losing or misplacing things — keys, credit cards, homework
  • Easily distracted by background noises and activities
  • Excessive procrastination
  • Difficulty remembering what was just read
  • Hyperactivity & interrupting others when they're speaking
  • Difficulty unwinding and relaxing

Briana Mauldin-Looby

MS, LPC, ADHD-CCSP · Licensed Professional Counselor

An Oklahoman, Briana attended Southern Nazarene University, graduating Psi Chi with a B.S. in Psychology (Honors). She trained at MidAmerica Christian University as a psychotherapist under supervision at St. Anthony's Behavioral outpatient program and MidAmerica's Counseling program, graduating with a Master of Science in Counseling — clinical emphasis.

After graduation she was trained in giving mental health assessments at an agency in Oklahoma, and earned her certification as an ADHD-Certified Clinical Service Provider. After finishing her schooling, she learned she had ADHD herself — and it has since become her personal and professional mission to help others recognize and manage the lifelong condition.

№ 09 Good to know

Common questions

A substantial percentage of people who have been diagnosed with ADHD have reported feeling relieved after receiving the diagnosis. Stop questioning if you have ADHD and start getting the answers you need by getting tested. We are here to guide you through the process, to help mitigate the negative aspects of ADHD symptoms, and to help you tap into the strengths and benefits of ADHD in order to use it as your superpower.

Your assessment is performed by a Licensed Professional Counselor — ADHD-Certified Clinical Service Provider, who incorporates the latest research and up-to-date guidelines and testing practices in ADHD.
