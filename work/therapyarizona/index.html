№ 08 Common questions

Questions to ask your insurer

Services may be covered in full or in part by your health insurance or employee benefit plan. Please check your coverage carefully by asking the following questions.

Check with your insurer directly to confirm you have mental health insurance benefits under your current plan.

Ask your insurance provider what your deductible is and whether it has been met for the current benefit year.

Contact your insurer to confirm the number of covered therapy sessions per year under your plan.

Ask your insurer the specific dollar amount or percentage they will cover per therapy session.

Some insurance plans require a referral from your primary care physician before beginning therapy — check with your plan directly.
