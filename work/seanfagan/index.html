Driving Charges & Impaired Driving

A complex, constantly changing area of criminal law.

Impaired driving law is complex and constantly changing. No criminal offence affects more Canadians than driving charges. One drink too many, an error in judgment, or momentary inattention can result in disaster — and the consequences of conviction are severe: criminal record, mandatory driving prohibition, potential jail time, and career consequences for commercial operators.

  • Commercial “Over .08” resolved by traffic ticket — no criminal record, no jail, no licence suspension
  • Impaired Driving Causing Bodily Harm — non-custodial sentence achieved
  • Dangerous Driving Causing Bodily Harm — resolved by traffic ticket
