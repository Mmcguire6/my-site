№ 02 Who we are

A behavioral health clinic, rooted in community outreach

The Capelli Group, LLC — a BIPP, Domestic Violence, Anger Management and Mental Health agency — was initially known as EPIC Association, established in 2004 by Professor Samuel Capelli to provide community outreach education, aid in reducing high-risk activities and violent behaviors, and promote healthy decision-making.

In October 2014, Professor Samuel Capelli and Vice President Sandra Capelli expanded their innovative ideas and changed the agency's name to The Capelli Group, LLC. Today we provide more in-depth assistance to those searching for a better way of life through awareness and education — collaborating closely with family courts, probation departments, and other State and Federal agencies to help abolish family violence and unhealthy decision-making.
