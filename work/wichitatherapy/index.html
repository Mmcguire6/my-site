№ 04 A specialty within a practice

Specialized modalities

A few of the approaches our therapists are trained in — chosen carefully for the people and situations they fit best.

i.

Brainspotting

Locates points in the client's visual field that help to access unprocessed trauma in the subcortical brain.

ii.

EMDR®

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing — developed to help people recover from disturbing, life-changing events by reprocessing traumatic memories.

iii.

Sand Tray

An expressive method of therapy often used with children, but can be applied to adults, teens, couples, families, and groups as well.

iv.

Play Therapy

Allows therapists to understand the child's world and help them make sense of it — play is a child's language and toys are their words.

№ 08 Common questions

Good to know

Each therapist's page has more information about the age group they work with, insurance plans they accept, and their specialties. We also have information about some of the specialized treatments we offer and the therapists who provide each type of therapy under our "services" tab. Feel free to contact the therapist directly to see whether they would be a good fit for your needs and therapy goals.

Accepted insurance plans vary from therapist to therapist.
