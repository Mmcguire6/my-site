Personal Injury & Car Wrecks

Aggressive representation against insurers and corporate defendants.

Personal injury lawsuits are filed by people injured due to the negligence of someone else. The injury may be either physical or emotional, arising from automobile accidents, 18-wheeler collisions, assaults on business premises, medical malpractice, or product liability. The goal is to determine who was responsible and compel them to compensate the injured person for all losses sustained. Tatum & Wade brings the expertise, technology, and tenacity required to take on large corporations and insurance companies.
