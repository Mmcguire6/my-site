№ 02 About Dr. Soward

A psychologist on a quest to empower

Let me just start off by saying that I love what I do. I've been offering psychotherapy to individuals and families for over 20 years, with great compassion for people facing difficult life experiences. I help people navigate tough transitions, recover from perfectionism, achieve healthier relationships, heal from the pain of past trauma, and tune in to their true self to make powerful changes in their lives.

My background stems from positive psychology and resilience, and my approach is focused on solutions and choices that will change your life for the better. I particularly enjoy working with high-energy, achievement-oriented people who may be finding themselves stuck — to help them get more out of their lives, well-being, work, and relationships.
