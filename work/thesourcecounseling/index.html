№ 03 What I offer

Counseling services

At The Source, I offer a variety of counseling services that range from individual work to family counseling to play therapy with younger children. We can discuss your options in more detail during your intake session.

i.

Individual Counseling

One-on-one work for adults navigating life's challenges, growth, and transitions.

ii.

Play Therapy

Developmentally-attuned therapy with younger children, through the language of play.

iii.

Family Counseling

Whole-family work — communication, connection, and the everyday dynamics of home.

iv.

Pre-adolescent / Teen Counseling

A safe space for tweens and teens to be heard and to build resilience.

v.

Couples Counseling

For couples working through conflict, repair, or simply growing together with intention.
