№ 04 Certified EFT & EMDR

Certified Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) Therapist

I am a Certified Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) Therapist who has been evaluated as competent by the International Centre for Excellence in Emotionally Focused Therapy (ICEEFT). EFT is a unique and highly effective form of therapy that helps people understand and improve their emotional connection with themselves and others.

  • i.
    ICEEFT Certification
    Evaluated as competent by the International Centre for Excellence in Emotionally Focused Therapy. Research shows 70–75% of couples move from distress to recovery, with approximately 90% showing significant improvements.
  • ii.
    EMDR
    Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing — a trauma-focused modality integrated alongside EFT to help the mind heal from disturbing life experiences.
