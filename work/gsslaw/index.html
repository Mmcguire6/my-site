What We Do

A full range of legal services.

From real estate transactions to courtroom representation — GSS Law Firm provides considered, professional counsel across the matters Albertans rely on.

Real Estate Law

Residential sales and purchases, commercial leases, and commercial transactions — handled with care from offer to closing.

Immigration Law

Permanent residency, work and study permits, family sponsorship, and citizenship — guidance through Canada's evolving immigration landscape.

Family Law

Divorce, separation, custody, child and spousal support, and matrimonial property matters — guided with sensitivity and discretion.

Civil Law

Disputes between individuals, businesses, and institutions — from breach of contract claims to commercial litigation in Alberta courts.

Criminal Law

Defence representation across criminal matters — from impaired driving and assault charges through to serious indictable offences.

Notary Services

Notary public services and commissioning of oaths — documents authenticated for use in Alberta and abroad.
