Our Approach

Client-first. Resource-rich.

Four principles that have shaped how Childers & McCain handles every file — from the initial consultation through verdict.

01

Pillar I.

Client First, Always

The legal process, less stressful.

We strive to make the legal process less stressful and encourage our clients to let us handle their legal worries so they can focus on their recovery. It is important for every family to have a lawyer whom they trust and who they can call when they need representation.

02

Pillar II.

Full-Resource Representation

From day one to verdict.

Our firm has the resources to fully support your case from start to finish. We work with accident reconstruction specialists, hire investigators, and ensure critical evidence is preserved. We also prepare evidentiary and trial exhibits and consult with medical experts.

03

Pillar III.

No Fee Unless We Win

No risk in reaching out.

You won't owe us any attorney's fee in a personal injury case unless we recover compensation for you. Your initial consultation is always free, so there is no risk in reaching out and sharing your story with our team.

04

Pillar IV.

Trusted Statewide & Nationwide

Co-counsel partner of choice.

Georgia law allows a division of fees between lawyers who are not in the same firm. We routinely co-counsel with lawyers who have reached out to us for our experience and resources in handling personal injury and truck accident claims across Georgia.
