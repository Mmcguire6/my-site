Preferred Approach

Practice Area · Dispute Resolution

Mediation Services

Resolving conflict together — without the courtroom.

Ashford Law is pleased to provide mediation services to those who wish to navigate their differences through interest-based negotiation. A mediator cannot provide legal advice but is trained to assist the parties in coming to agreements together in a fair, diplomatic and reasonable way — resolving conflict without the cost and adversity of courtroom proceedings. Ferne is a member of Family Mediation Canada (FMC) and has continuing legal education in this ever-growing area of dispute resolution.

  • Interest-based negotiation
  • Fair, diplomatic & cost-effective process
  • Mutual agreements reached together
