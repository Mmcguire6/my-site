№ 02 Who we are

Promoting intimate connections

Cedar Rapids Relationship Center was created from the minds of Marriage and Family Therapists. As MFTs, we believe an important piece that is often overlooked is the impact that our relationships have on us. Whether it is depression, anxiety, or self esteem — changing how we connect to people around us makes a difference.

We are a therapy office of independent therapists with a special interest in relationships. While we work with the majority of mental health diagnoses and life stressors, we specialize in working with all relationships and couples, traditional and non-traditional.

№ 04 A distinct approach

Why Marriage & Family Therapy?

Different mental health professions take different approaches — here is what shapes ours.

i.

Systemic, Relational Training

Marriage and Family Therapists are trained in systemic, or relational therapy, and believe that throughout life we exist in a number of relationships that directly and indirectly impact our wellbeing.

ii.

All Relationships Welcome

We specialize in working with all relationships and couples, traditional and non-traditional.

iii.

Experiential Approach

Using a systemic framework as well as an experiential approach, to help our clients improve their relationships and make lasting changes in their lives.

iv.

Clinical Supervision

Supervision available for therapists seeking licensure or professional development.

№ 08 Common questions

Good to know

Your first meeting is a chance to share what's bringing you in and for your therapist to learn about the relationships and patterns shaping your life right now. There's no pressure to have it all figured out — your provider will walk you through what to expect when you schedule.

You don't need your partner present for relational work to be meaningful. Marriage and Family Therapists are trained to work systemically — even with one person in the room, we can help you understand the patterns at play and what shifts feel possible from your side.

MFTs are trained in systemic, or relational, therapy — we believe that throughout life we exist in a number of relationships that directly and indirectly impact our wellbeing. We use a systemic framework alongside an experiential approach to help clients improve their relationships and make lasting changes.

Each of our therapists is independent. Choose the provider you'd like to work with and call or email them directly — their numbers are listed throughout this page and in the Contact section below.

Because each therapist runs their own practice, fees and accepted forms of payment are set individually. Contact your provider directly for fee and payment information — a Good Faith Estimate is available on request.
