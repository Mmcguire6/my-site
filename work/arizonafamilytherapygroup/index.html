№ 02 Who we are

A co-op of therapists — learning & healing, side by side

Arizona Family Therapy Group is a network of therapists that create a place of learning and a place of healing for individuals, couples, and families. Each therapist is independent and brings their own strengths and expertise — and seeks consultation and refers among the group to give clients the best quality of care.

Collectively, the therapists strive to strengthen families through quality education — helping couples strengthen their marriage and helping parents gain the skills necessary to raise their children in a loving way that motivates them to become capable, responsible, and respectful.

№ 04 Parenting education & community groups

More than the therapy room

A family-first program of classes, groups, and in-home support — built around the conviction that families grow strongest when parents are equipped, supported, and not walking the path alone.

  • i.

    Love & Logic Parenting Classes

    Evidence-based parenting classes designed to raise capable, responsible, and respectful children.

  • ii.

    In-Home Child & Family Counseling

  • v.
