№ 04 An evidence-based therapy approach

What is EMDR?

EMDR — Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing — is an evidence-based therapy that helps the mind move through experiences that feel stuck. Ask our team whether this approach is available at our practice.

  • i.
    For trauma & anxietyAn approach particularly suited to processing distressing memories and the responses that follow.
  • ii.
    Ask about availabilityContact us to find out whether this approach is offered and may be a good fit for your goals.

    We'll help you decideReach out and we'll match you with the right clinician and approach for your goals.

№ 07 Insurance & fees

Clear, accessible coverage

In-network with several plans — with a straightforward self-pay option if we're not.

i. Insurance

Walsh Counseling Services is in-network with several insurance plans. Commonly accepted plans may include:

  • Blue Cross / Blue Shield
  • Presbyterian
  • Turquoise Health Care Medicaid
  • Optum
  • Molina
  • Magellan

Not sure if we take your plan? Send us a message and we'll help verify benefits and match you with a clinician.

ii. Self-pay

A straightforward private-pay option for anyone out-of-network, or who prefers not to use insurance.

Self-Pay Session

Per session · private pay
$120

Insurance participation can vary by clinician, so we'll confirm coverage when you contact us. If we're out-of-network for your plan, we may still be able to offer private-pay options.

Important notes. Being in-network does not guarantee coverage; benefits depend on your specific plan. Clients are responsible for copays, coinsurance, and deductibles as outlined by their plan.
