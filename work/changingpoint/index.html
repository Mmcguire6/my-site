№ 04 Therapeutic approaches

Three traditions of practice

A holistic, meaning-centered framework drawn from family-systems thinking, psychosocial rehabilitation, and the facilitation work of PSYCH-K.

i.

Bowen Family Systems Theory

Trained through The Bowen Center for the Study of the Family — a way of understanding individuals as part of the emotional systems that shape them. thebowencenter.org

ii.

Psychosocial Rehabilitation

Applied across hospital, residential, community, and private-practice settings — supporting recovery, resilience, and meaningful day-to-day functioning.

iii.

PSYCH-K®

A facilitator of PSYCH-K, a process for noticing and shifting the subconscious beliefs that shape how we move through life. psych-k.com
