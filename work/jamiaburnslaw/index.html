ESTATE ADMINISTRATION

Featured Practice

Probate support for families.

After the passing of a family member, it may be necessary to go through probate and the firm can assist you with this process. Probate is the legal procedure for administering an estate, which involves collecting assets, identifying beneficiaries, notifying and paying creditors, filing tax returns and paying taxes due, and distributing the remainder of the assets to the proper beneficiaries.

  • Asset collection & estate inventory
  • Creditor notification & payment coordination
  • Beneficiary distribution & tax filings
