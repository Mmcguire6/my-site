№ 02 Welcome — meet Mark

Forty years counseling couples — and one wife I'd marry again.

My name is Mark Oelze. I have been counseling couples for 40 years and have been married 44 years to the woman I married right out of college. She was my best friend then and still is. That doesn't mean we haven't had difficulty or conflict — we have. But we have grown through it all and want to share all we have learned with you. Though not limited to such, my expertise is in helping couples resolve conflict, communicate, and connect using PLEDGEtalk: a six-step guide for the conversation.

№ 03 Our ideal fit

A small roster, by design.

I work with only a few select clients at a time — engaged, dating, or married couples ready for deep, focused work.

  1. i.

    Couples who know there is more to experience in their communication and are ready to work at deep personal growth.

  2. ii.

    Who are ready and willing to take an inside look at their own heart and admit what they see isn't always pretty.

  3. iii.

    Who want to master communication and do whatever it takes to do so — committing to the weekly gathering, reading, studying, and practicing what they learn.

  4. iv.

    Who know this is easily worth the investment of finances, time, energy, and focus.

  5. v.

    Who want to experience the greatest possible connection with their spouse here on earth.

  6. vi.

    Who want to pass what they learn on to their children — and share it with others who also want their marriage and family to be all it can be.
