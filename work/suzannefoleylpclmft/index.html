№ 02 Who I am

A counseling practice for adults, children & families

The counseling practice of Suzanne Foley, M. Ed. includes adults and children and focuses on individual, marriage, and family counseling. I believe that change occurs most readily for clients when they experience the therapist as caring, genuine, congruent, empathetic, and accepting — warm, concrete, respectful, and positive.

My approach is grounded in cognitive-behavioral work: the way people interpret what they think affects how they feel, and how they feel shapes how they react. I accept clients who believe they have the capacity to resolve their own problems with my assistance.

№ 04 A particular kind of care

Faith-integrated counseling

For clients who value faith integration in their counseling, therapy can be informed by Christian-based beliefs and grounded in the client's own belief system and values — alongside the cognitive-behavioral methods at the heart of the work.
