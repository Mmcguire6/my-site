Areas of Practice

Full-service counsel, litigation at the core.

Sockett Law practices in most areas of law with a particular focus on litigation — from criminal defence through to civil claims and personal injury matters.

Criminal Law

If you have been charged with a criminal offence, you need the proper representation to ensure that you obtain the best possible outcome. Our lawyers have years of experience dealing with criminal law matters up to and including trial matters.

Criminal Defence Trial Charges

Personal Injury

If you have suffered a personal injury, you should contact our team as soon as possible. Our lawyers will be able to guide you through the process in order to get you reimbursed for what you have suffered.

Injury Claims Compensation Litigation

Family Law

Whether you are having difficulty with custody or simply need to finalize a divorce, having proper representation is the best way to ensure that you do not endure needless stress or expense. Our lawyers have the experience necessary to help you navigate the process.

Divorce Separation Custody

Civil Litigation
