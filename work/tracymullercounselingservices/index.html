№ 02 About the practice

Authenticity, acceptance & a strengths-based approach

As a therapist I enjoy working with a variety of groups but particularly enjoy working with adults navigating the many ups and downs of life. I primarily work with clients experiencing anxiety, depression, life adjustments, interpersonal conflict, relationship issues, and women's issues — using a person-centered and solution-focused approach to help individuals discover their strengths and overcome daily challenges.

№ 05 Meet your therapist

Tracy Muller

M.S. · Licensed Independent Mental Health Clinician

I have a Masters of Science in Clinical Counseling and am a Licensed Independent Mental Health Clinician. Authenticity and acceptance are essential components of a successful therapeutic relationship — as well as my personal counseling style. Throughout the therapeutic process, my goal is for clients to gain insight about their experiences, emotions, and thoughts to facilitate new responses to old patterns of behavior. Together, we can approach a treatment plan to meet your needs.

Primary modalities

  • i. Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)
  • ii. Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
  • iii. The Gottman Method
  • iv. EMDR — trauma reprocessing
