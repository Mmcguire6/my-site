Real Estate

Over 125 years of combined experience — from a starter home to a Fortune 500 closing.

Our Real Estate practice group represents individuals and entities in the purchase, finance, lease, sale, and development of commercial properties. For decades, Evans Fox attorneys have assisted clients by preparing and negotiating commercial leases, purchase and sale agreements, loan documents, joint venture agreements, easements, property restrictions and nearly every other form of real property encumbrance — from small multi-family housing to large-scale multi-use commercial developments. We also serve as a title agency for Stewart Title Insurance Company.
