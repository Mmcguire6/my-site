About the park

A family-friendly community park.

Leduc Lions Campground & RV Park is owned and operated by the Leduc Lions Club — a true family-friendly location with full-service RV sites, tenting and overflow, and plenty for the kids to do.

It's right next to the Leduc Recreation Centre, with two playgrounds, two pickleball courts and a recreational area on the grounds — and it's recognized with a 4-star Woodall's rating and green-park certification for environmental sustainability.

At a glance

  • Owned & operated by the Leduc Lions Club
  • 15-amp partial to 50-amp full-service sites
  • Tenting & RV overflow available
  • Adjacent to the Leduc Recreation Centre
  • The Leduc Lions Hall available for rentals

Before you arrive

Good to know.

A few clear rules keep the park calm, safe and family-friendly.

1

Check-in & out

Summer check-in 3–7pm; spring/fall 3–5pm. Check-out 12 noon — after 3pm a full day is charged.

2

Quiet hours

Quiet time 11:00pm–8:00am. Visitors to your site must leave the park by 11pm.

3

Pets

Maximum 2 dogs per site, registered and leashed, barking controlled, never left unattended. Clean up in dumpsters.

4

Speed limit

10 km/h, strictly enforced — speeding can result in eviction without refund.

5

Vehicles

Max 2 guest vehicles per site if space allows. Visitors park in visitor parking — no visitor vehicles at sites.

6

Fires

Campfires only in the fire pits provided and fully extinguished. Outside firewood prohibited; available at the office.

7

Occupancy

One sleeping unit per site (trailer or tent) unless prior arrangements are made.

8

Minors

No one under 18 may camp unless accompanied by a parent or guardian.

9

Booking

Same-day reservations must be completed by 4:30pm. Aircraft noise is possible — the park is near the airport.
