№ 04 Treatment approach

Evidence-based, & respectfully your own

Treatment is grounded in empirical, research-based interventions delivered with respect for your identity, culture, values, and belief systems. Together, we develop tools you can use now and work toward solutions that leverage your strengths.

i.

Cognitive Behavioral (CBT)

Evidence-based, targets thought-behavior patterns.

ii.

Interpersonal

Focuses on relationships and communication.

iii.

Motivational Interviewing

Collaborative, client-driven goal exploration.

iv.

Person-Centered

Non-judgmental, client-led self-discovery.

v.

Psychoanalytic

Explores deeper patterns and their origins.

vi.

Strength Based

Leverages existing strengths toward growth.

№ 05 About me

Robert Reed, LSCSW

Licensed Specialist Clinical Social Worker10+ years in practiceWichita-born

Born and raised in Wichita, for over a decade I have been providing therapy services to people who are dealing with a variety of issues, like anxiety and depression. Many of the problems people present with can be viewed as unhealthy, yet normal adjustments to life's challenges. These adjustments may have become problematic and created concerns for you or in your relationships.

Together, we will create a supportive, non-judgmental atmosphere for you to work through life's struggles, help develop tools you can use now, and work toward solutions to leverage your strengths.
