What We're Known For

Four pathways. One trusted guide.

From the moment you begin assessing your options to the day you land in Canada, our work falls along four well-trodden lines — the streams where Youness Canada Immigration has built its reputation.

01 Express Entry

Canada's fastest pathway to permanent residency

Express Entry is Canada's flagship skilled worker immigration system, and Youness Canada Immigration has guided clients through every stage — from CRS score assessment to Invitation to Apply. Our consultant navigated this route personally before becoming RCIC-regulated, giving clients an advisor who has lived the process.

RCIC Regulated
02 Work Permits

Temporary work — your fastest route to landing

Temporary employment is one of the best ways to start an immigration process in Canada. A work permit allows you to familiarize yourself with the country, build Canadian work experience, and strengthen your permanent residency profile for the future.

Open & Employer-Specific
03 Family Sponsorship

Reunite your family on Canadian soil

If you are a Canadian citizen or Permanent Resident, you can sponsor your loved ones to become Permanent Residents. Our team manages every element of the sponsorship file — from eligibility assessment to document preparation and government submission.

Citizens & PRs Welcome
04 Study Permits

Build your future through Canadian education

To study in Canada, you must obtain a Canadian study permit, which serves as a Canadian student visa for the duration of your stay. A well-prepared study permit application opens the door not only to world-class education but to post-graduation work and eventual permanent residency.

Student-to-PR Pathways
