Structured Repayment

Chapter 13 Bankruptcy

Pay your fees through the plan itself.

Chapter 13 attorney fees can be handled through a Chapter 13 plan. The law allows Chapter 13 attorney fees to be paid through the payments made in a Chapter 13 plan. For Wyoming filers who can't afford the full Chapter 7 fee upfront — or who need to protect specific assets — Chapter 13 offers a structured path that meets you where you are.

  • Attorney fees paid through the Chapter 13 plan itself
  • Structured repayment that protects key assets
  • Affordable payment arrangements throughout the case
