Founder & Principal Lawyer

Rani El Khatib

J.D. · M.A. · B.Sc.

Before becoming a lawyer, Rani spent 15 years as a university professor and business consultant, enriching his skill set and gaining invaluable experience. He holds a Juris Doctor (J.D.) degree from Thompson Rivers University, complemented by a Master's degree from the University of Texas at Austin and a Bachelor's degree from the American University of Beirut. Rani's legal practice primarily focuses on corporate tax law, intergenerational business transfer, wills and estates, contracts, and family law agreements — and he is fluent in English, French, and Arabic.
