About AILC

Immigration is complicated. You don't have to handle it alone.

Do you understand all the pitfalls and detailed requirements? Make sure you don't accidentally cause a delay or even denial of your case. Let us guide you safely through your immigration process.

Alaska Immigration Law Center is Anchorage's dedicated immigration law center — serving the unique needs of Alaskans and clients across the United States and around the globe. Our sister firm, United States Immigration Law Center (USILC), extends that reach to every corner of the world.

Legal Services

Achieve your immigration goals with ease and confidence.

For yourself, your loved ones, or your business — we handle every area of US immigration law with precision and genuine care.

Family Immigration

Are you separated from loved ones? Do you have a spouse, child, step-child, parent, or sibling who needs a green card to stay legally in the US? We'll guide you through the procedure to get permission for your family members to live here in America so you're together again.

Green Cards Spousal Visas Family Reunification

Business & Employment Immigration

Do you need to obtain an employment-based green card, E-2 Treaty Investor visa, or E-1 Treaty Trader visa so you can legally work, trade, or employ others in the US? We can facilitate your successful application so you can prosper in America.

E-2 Visa E-1 Visa Work Permits

Immigration Appeals

Have you received a denial of your immigration case? We'll help you quickly request an appeal, so you have the best chance of overturning your denial. The opportunity for appeal is time-limited, so contact us right away.

Denial Reversal Appeals Urgent

Short-Term Visitor Visas

Would you like to visit the US for a short time for pleasure, business, or for a medical procedure? We'll suggest the best kind of visa for you, and advise you of all requirements and necessary documentation so you can focus on your travel plans.

B-1 B-2 Visitor Visas

Waivers

Do you need special forgiveness from the US government because you violated an immigration regulation? Small mistakes, or circumstances from your past, can result in inadmissibility to the US, but there may be a waiver that can help you. We'll identify any waivers that may allow you to successfully enter the US.

Inadmissibility I-601 Forgiveness

Citizenship

Are you ready to enjoy the full benefits of United States citizenship? We'll show you the steps to take so you can experience US citizenship with all its rights and freedoms.

Naturalization US Passport Civics Test
