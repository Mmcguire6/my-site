Client-First Pricing

Fixed fees, no ticking clock — transparent pricing from day one

A fixed fee arrangement is an alternative to the standard hourly rate used by most solicitors; it is a fair approach to pricing legal services. Travers Law has fixed closing costs so at the end of the day, there are no surprises — the figure you see at the start is the figure you settle on.

Our Team

Meet the partners at Travers Law.

Forty-plus years of consistent practice under the lead of Don Travers, joined by Bryan Mayes — a partner with a keen interest in residential and commercial real estate across the Golden Horseshoe.

DT

Managing Partner

Don Travers

Don graduated from Queen's University after obtaining his Juris Doctorate in 1972 before being called to the Bar in 1974. He has practiced in many legal sectors, including commercial and residential real estate, corporate law, commercial and bank financing, franchising, and wills and estates. In 1981, Don founded Travers Law (then known as Paquette Travers) and has been consistently practising for over 40 years. He served as Chair of the Finance Committee of both the City of Kitchener and the Region of Waterloo during most of his terms on the council.

Education Queen's University, J.D. 1972
Bar Ontario, 1974
Focus Real Estate · Corporate · Commercial Financing · Wills & Estates
BM

Partner

Bryan Mayes

Bryan completed his undergraduate degree at Queen's University and his law degree at the University of Windsor. He was called to the Ontario Bar in 2015, initially focusing on residential and commercial real estate and corporate law at a well-respected local Hamilton law firm, before proudly joining Travers Law in 2017. Bryan currently focuses his practise on residential and commercial real estate law throughout the Golden Horseshoe and also practises corporate law and estate planning. He understands the importance of thorough and thoughtful representation when entering into any agreement concerning real property.
