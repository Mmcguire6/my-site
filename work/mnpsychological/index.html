№ 02 Our treatment philosophy

Professionalism, fairness, evidence-based assessment

Our evaluation philosophy is grounded in professionalism, fairness, and evidence-based assessment. We understand the unique demands placed on individuals working in law enforcement, security roles, and other public-safety positions. Our role is to provide clear, objective evaluations that support both individual well-being and organizational safety.

We approach each assessment with neutrality and respect — offering a balanced, accurate understanding of psychological functioning as it relates to job-relevant competencies, not to pathologize normal reactions to high-stress work.

№ 04 A specialty within the practice

Public Safety Psychology

A psychological evaluation is required of all licensed peace officers and all police communication dispatchers in the state of Texas. We provide clear, objective evaluations that support both individual well-being and organizational safety.

  • i.

    L-3 Police Officer Evaluations

    Required for all licensed peace officers and police communication dispatchers in Texas; completed in approximately 2 hours.

  • ii.

    Level 3 & Level 4 Security Officer Evaluations

    Psychological evaluations for Level 3 and Level 4 security officers.

  • iii.

    Fitness for Duty Evaluations

    For officers and dispatchers dealing with stress, anger management, anxiety, depression, or interpersonal concerns.

№ 09 Common questions

Good to know

The L-3 is comprised of a review of background information, two psychological tests, and a clinical interview. Altogether, the evaluation takes approximately 2 hours to complete.

We are typically able to schedule the L-3 within one week or less of contacting our office.

In addition to the L-3, we also provide Fitness for Duty (FFD) evaluations for police officers and dispatchers dealing with stress, anger management, anxiety, depression, interpersonal problems, or other psychological concerns. The FFD evaluation will vary depending on the nature of the concern, so please contact our office for more information.

We rely on validated assessment tools, structured clinical interviews, and established professional guidelines to ensure that our conclusions are consistent, defensible, and aligned with best practices. We recognize that participating in a psychological evaluation may feel stressful. We strive to create an environment that is transparent, professional, and respectful.
