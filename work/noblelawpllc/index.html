Divorce & Dissolution

Divorce & Dissolution of Marriage

No-fault, no judgment, no surprises.

Iowa is a no-fault divorce state, meaning either party can file for dissolution of marriage without stating a reason. Noble Law guides clients through asset division, parenting plan negotiations, and spousal support determinations — all with the clarity of flat-fee pricing from day one.

  • No-fault filing — no reason required under Iowa law
  • Flat-fee pricing so you always know your cost upfront
  • Bilingual support available in Spanish and Vietnamese

Protecting Your Children

Child Custody & Parenting

Child Custody & Parenting

Legal custody, physical care, best interests.

Iowa distinguishes between legal custody — the right to access records and make major decisions — and physical care, where the child lives day-to-day. Noble Law advocates for parenting arrangements that genuinely protect each child's best interests and preserve meaningful family bonds.

  • Legal and physical custody guidance for Iowa families
  • Emergency custody orders when children face immediate risk
  • Parental alienation intervention and court enforcement

Practice Areas

The full range of Iowa family law.

From divorce to guardianship to the newly added Life and Legacy Estate Planning practice — Noble Law offers flat-fee representation across every matter Iowa families need most.

I

Divorce

Iowa is a no-fault divorce state. An individual does not need to have a reason to seek a divorce — either party can file for dissolution of marriage.

Dissolution No-Fault Asset Division

II

Child Custody

There are two types of custody in Iowa: Legal Custody — the right to access a child's confidential records and make major decisions — and Physical Care, where the child resides.

Legal Custody Physical Care Best Interests
Learn More
III

Domestic Abuse

Iowa courts take domestic abuse situations seriously. No person has a right to put their hands on another in a violent manner. Noble Law provides strong, compassionate representation.

Protective Orders Safety Emergency Relief
Learn More
IV

Spousal Support

Iowa categorizes three types of spousal support in a dissolution proceeding: Reimbursement support, Traditional Alimony, and Transitional support.

Alimony Reimbursement Transitional
Learn More
V

Modifications

Child support can be modified if either party has an increase or decrease in income that would result in a ten (10) percent change. Parenting and custody orders may also be modified when circumstances substantially change.

Child Support Custody Post-Decree
Learn More
VI

Emergency Family Law

Noble Law recognizes that there are times that are emergencies when children are involved. If a parent has taken a child out of state and will not return the child, an emergency can be filed with the court.

Emergency Orders Child Safety Urgent Relief

VII

Parental Alienation

Parental Alienation can occur when one parent purposely and intentionally attempts to push the other parent out of the child or children's life.
