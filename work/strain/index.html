Our Approach

Three commitments, woven into every conversation.

Every client at Strain and Company is met with the same three principles — the foundation of how Elisabeth has practised law since 1989.

01

Principle I

Compassion

Patience, empathy, and care.

We understand that legal matters often arise during life's most difficult moments. Every client at Strain and Company is met with patience, empathy, and genuine care for their family's outcome.

02

Principle II

Inclusivity

Every family welcome.

We welcome all families — diverse in background, culture, and circumstance. Our inclusive, welcoming environment ensures every client feels heard, respected, and fully supported throughout their matter.

03

Principle III

Community

Engaged and accountable.

Actively engaged in Indigenous and environmental advocacy, we use our legal expertise to support the communities we serve and honour the traditional territories upon which we live and work.
