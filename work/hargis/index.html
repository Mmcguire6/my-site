The Attorney

Meet Kevin Hargis.

Founding attorney of Hargis Law — the only graduate of the University of Tennessee College of Law on record to complete both the Concentration in Business Transactions and the Concentration in Advocacy & Dispute Resolution.

KH

Founding Attorney

Kevin Hargis

Kevin grew up in rural East Tennessee. His parents divorced when he was nine years old but continued to feud throughout the remainder of his childhood — an experience that gave him unique insight into the needs of Family Law clients. He eventually graduated from Central High School in Wartburg, Tennessee.

Preferring work to school, Kevin spent years working as a lineman before going to college — starting as a ditch-digger on a buried cable placement crew, advancing to lineman, then inspector, and finally to design engineer. He traveled the nation building utility systems and ultimately joined the team that designed the fastest internet in the world for Chattanooga's EPB.

After his children finished school, Kevin pursued his lifelong calling and entered law school. He is the only graduate of the University of Tennessee College of Law on record to complete both the Concentration in Business Transactions and the Concentration in Advocacy & Dispute Resolution. He clerked for U.S. District Judge Clifton Corker and Chief Bankruptcy Judge Marcia Parsons in Greeneville, TN, then spent a decade at a Chattanooga-based firm before founding Hargis Law in January 2025.
