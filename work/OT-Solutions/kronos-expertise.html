The platform moment

Workforce Central is sunsetting.
The knowledge can’t.

UKG has ended engineering on Workforce Central, and the clock is running on support. Moving to UKG Pro Workforce Management is a reimplementation, not an upgrade — your rules, your structures, and your integrations all get rebuilt. Whoever does that work needs to know what your current build actually does.

Dec 31, 2025
Engineering & cloud support ended

Product engineering stopped and support ended for Workforce Central in UKG’s cloud. No new fixes are coming.

Mar 31, 2027
On-premise support ends

Organizations running Workforce Central on-premise have a hard deadline — and migrations regularly take a year or more.

Today
The window that matters

Between now and cutover, the system still has to run payroll-perfect every single pay period. That doesn’t pause for a migration.

a.

Keep Workforce Central steady to the end.

Senior support for the platform you’re still running — pay rule changes, fixes, interfaces, and reporting — so operations hold while the future gets built.

b.

Document what your build actually does.

Twenty-five years of accumulated configuration doesn’t live in the manuals. We map the pay rules, accruals, interfaces, and reports your migration team needs as their source of truth.

c.

Validate the new build against the old truth.

Side-by-side testing of totals, accruals, and schedules against what Workforce Central calculates today — plus reporting continuity, so history survives the move.
