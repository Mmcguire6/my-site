02 — Writs & Appeals

Post-Conviction

An ethical, comprehensive review — before we ever agree to file.

Moran Law Firm offers a unique service to our clients. Prior to agreeing to take on a Writ and/or appeal, we will review the case file, the trial transcripts, talk to the client in custody, speak with the trial attorney, and follow that up with a meeting in our office with the client and/or his or her family. Many times, the Writ Petition is the last legal remedy available to the client, and Moran Law Firm wants to ensure that any Writ or Appeal we accept has a potentially meritorious legal claim.

  • Comprehensive case file and trial transcript review before acceptance
  • In-custody consultation and in-office family meeting included
  • Reasonable sliding-scale fee based on case complexity and location
