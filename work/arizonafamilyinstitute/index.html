№ 02 Who we are

A cooperative of therapists, grounded in attachment

Arizona Family Institute (AFI) is a cooperative of therapists and mental health experts with a shared focus on helping individuals, couples, and families restore connection and emotional health. Our focus on attachment, as a primary function of human connection and emotional regulation, guides our clinical work.
