07 / 11 — The Lawyer

The Lawyer

Meet Sarabjeet Singh.

Founding Barrister & Solicitor — trained in India, accredited in Canada, and called to the Saskatchewan Bar in 2019.

SS

Founding Barrister & Solicitor

Sarabjeet Singh

B.A. · LL.B. · Certificate of Qualification (NCA)

Singh attended the Guru Nanak Dev University in India where he completed his B.A. degree in 2004 and furthered his studies by obtaining a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) in 2008. He joined a full service law firm and worked for five years as a lawyer in Punjab, India before moving permanently to Saskatchewan, Canada in 2013.

He cleared Canadian law exams, obtained his Certificate of Qualification from the National Committee on Accreditation in 2016, articled with Mr. Douglas Racine in Saskatoon — gaining knowledge of aboriginal laws, class actions, injury claims and wills & estates — and was called to the Bar in 2019. In 2020 he founded S & S Lawyers to serve the community.
