The Lawyer

Meet James (Jim) Moon.

Barrister & Solicitor — called to the Bar of Ontario in 1982. Sessional lecturer in real estate law at the University of Guelph for over 25 years.

JM

Barrister & Solicitor

James (Jim) Moon

B.A. (Hons.) · M.A. · LL.B. · Called to the Bar, Ontario 1982

Prior to receiving his LL.B from the University of New Brunswick, Jim graduated from Trent University with an Honours BA in Canadian Studies and from the University of Waterloo with an MA in History. He was called to the Bar in 1982. Jim is an active member of the Guelph community — he has been a Big Brother and served on the Board of Directors for Big Sisters of Guelph, Women in Crisis, the Canadian Mental Health Association (Wellington-Dufferin), St. Joseph's Hospital, and Kids Can Play. He is a Past Chairman of both The Guelph Museums and the River Run Centre Board of Directors, and has served on the Board of the Hillside Community Festival of Guelph for over 25 years.
