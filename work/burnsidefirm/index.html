Why Burnside

Three things that set us apart.

The Burnside name carries weight in Georgia personal injury law — a multi-generational legacy of trial excellence, statewide reach, and an uncompromising mission to identify every at-fault party and force them to provide full and fair compensation.

01

Generational Legacy

A Tradition of Excellence built across generations

Thomas R. Burnside, Jr. received the State Bar of Georgia's Tradition of Excellence Award in 1998 — given annually by the General Practice and Trial Section of the State Bar of Georgia to a plaintiff's lawyer who has demonstrated a tradition of excellence in the profession. In 2008, the State Bar renamed its prestigious leadership award in his honour. Burnside Law Firm LLP continues that legacy in every case we handle.
