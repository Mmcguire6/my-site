№ 04 A specialty within the practice

Neurotechnology & brain-based treatments

Trained at the nationally recognized University of North Texas Neurotherapy Lab, with peripheral biofeedback training through UNT Health Science Center.

  1. Neurofeedback (EEG-Biofeedback)

    QEEG-guided brainwave training; Dr. Fisher was trained at the nationally recognized University of North Texas Neurotherapy Lab.

  2. Quantitative Electroencephalography (QEEG)

    Brainmapping to identify patterns of dysfunctional brain electrical activity and guide individualized neurofeedback protocols.

  3. Peripheral Biofeedback

    EMG, temperature, heart-rate variability (HRV), and electrodermal response (EDR) training targeting the autonomic nervous system.

  4. Cranial Electrotherapy Stimulation (CES)

    Non-invasive cranial stimulation; Dr. Fisher holds a Certificate of Proficiency in CME for CES.

  5. Freespira

    A respiratory biofeedback treatment offered at the practice.

№ 07 Payment & insurance

A cash-based practice, by design

Removing the layers of middlemen so Dr. Fisher and his patients can work closely together.

i. Payment

  • Cash-pay onlyOnly cash payments are accepted for psychological services.
  • Out-of-network invoicesUpon request, we provide invoices with diagnostic codes that you may submit to your insurance for potential out-of-network reimbursement.
  • Medicare / MedicaidPatients covered by Medicare/Medicaid who want to see Dr. Fisher must sign an opt-out agreement, as required by law.

ii. Why cash-based

  • The doctor-patient relationshipCash-based services emphasize working closely together, free of insurance pre-approvals and review boards.
  • Treatment guided by you & Dr. FisherUtilize treatments as you see fit, regardless of insurance's opinion about treatment necessity.
  • Neurofeedback & specialty careSome treatments — such as neurofeedback — are covered by few, if any, insurance companies.
No guarantee is made that you will be reimbursed by your insurance company for all or part of the amount paid for our services. It is our experience that many patients are reimbursed for part of the treatment cost where their plan covers mental health and provides out-of-network benefits. The patient is responsible for submitting all paperwork related to out-of-network claims, though we do our very best to help with the process.

№ 08 Common questions

Good to know

Only cash payments are accepted. Upon request, Dr. Fisher's staff will provide the patient with relevant invoices with diagnostic codes that a patient may submit to their insurance company for potential out-of-network benefit payments. However, no guarantee is made that the patient will be reimbursed by their insurance company for all or part of the amount paid for our services. It is our experience that many patients will be reimbursed for part of the treatment cost given that their insurance covers mental health and provides out-of-network benefits. The patient is responsible for submitting all paperwork related to out-of-network claims, though we do our very best to help with this process.

Dr. Fisher strongly believes that cash-based services provide the very best approach to psychological treatment. Cash-based services emphasize the doctor-patient relationship and removes layers of "middlemen" (health insurance pre-approvals, health review boards, government paid administrators, etc.) that can hinder or limit the healthcare process. Insurance companies may limit treatment type, length, and cost whether or not the treatment is in the patient's best interest. Some treatments, such as neurofeedback, are covered by few, if any, insurance companies; thus, neurofeedback is a cash-based service for nearly all patients even if they have health insurance. An additional advantage is that patients can typically get an appointment with Dr. Fisher more quickly, often without waiting weeks or longer.

Only cash payments are accepted. As required by law, patients who are covered by Medicare/Medicaid and want to see Dr. Fisher must sign an opt-out agreement.
