02 · OVI · DUI

OVI / DUI Defense

Mandatory jail time. Long-lasting consequences.

OVI charges carry mandatory jail time and long-lasting consequences for your driver's license and daily life. Frank Malocu has watched Ohio OVI law evolve across three decades, and he knows exactly how to challenge the state's case — including scrutinizing whether the arresting officer used the proper procedure during your arrest. Whether you face a first offense or a felony OVI, Frank will build a strategy unique to the facts of your case.

  • First through felony OVI — all charge levels handled
  • Procedural challenges to arrest and field testing
  • High-tier OVI and repeat offense defense
