The Fresh Start

Chapter 7 · Liquidation

Chapter 7 Bankruptcy

Complete discharge. Immediate stay. A clean slate.

Chapter 7 liquidation bankruptcy — the “Fresh Start” — allows eligible debtors to completely eliminate all dischargeable debts and step out from under the weight of creditor pressure. McNeil Law Firm guides clients through the means test, petition preparation, and discharge process in Ohio state court and the Northern District of Ohio federal court, ensuring the right strategy is in place from day one.

  • Complete discharge of eligible unsecured debts
  • Immediate automatic stay halts all creditor collection
  • Experienced guidance through federal bankruptcy court filings
Learn More
Reorganization Path

Chapter 13 · Reorganization

Chapter 13 Bankruptcy

Keep what matters. Repay on your terms.

Chapter 13 bankruptcy provides individuals with regular income the opportunity to restructure their debts into an affordable three-to-five-year repayment plan — all while retaining their home, vehicle, and other key assets. Attorney Ryan McNeil has appeared in Northern District of Ohio federal court on Chapter 13 matters for over a decade, delivering structured relief for clients across Northeast Ohio.

  • Retain your home and vehicle while repaying debts
  • Structured 3–5 year repayment plan under court supervision
  • Stop foreclosure and protect property from creditors
