№ 02 Our focus

More than a mental health provider — partners in healing

At Associated Behavioral and Trauma Specialties, LLC, we offer comprehensive trauma-focused counseling and psychiatric services for children, adolescents, adults, couples, and families. We provide individualized behavioral health care that integrates trauma-informed psychotherapy and psychiatry — ensuring that emotional, psychological, and biological needs are addressed together when appropriate. Trauma-focused treatment is central to our work, with thoughtful pacing, clinical structure, and collaboration supporting healing without overwhelming the individual.
