Our Approach

Resolution first. Court last.

Nora's preference for out-of-court settlements isn't a slogan — it's a discipline. Three principles shape how every file at LeBlanc Family Law is handled.

01

Principle

Resolution First

Identify the most efficient path.

Nora explores all options for conflict resolution and assists clients to identify the most efficient and cost-effective means of settlement while minimizing conflict and preserving relationships to the extent possible.

02

Process

Collaborative Process

A structured alternative.

As a Registered Collaborative Family Lawyer, Nora uses the structured Collaborative Family Law (CFL) process to help separating couples reach agreements entirely outside the courtroom.

03

Philosophy

Minimizing Conflict

Especially when children are involved.

The firm's philosophy centres on preserving relationships and minimizing emotional stress — especially when children are involved — while still ensuring your legal rights are fully protected.
