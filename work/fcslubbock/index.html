№ 02 Welcome to FCS

A place where you feel welcome and accepted

We are a non-profit counseling agency located in Lubbock, Texas, offering a broad array of counseling and educational services to Lubbock and the surrounding areas. Our counselors are LPC's, LPC-Associates, and Master's Level Interns from various universities — and they all set their own appointments. Our main goal is to provide access to affordable counseling, regardless of your ability to pay.

№ 05 Meet our counselors

Connect with a counselor in Lubbock

Our team includes Licensed Professional Counselors, LPC-Associates, and Master's Level Interns from various universities — each setting their own appointments and offering a different price point.

Sign up & be matched

A note on choosing a counselor: LPCs are fully licensed; LPC-Associates are practicing under supervision toward full licensure; Master's Level Interns are graduate trainees from area universities. You can choose the price point that fits — and the level of clinician along with it.

№ 06 Getting started

Three steps to your first session

i.

Submit a request

Click the Sign Up tab on our site or email admin@fcslubbock.org. Your submission will be reviewed by our team.

ii.

Watch your email

If we have any questions, we'll reply by email. Please watch your inbox — you will not receive a phone call.

iii.

Set your appointment

A counselor will contact you to schedule. All counselors set their own appointments and confirm format — in-person or Zoom.
