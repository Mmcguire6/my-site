06.5 — THE ATTORNEY

The Attorney

Meet Michael Spano.

A decade as a prosecutor. Nearly two decades exclusively defending the accused. One office in downtown Syracuse.

Principal · 30+ Years

Principal · Criminal Defense Attorney

Michael Spano

Former Prosecutor. Relentless Defender.

Michael Spano graduated from Nazareth College of Rochester with a B.S. in Economics in 1989 and from Syracuse University College of Law in 1992, and was admitted to the New York State Bar in 1993. He joined the Onondaga County District Attorney's Office in 1998, spending the next ten years prosecuting violent felony offenses, ultimately promoted to Chief Assistant District Attorney in charge of the Homicide Bureau, overseeing the prosecution of all homicides in Onondaga County.

For nearly two decades, Michael has focused his practice exclusively on criminal defense, bringing a prosecutor's inside knowledge to every case he defends. He has conducted trainings for the Syracuse Police Department and Onondaga County Sheriff's Department, and led Continuing Legal Education seminars for the Onondaga Bar Association.

  • B.S. Economics, Nazareth College of Rochester, 1989
  • J.D., Syracuse University College of Law, 1992
  • Admitted, New York State Bar, 1993
  • Member, Onondaga County Bar Association
