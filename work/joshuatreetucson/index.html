№ 02 Who we are

A collective for healing — mental, emotional & relational

Joshua Tree Counseling Tucson was founded in 2021 by Rachel Neville, LPC, with a specific vision: a comfortable, home-like setting where clients come to heal from mental, emotional, and relational pain and trauma. We are a collective of mature Christian believers who share a passion for meeting people in the midst of suffering and partnering with them toward healing.

We believe healing is possible when people feel seen, supported, and understood. In every session we value —

№ 04 What makes us distinct

Licensed psychotherapy & pastoral counseling — both under one roof

Choose the path that fits your needs. If you prefer counseling without a faith component, you can request that — we honor it with integrity.

i.

Licensed Psychotherapists

State-licensed counselors equipped to evaluate, diagnose, and treat mental health conditions — including approaches such as EMDR, CBT, DBT, and Deep Brain Reorienting.

ii.

Pastoral Counselors

Certified pastoral counselors offering Christ-centered soul care and story work — faith-integrated healing that does not require a clinical diagnosis.

iii.

Founded in memory of Joshua

Named in honor of Rachel's brother-in-law, whose long battle with mental illness shaped her clinical specialty in complex trauma, dissociation, and suicidality.

iv.

Your counselor, your choice

Browse detailed bio pages and select the counselor you feel most drawn to. In our experience, clients have better outcomes when they choose their own counselor.
