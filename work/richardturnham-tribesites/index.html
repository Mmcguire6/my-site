№ 04 Treatment approach

A foundation, then the tools to sustain growth

I use a variety of approaches to address each client's unique needs — beginning with a foundation of evidence-based therapies, then quickly moving toward tools to help sustain the growth experienced in regular therapy.

  • i.

    Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy

    Identifying and reshaping unhealthy thinking patterns.

  • ii.

    Person-Centered

    A safe, non-judgmental space for you to tell your story.

  • iii.

    Solution-Focused Brief Therapy

    Tools to sustain growth beyond the therapy room.

  • iv.

    Psychoanalytic

    Exploring the root causes of distress and dysfunction.

  • v.

    Mindfulness & Self-Compassion

    Neuroscience-backed practices for resilience and healing painful memories.

  • vi.

    Faith-Integrated (Optional)

    Christian perspective available as requested.
