№ 08 Frequently asked

Good to know

Most people can benefit from therapy at some point in their lives. Sometimes the need is clear — but other times, it's more subtle. You may be experiencing persistent sadness, anger, or hopelessness that feels hard to manage; a sense of being overwhelmed, stuck, or emotionally numb; difficulties in relationships, at work, or with self-esteem; unresolved trauma, such as abuse, neglect, or past painful experiences; or grief or loss. Therapy can provide a safe and non-judgmental space to explore these feelings, understand their roots, and develop healthier ways of coping.

Because each person is unique, we may use a variety of therapeutic approaches to address the challenges you're facing. Our work together will be tailored to meet your specific goals, and part of the process may include exploring and identifying what those goals are. As therapy progresses, we may adjust our approach based on what is or isn't working for you, always with your needs and well-being at the center of the process.

Our receptionist will email you forms prior to your session. You can complete forms in person prior to appointment — please come 10 minutes before your scheduled appointment if completing in office. You will be asked to bring your ID and insurance card with you to your first appointment.

A best friend offers love, empathy, and comfort shaped by mutual history, but may have bias and is not bound by obligations of confidentiality or neutrality. A therapist brings professional training, clinical techniques, a formal obligation to confidentiality, and a structured, non-reciprocal relationship focused entirely on your growth and well-being.
