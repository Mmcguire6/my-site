Becoming a patient

A simple, three-step pathway — designed around your comfort and fit.

Complete the pre-screening

Visit the New Patients tab and complete the pre-screening form to request a free 10-minute initial phone consultation with a psychiatrist.

On the call, we'll review your goals for treatment and determine together whether Mind and Mood is the right fit for you.

Initial diagnostic visit

If we both agree to move forward, we'll schedule your initial diagnostic consultation — virtually, or in-office at the discretion of your doctor.

№ 08 Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Mind and Mood Mental Health, LLC offers services ranging from diagnostic evaluation, psychotherapy, and medication management. We treat adults with depression, anxiety, ADHD, PTSD, bipolar disorder, anxiety disorders, insomnia, and many others. We enjoy working with patients using a collaborative approach involving other clinical providers such as therapists, counselors, and primary care physicians when needed.

Mind and Mood Mental Health, LLC does not treat patients with the following conditions: self-harm behaviors in the last three months; suicide attempts in the last three months; recent inpatient psychiatric hospitalizations; active suicidal or homicidal ideations; active eating disorder; active substance use disorder.

Erin Walling, MD is currently licensed to practice psychiatry in the states of Nebraska and Oklahoma. Nancy Handler, MD is currently licensed in Nebraska and Kansas. Parostu Rohanni, MD is currently licensed in Nebraska, California and Texas.

Appointments are virtual. This is considered telemedicine and requires the patient to have a working camera and microphone to allow us to see and hear each other. You can use a computer, tablet, or a smartphone. For most people, this is a convenient option. There may occasionally be the need for an in-office appointment but this is at the discretion of your doctor. During the screening call, we will discuss if the practice is a good fit for you or if a larger in-person practice would be a better fit. The sessions are HIPAA compliant using our telemedicine platform.
