01 — Estate Planning

Estate Planning

The foundation of every family's security.

A comprehensive estate plan is the foundation of every family's financial security. At Safe Harbor, we craft tailored plans encompassing wills, revocable living trusts, durable powers of attorney, and healthcare directives — designed to avoid probate, minimize estate and death taxes, and ensure your wishes are honoured exactly as you intend.

  • Custom wills, trusts and powers of attorney
  • Probate avoidance strategies built into every plan
  • Family meetings — involving your loved ones in the process

02 — Elder Law & Medicaid

Elder Law & Medicaid

Clarity when long-term care arrives.

Many families are misled or confused about how Medicaid and long-term care planning actually work — and that confusion can cost them everything. Phil George explains it clearly, honestly, and in a way that finally makes sense: protecting your assets, qualifying for benefits, and ensuring you never run out of money for care.

  • Medicaid qualification & asset protection planning
  • Long-term care cost strategies for seniors & families
  • Clear guidance on protecting family assets from care costs

Attorney & Founder

Phillip George

Phillip George is the Attorney and Founder of Safe Harbor Legal Solutions, dedicated to helping Western Washington families navigate estate planning, elder law, and retirement preparedness with clarity and confidence. He hosts The Aging Hour, a weekly radio program on KGMI 790AM and 96.5FM, and conducts free educational seminars throughout the region — from Whatcom County community halls to San Juan Island senior centres — bringing accessible, practical guidance to every family he serves.

Phil is known for his ability to take complex estate-planning topics and make them understandable, relevant, and actionable for everyday people.
