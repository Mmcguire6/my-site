About the Firm

Rooted in the Community. Committed to Results.

"Commitment to excellence, creative problem-solving, fairness, and integrity — these are not slogans. They define how we approach every client and every file."

City Law Offices was founded in 2006 by Emmanuel Alade in Fort Saskatchewan, Alberta. What began as a small practice has grown into a trusted full-service firm representing clients across the region — from Fort Saskatchewan and Sherwood Park to Ardrossan, Redwater, Lamont, and communities throughout central Alberta.

With over 25 years of combined legal experience and multi-jurisdictional expertise spanning both Nigerian and Canadian bar admissions, our team brings a uniquely broad perspective to Alberta law. We offer flexible scheduling, including evening and weekend appointments, because we understand that legal matters don't always fit a nine-to-five window.

We measure success not only by outcomes but by the clarity and confidence our clients feel throughout the process. You will always know where your matter stands.

Our Team

Trusted Legal Professionals

Put your case in capable hands. Our team of lawyers and legal assistants is here to help you get the results you deserve.

Emmanuel B. Alade LL.B LL.M
Barrister & Solicitor — Principal Counsel

Emmanuel founded City Law Offices in 2006 and has built it into a cornerstone of the Fort Saskatchewan legal community. He brings over 24 years of multi-jurisdictional experience to every matter, having been called to the bar in both Nigeria and Alberta.

His LL.M from the University of Alberta included a thesis on Alberta's oil sands development as a framework for Nigeria's energy sector — reflecting the depth of analytical rigour he applies to client work. Outside the office, Emmanuel volunteers with the Mariam Centre and local homeless shelters, and has coached youth soccer in the community.

  • LL.M — University of Alberta
  • LL.B — Obafemi Awolowo University (University of Ife)
  • Called to the Bar: Nigeria & Alberta
  • Former Senior Manager (Legal), Odu'a Investment Corporation
Adesola Alade CGA, CPA
Practice Manager & Real Estate Conveyancer

Adesola is the operational backbone of City Law Offices. As a Chartered Professional Accountant with deep expertise in real estate conveyancing, she oversees office procedures, financial management, and technology systems — ensuring the firm runs with precision on every file.
