Our Flagship

Disability Law

Social Security Disability (SSDI & SSI)

When you can no longer work, the fight begins.

Liebenhaut Law represents individuals throughout the United States who are unable to work because of physical, mental, or emotional disability. We assist clients in obtaining Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) and Supplemental Security Income (SSI) benefits, and fight insurers in private long-term disability (LTD/ERISA) disputes. If you have been denied, the clock is running — appeals must be filed within sixty days.

  • No fee unless we win your disability appeal
  • SSDI, SSI, and private LTD / ERISA insurance disputes
  • Free case evaluation with response within one business day
