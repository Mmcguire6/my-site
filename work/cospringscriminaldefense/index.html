DUI Defense

Field tests challenged. License protected. Charges fought.

DUI charges in Colorado Springs are severe — many drivers face the challenging reality of a DUI charge each year in El Paso County, and the consequences include jail time, heavy fines, and license suspension. Colorado Springs Criminal Defense, LLC navigates every stage of the process: from challenging field sobriety tests and blood alcohol testing results to representing clients at DMV hearings and negotiating with prosecutors for reduced or dismissed charges.

  • Challenge field sobriety tests and BAC testing evidence
  • DMV hearing representation to fight license suspension
  • Negotiate reduced charges or pursue full dismissal
