Areas of Practice

Nine disciplines, one team that knows the file.

From start-up incorporation to commercial real estate, hospitality, IP, and estate planning — Craig Law's lawyers are cross-trained, so the matter stays with people who already understand your business.

Start-Up Companies

ii.

Corporate & Commercial

Debt and equity financings, mergers and acquisitions, joint ventures, recapitalizations, restructurings, and changes in ownership.

iii.

Residential Real Estate

Buying or selling a house, condominium, or raw land — with lawyers and staff who handle conveyancing every day.

iv.

Commercial Real Estate

Developers, investors, and lenders through every phase — planning, land acquisition, construction, property management, and financing.

v.

Hospitality
