What We're Known For

Three things, done at the highest level.

Beresh Law has built its reputation on the matters that ask the most of a defence team — serious trials, complex appeals, and the cases that change Canadian law.

01

Trial

Trial-tested defence at every level of court in Canada — from Provincial Court through to the Court of King's Bench. The room where the verdict is decided is the room we know best.

02

Appeal

Convictions reviewed, reargued, and challenged at every appellate level — up to and including the highest courts of appeal. When the first verdict isn't the right one, the appeal is where it gets revisited.

03

Precedent

Difficult cases that set precedent and pave the way for a fairer, more just Canadian legal system. The matters that matter beyond the file — the ones that change the law for everyone who comes after.
