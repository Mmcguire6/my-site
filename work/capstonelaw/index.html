What We Do

Six focused areas of practice.

From buying a home to handling a serious criminal charge to navigating an immigration application — Capstone covers the full breadth of personal and business legal matters Regina families need.

Real Estate

Residential and commercial purchases, sales, refinances, and title work. Plain-language guidance through the closing process so you know exactly what's happening, when.

Criminal Law

Defence representation for individuals facing criminal charges in Regina and surrounding Saskatchewan. Focused, rigorous advocacy at every stage of the process.

Immigration Law

Permanent residency, work and study permits, family sponsorship, and citizenship applications. Practical, dependable guidance through the Canadian immigration system.

Corporate Law

Incorporations, shareholder agreements, contract review, and ongoing legal support for small and growing Saskatchewan businesses. Clear advice you can act on.

Wills & Estates

Wills, powers of attorney, and estate administration. Make decisions today that ease your family's burden tomorrow — with clear, careful drafting.

Family Law

Separation, divorce, custody, support, and matrimonial property matters. Sensitive, focused representation through some of life's most difficult decisions.
