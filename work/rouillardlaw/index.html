About Rouillard Law

From a home-based practice to a Sylvan Lake fixture.

Rouillard Law Office opened May 1, 2007 as a home-based practice and moved to a dedicated office in January 2008 as the community grew. Eighteen years later, Sylvan Lake is still where the firm calls home — and where every file is handled.
