№ 03 Services

Customized care, at your own pace

A fully customized, one-on-one approach — tailored to your needs and your goals, in the format that fits your life.

i.

Individual Therapy

Perfect for the person who wants a fully customized approach. You'll work one-on-one with your provider to tailor treatment to your specific needs and goals — listened to without judgment, in a safe space to discuss your thoughts and emotions, moving at your own pace.

ii.

Family Therapy

Beneficial for those seeking healthier family relationships and for families navigating a member's behavioral concerns. Focused on improving communication, building empathy and validation, identifying problem behaviors, and offering practical behavior-management strategies.

iii.

Telehealth

Ideal for the person who can't make it to the office — whether a busy schedule, long commute, or simply wanting to stay home. Mental health counseling via a HIPAA-compliant audio/video platform. (Audio-only sessions are not offered at this time.)

iv.

40+

Participating states

Virtual counseling, where you live

Through PsyPACT, telehealth services are available to residents of 40+ participating states — opening access to care for clients beyond the DFW Metroplex.

See if your state participates at psypact.gov/page/psypactmap

Why it matters

Continuity for clients on the move

For Texas residents who relocate, travel for work, or live across state lines, PsyPACT allows continuity of care with a single licensed psychologist — a meaningful difference for trauma work, anxiety treatment, and longer-term therapeutic relationships.

Sessions are conducted via a HIPAA-compliant audio/video platform. Telehealth is not appropriate for everyone and is offered at the sole discretion of your provider.

№ 08 Frequently asked

Good to know

As defined by the World Health Organization, "Mental health is a state of mental well-being that enables people to cope with the stresses of life, realize their abilities, learn well and work well, and contribute to their community. It is an integral component of health and well-being… Mental health is a basic human right." The CDC adds that mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social well-being — it affects how we think, feel, and act, and helps determine how we handle stress, relate to others, and make healthy choices.

The choice to seek mental health counseling and/or pharmacological management is a deeply personal decision. Please feel free to call (682) 273-8501 for a complimentary 15-minute consultation. Basic information about psychotropic medications and referrals for local psychiatric providers may be offered; consulting with your preferred medical provider is strongly recommended. Note that your provider is not medically trained and cannot provide medical advice regarding prescribed medications.

There are no staff with prescription privileges employed at Paradise Psychology at this time. However, local referrals for psychiatric providers can be provided upon request.
