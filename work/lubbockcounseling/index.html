№ 02 Who we are

A ministry of Biblical counseling

Real Life Christian Counseling offers a clear path toward peace and wholeness. We focus on spiritual formation to bring healing to both life and relationships. RLCC is a ministry of Biblical Counseling based in Lubbock, Texas — a 501(c)(3) nonprofit whose donations make quality Christian counseling available to anyone who needs it, regardless of ability to pay.
