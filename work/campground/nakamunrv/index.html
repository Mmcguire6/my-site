About Nakamun

Store, RV park & lake life.

Nakamun Store & RV Park is a full-service store and the only RV park in the Nakamun Lake area, near Busby, Alberta — the practical heart of a quiet lake community.

It's where you fuel up, stock up and set up: groceries and essentials in the store, a place to park the rig, and a 160-acre nature reserve and trails to enjoy while you're here.
