№ 02 Who we are

A team built around realistic solutions & the right match

The licensed and experienced therapists at Wichita Family Counseling see clients for a range of needs — including individual therapy, couple and marriage counseling, family therapy, depression and anxiety, abuse and trauma, play therapy, chronic pain, grief counseling, and special therapies like EMDR, ART, and DBT. Using a variety of tools, we partner with our clients to develop realistic solutions that improve the quality of health, life, and relationships.

№ 06 First steps for finding a therapist

How to get started

i.

Determine the support you need

What factors in your life led you to seek help? Keep those in mind as you read our therapists' profiles or complete an inquiry.

ii.

Create a list of potential therapists

Review our therapists by clicking their profiles. If you have an insurance plan you intend to use, check that the provider you select is a match — not all therapists can take all plans.

iii.

Inquire & schedule

Inquire about the possibility of becoming a client and scheduling an initial appointment. We respond best to emails and to our inquiry form.
