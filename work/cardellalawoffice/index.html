Why Cardella

Constitutional victories at the highest courts, courtroom miles measured in trials not appearances, and the academic authority to teach the same law I argue in court. This is what nearly two decades of Indiana criminal defense actually looks like.

01

Constitutional Victories

Landmark Cases at the Highest Courts

Jeff Cardella filed the Federal Class Action of Washington v. Marion County Prosecutor, in which Indiana's vehicle forfeiture statute was held unconstitutional under the Due Process Clause of the United States Constitution. He also co-authored the amicus brief for Timbs v. State of Indiana, in which the United States Supreme Court held that Indiana's forfeiture practices violated the Excessive Fines Clause of the Eighth Amendment.

Amicus Brief at the U.S. Supreme Court
