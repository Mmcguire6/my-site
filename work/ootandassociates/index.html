No. 02 · Social Security Disability

Social Security Disability

The federal system denies most initial claims. We know how to win on appeal.

If you have paid into the Social Security system, you are entitled to disability benefits if you are unable to work. Unfortunately, the vast majority of Social Security Disability applicants are initially denied benefits. We work to get you the social security disability benefits you deserve — guiding clients through the whole claim's process, putting your best interests at the forefront of every decision, and representing you before the Social Security Administration until you receive the benefits you have earned.

  • SSD & SSI applications — from initial filing through approval.
  • Reconsideration & ALJ hearings when initial claims are denied.
  • Combined workers' comp & SSD coordination — offset planning across both systems.
