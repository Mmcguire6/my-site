№ 02 Our mission

A community service system, responsive to the people it serves

The mission of Buoyant Family Services Counseling and Consulting, LLC is to promote and provide effective strength-based co-occurring, integrated services — a community service system that embraces multiculturalism, efficiency, empowerment, and is responsive to community needs.
