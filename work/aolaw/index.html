Probate & Trust Administration

Probate & Trust

Working with families through the loss of a loved one.

Probate administration is the process of transferring ownership of an asset through the county probate court to an heir or named beneficiary. Trust administration is similar, but generally happens intra family without the involvement of the county probate court. Andrew lost a parent young, and understands the emotions and complexities surrounding the death of a loved one.

  • Probate & non-probate transfer representation
  • Trustee guidance through the Ohio Trust Code
  • Reducing administrative burden on grieving families
