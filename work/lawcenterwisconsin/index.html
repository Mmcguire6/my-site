07 / 11 — Approach Our Approach · Our Conscience · Our Promise

Our Approach

Law, with a conscience.

Three founding pillars guide every decision The Law Center makes — from the cases we accept to the strategies we pursue.

01

Pillar One

The Child Comes First

Children deserve safety, love, and certainty.

The best interests of the child are paramount, always. As a law firm with years of experience in family law cases and child custody, we believe children have the right to be loved, nurtured, respected, and free from harm, fear, and uncertainty. This is what drives us.

02

Pillar Two

Law With A Conscience

We ask the right question before we accept.

Before we accept a case, we ask ourselves: if we win, will it be good for the child? If the answer is yes, then we gather our resources and our team gets to work. It is the standard that has shaped every client relationship at The Law Center since the firm's founding.

03

Pillar Three

Families Come In Many Forms

Every family deserves equal protection.

Some families have one, two, or more parents. Some parents are of the same sex or gender. Some are multi-generational or not related by blood at all. To each other, they are family, and our Wisconsin family law attorneys will protect those relationships.
