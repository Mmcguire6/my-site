№ 04 A practice that gives back

Training the next generation of mental-health professionals

Our practice is committed to training the next generation of mental-health professionals. Advanced graduate students who have completed most or all of their core coursework — and are in the clinical phase of their training — provide counseling services under the close supervision of a licensed clinician.

  • i.
    Advanced graduate studentsClinical-phase trainees, supervised by a licensed clinician on our team.
  • ii.
    Closely supervised careEvery intern session is overseen by a licensed clinician — so clients still receive whole-team support.
  • iii.
    Fresh perspectivesInterns bring new training, current research, and an additional layer of attention to client care.

№ 06 Getting started

Your first steps

We understand that the first step may be the hardest. Call us today — we will help you take the next step in your journey.

i.

Reach out

Call 505-557-4656 or submit an Appointment Request online — whichever feels easier for the first step.

ii.

We'll match you

We'll connect you with a clinician on our team whose training fits what you're working through — in person or by secure video.

iii.

Begin your work

Meet your therapist at our downtown Albuquerque office, or over secure video from any private location and any device.
