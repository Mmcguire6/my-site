Our Approach

Results, Truth, Attention, Community.

Four convictions that shape every file at this firm — from the first phone call through to the verdict slip the jury signs in open court.

01

Pillar · I

Results

Best interest of the client.

The only acceptable result is one that is in the best interest of the client. This means I don't resolve cases fast and cheap. Not having a large advertising budget or monthly settlement quota allows for maximum recovery. A good result to me is money in my client's pocket. End of story.

02

Pillar · II

No Garbage

I will tell you the truth.

I'm not going to yell at you in a TV commercial or embarrass myself to get clicks or clients. I will also tell you the truth. Cases have good and bad facts, evidence, and circumstances. My job is to lay that all out and make something happen from it. I don't get paid until my clients get compensated.

03

Pillar · III

Attention

Guidance where it's needed.

Most lawyers don't know it, but we are in the customer service business. That means people pick up the phone at my office and answer your questions. We don't dodge hard work. And if your case needs to go to trial it is prepared as such and taken in front of a jury. I prepare clients for deposition, mediation, and anything else where guidance is needed.

04

Pillar · IV

Community

Louisville built in.

I support the local community because I live here. My family lives here. We play sports in local leagues, and the firm supports Roo's Wish, Wednesday's Child, Hope's Closet, Promise Kids a Future, Via Colori, University of Louisville Rugby & Hockey, Crash Rugby, Mega Jags, Shepherdsville Steelers, Ice Skating Academy, and the Derby City Dynamite.
