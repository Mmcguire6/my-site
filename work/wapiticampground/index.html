About the campground

Camp in the heart of the Bow Valley.

Wapiti Campground is a first-come, first-serve, family-friendly campground located at 100 Ray McBride Street at the northwest end of the scenic town of Canmore, Alberta.

We're nestled in the foothills of the Canadian Rockies, close to the majestic Three Sisters Mountains and only minutes away from world-renowned Banff National Park. Bring your camera to capture the breathtaking scenery and wildlife.

Planning your stay

First come, first serve.

We don't take reservations — every site is first-come, first-serve. Here's how to plan around it.

Step one

Call or email ahead

Call or email 2–3 days before you arrive to check occupancy at 403-678-9511. July and August are very busy months.

Step two

Arrive & choose a site

Sites are first-come, first-serve. Find an open site, then register and pay within 30 minutes of selecting it.

Step three

Settle in

Check-in is after 2:00pm — sometimes earlier with management approval or when the campground isn't at capacity. Check-out is 11:00am.

Payment & registration

Simple to check in.

Pay in person at the store, or use the secure self-registration kiosk at the entrance whenever the store is closed.

Accepted payment

All forms are accepted in person — Visa, Mastercard, Amex, debit and cash. Debit is available in person only.

Self-registration kiosk

When the store is closed, a kiosk at the entrance has the rules, rates, envelopes and site tags. Cash or credit only at the kiosk — include your full card details, including the CVV.

Secure & private

The self-registration booth is secured at all times and payment goes into a sealed vault. Your payment information is securely destroyed when you depart.

Shower tokens are purchased at the store — note that the showers don't accept loonies or toonies.

On-site facilities

What you'll find here.

Washroom facilities

Clean washrooms available to all campers throughout the grounds.

Token showers

Hot showers available on site, operated with paid tokens.

Potable water

Two potable water and fill stations for drinking and filling — located on the grounds, not at individual sites.

Outdoor dish sink

A shared outdoor sink for washing dishes — please keep it clean for other campers.

Small general store

Sells firewood, ice, ice-cream and limited essentials — priced fairly, often better than in town. Hours vary.

Office hours

Typically 10:00–12:00 and 2:00–8:30pm, though this can vary.

Before you arrive

Good to know.

A few clear rules keep Wapiti calm, clean and welcoming for every camper.

1

Check-in & check-out

Check-in after 2:00pm, check-out by 11:00am. Any site occupied before 11:00am is charged for the previous night.

2

Maximum stay

5-day maximum. Up to 2 extra days only with management approval. No long-term stays of any kind — this is non-negotiable.

3

Quiet hours

10:00pm to 8:00am, strictly enforced. Excessive noise, loud music or prolonged generator use isn't tolerated at any hour.

4

Site occupancy

One family or group per site, four adults maximum. One sleeping unit and one vehicle per site — an extra pup or children's tent is fine.

5

Extra vehicles

One vehicle per site. Additional vehicles, trailers or equipment must be parked off-site, or a $35 fee applies.

6

Pets

Dogs and other animals leashed at all times, even on your own site. Never left unattended; owners clean up after them.

7

Fires & firewood

Fires only in designated pits and never left unattended. No outside firewood (invasive-species risk) — firewood is sold on site below market price.

8

Bear country

Food, coolers and garbage must never be left unattended, and must be stored inside a vehicle overnight.

9

Alcohol

Open alcohol must stay on your campsite. Carrying open drinks between sites isn't permitted.

10

Visitors

Any visitors to a campsite must leave by 11:00pm.

11

Refunds & conduct

No refunds and no partial-day stays — all sales are final. Stays are revocable, and not following the rules may result in eviction.

12

No dump station

No dump station or septic on site. The closest options are in Banff or at Bow Valley Campground.
