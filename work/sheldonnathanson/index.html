Founder · Principal Lawyer

Sheldon Nathanson

Sheldon Nathanson is the founder of Sheldon Nathanson Law — a cornerstone of the Cape Breton legal community since 1989. With more than 30 years of practice, Sheldon has extensive experience in Education Law, Contract Law, Corporate and Commercial Law, Civil Litigation, Criminal Law, Family Law and Property Law — and has served as Per Diem Crown Attorney for the Public Prosecution Service of Nova Scotia.
