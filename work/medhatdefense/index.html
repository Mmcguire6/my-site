— FEES

Fees

A simple goal, and an honest conversation.

The goal is simple: to satisfactorily and expeditiously conclude a client’s matter while exposing them to as little risk as possible.

We understand that being criminally charged is often something you were not financially preparing for. Yarshenko & Heidinger are fully transparent about what it will cost to defend your matter — you will be aware early on (generally after the no-charge first meeting) what the total cost will be to conduct your defence.

i.

No-Charge First Meeting

The first meeting is no-charge. We talk through the matter, the path forward, and the realistic range of outcomes before you commit to anything.

ii.

Flat-Rate Pricing

Typically, the lawyers charge a flat rate, dependent upon the type of offence you have been charged with — you will know the total cost to conduct your defence early on.

iii.

Payment Options

Depending on which lawyer you choose to work with from Yarshenko & Heidinger, there are a variety of payment options available.
