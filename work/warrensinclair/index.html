05 / 11 — Known For

Why Warren Sinclair

Three pillars, built on decades of practice.

From a small founding partnership to a firm of more than twenty lawyers serving four Central Alberta communities, Warren Sinclair LLP has built its reputation on the same three commitments since day one.

01

Full-Service Depth

Every area of law your business and family will ever need — under one roof

Warren Sinclair LLP offers one of the broadest practice portfolios in Central Alberta, spanning corporate and commercial law, real estate, family law and divorce, civil litigation, employment law, wills and estates, banking, administrative law, securities, and intellectual property. From a three-lawyer founding partnership in 1982, the firm has grown to over twenty lawyers, each with deep expertise in their own practice areas.

02

Community Roots

Lawyers who live here, serve here, and give back here

All of our lawyers generously donate their time and energy to support our local community and become involved in social, charitable, political, and volunteer activities. Over the years, members of our Firm have been involved with the United Way of Central Alberta, the Red Deer Regional Hospital Foundation, the Red Deer Public Library Board, the Central Alberta Bar Association, and countless other civic organizations — and have coached high school basketball, hockey, soccer, and football.
