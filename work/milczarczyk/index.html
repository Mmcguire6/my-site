Meet Maciej (Matt) Milczarczyk.

MM
Principal Counsel · H.B.Sc. · LL.B.

Maciej (Matt) Milczarczyk

From a small Polish village to a Waterloo courtroom.

Maciej (Matt) Milczarczyk was born in Lublin, Poland, and spent his idyllic childhood in a small village called Łopiennik. His family immigrated to Canada in 1989. Matt has an Honours Bachelor of Science Degree from the University of Waterloo and a Bachelor of Laws Degree from Osgoode Hall Law School at York University.

He was called to the Bar in 2005 and has been operating as a sole practitioner since 2008. He speaks English, French and Polish fluently and can assist clients with their legal matters in those three languages, including conducting criminal and family proceedings in both of Canada's official languages.
