About the campground

A Lions campground in Devon.

Devon Lions Campground is a family-friendly campground in the town of Devon, Alberta — just minutes from Edmonton, near the junction of Highway 60 and Highway 19 and adjacent to the Devon Golf & Conference Centre.

Tucked into mature trees, the campground offers everything from quiet unserviced sites to full-service pads, with clean shower houses, a laundromat and rental facilities for groups and events.

Reservations

Booking your stay.

Reservations are recommended — long weekends and monthly sites book up quickly.

01
How to book

Call ahead — we book by phone.

We do not offer online reservations. Call the office at 780-987-4777 to book; a $10.00 non-refundable reservation fee applies per site, with campsite fees due at check-in.

There is a 2-night minimum on weekends and a 3-night minimum on long weekends. Bookings are accepted year-round for the following season.

Check-in is between 2:00pm and 8:00pm; check-out is 11:00am. Arriving after 8:00pm? Notify the office — the full stay is charged to your credit card.

Facilities & rentals

What you'll find here.

Two shower houses

Two shower houses with washrooms — free for registered campers.

Coin laundromat

On-site coin-operated laundromat, open 8:30am to 10:00pm daily.

Sites & fire pits

Picnic tables and fire pits at every site, set among mature trees. Firewood $15/bag.

Rental cabin

A rental cabin sleeping 2–6, available year-round.

Gazebo & chalet

Gazebo, chalet and camp-kitchen rentals for groups, reunions and events.

In-season hours

Office open 8:00am–8:00pm daily, May 1 through Thanksgiving Monday.

Before you arrive

Good to know.

A few clear rules keep Devon Lions calm, clean and family-friendly.

1

Check-in & check-out

Check-in 2:00pm–8:00pm, check-out 11:00am. After-8pm arrivals must notify the office.

2

Quiet time

Quiet time is 11:00pm to 8:00am.

3

Minimum stays

2-night minimum on weekends, 3-night minimum on long weekends.

4

Pets

Maximum 3 pets per site, and they must remain leashed at all times.

5

Speed & vehicles

15 km/h speed limit throughout. No ATVs permitted.

6

Tents

One tent per site if tent camping only. Extra tent with an RV is $10.50/night.

7

Cancellations

A $40.00 per-site cancellation fee applies with less than 48 hours' notice.

8

Quiet enjoyment

Karaoke is not permitted at any time. This is a cannabis-free zone.

The surrounding area

Plenty to do, close by.

Devon sits 15 minutes southwest of Edmonton — a quiet base with attractions in every direction.

01
Around Devon

Gardens, golf & the river.

Right nearby: the Devon Golf & Conference Centre, the University of Alberta Botanic Garden and the Leduc #1 Energy Discovery Centre.

On the water there's river touring, canoeing, fishing and tubing — plus RAD Torque Raceway for motorsport fans.

02
Edmonton, minutes away

The city when you want it.

Edmonton's attractions are a short drive away — West Edmonton Mall, the Muttart Conservatory, Telus World of Science and Fort Edmonton Park.

A wide variety of attractions and services can keep the whole family busy for an extended stay.
