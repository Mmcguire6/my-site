DWI Defense

From investigation through jury trial — results in WNY.

A DWI charge is a serious criminal charge in New York State. A conviction can result in thousands of dollars in fines, loss of driving privileges, higher insurance premiums, a criminal record, and even jail time. Mr. Saraceno will work to get a dismissal, reduction in charges, or have the criminal charges dropped altogether.
