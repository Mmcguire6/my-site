The Attorney

Meet Matt Green.

Former municipal court judge. Former Baldwin County prosecutor. Now a fighter for the injured, the accused, and the constitutionally aggrieved.

Attorney at Law · Founding Principal

Matt Green

As a former state prosecutor and counselor at law, Matt Green has been a zealous advocate for victims of crimes, personal injury, and those whose constitutional rights have been violated. As a municipal court judge in both the cities of Mobile and Saraland, Matt has presided over thousands of traffic offenses and criminal cases.

He served as an Assistant District Attorney in Baldwin County under the Honorable David Whetstone, securing convictions in kidnapping, rape, murder, and robbery trials. In 2006, the Mobile City Council appointed him to the Municipal Court bench, where he served until 2015. He currently focuses on personal injury, constitutional law, criminal defense, and traffic cases at The Law Office of Matt Green, LLC.
