Sole practitioner, Airdrie-based, family-rooted. Renée built Short Law in July 2014 on a clear conviction: that clients in the middle of life’s hardest transitions deserve a lawyer who listens first.

Principal · Sole Practitioner

Renée E. Short

Lawyer, advocate, and Airdrie neighbour.

Renée is a lawyer with a focus in family law. She graduated with an LLB from the University of Alberta in 2008. She articled and went on to practice at a full service firm in Edmonton before continuing to practice mainly family law at reputable law firms in Red Deer and Calgary.

Renée opened up her sole practice in Airdrie in July 2014, where she lives with her husband and children. Renée is a member of the Christian Legal Fellowship and is a volunteer for the Calgary Legal Guidance Clinic.
