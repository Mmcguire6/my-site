№ 05 How neurofeedback works

Old-school psychology, modern technology

Neurofeedback is a type of biofeedback for the brain. Using the principle of paired learning — operant conditioning — we train the brain to normalize brainwave activity. As the brain is rewarded for producing normal activity it produces more of it, and with repetition the patterns become memorized and permanent.

  • i.
    qEEG Brain MappingOur brain maps cover 20 locations and measure delta, theta, alpha, beta, and high-beta activity — the various speeds of electrical current in the brain.
  • ii.
    Personalized consultationEach client receives a personalized Brain Map consultation, so you understand exactly what your brainwave activity is doing — and how Neurofeedback will correct it.
  • iii.
    Targeted Neurofeedback therapyTreatment locations and protocols are guided by your brain map — for the highest accuracy and efficacy.
  • iv.
    Lasting changeAs brainwave activity normalizes, symptoms dissipate and can completely resolve — bringing lasting change and mental healing.

№ 10 Common questions

Good to know

Neurofeedback therapy is a type of biofeedback for the brain. It uses the psychological principle of paired learning — operant conditioning — to train the brain to normalize brainwave activity. As the brain is rewarded for producing normal activity, it produces more of it, and with repetition the normalized patterns become memorized and therefore permanent.

To determine the most effective treatment locations, we use advanced qEEG Brain Mapping. Our brain maps cover 20 locations and measure the relationship between delta, theta, alpha, beta, and high-beta activity — the various speeds of electrical current in the brain. The map gives a clear picture of what is in and outside normal limits, and directs the most efficacious Neurofeedback protocols.
