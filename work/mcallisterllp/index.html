Areas of Practice

Five focused areas, handled by lawyers who know the ground.

From the document that protects a family for generations to the business deal that takes a town's worth of work to close — we know what it means to own a business, and what it takes to settle an estate properly.

i.

Estate Planning

Wills, trusts, powers of attorney, and healthcare directives — with attention to tax considerations, asset protection, and long-term care planning. Designed to protect assets, honour wishes, and provide peace of mind.

ii.

Estate Administration

Helping executors, administrators, and beneficiaries navigate settling a loved one's estate — Grant of Probate, gathering assets, paying debts and taxes, distributing inheritances, and dispute resolution.

iii.

Real Estate Transactions

Comprehensive legal services for buying, selling, or leasing property — purchase and sale agreements, leases, financing documents, title examinations, and representation for residential and commercial transactions.

iv.

Business & Corporate

A full range of services for businesses, directors, and shareholders — entity selection, agreement drafting, financing, regulatory compliance, governance, M&A, contracts, and employment matters.

v.

Represented Adult — Guardianship & Trustee

Legal support for matters involving represented adults (formerly known as dependent adults) — guardianship establishment, trusteeship, ongoing administration, court compliance, and decision-making support.
