The Attorney

Meet Tai C. Bogan.

Principal Attorney

Tai Christopher Bogan

B.A. · J.D. · California State Bar Certified Criminal Law Specialist

Tai Bogan attended Modesto Junior College where he was a decorated speech and debate champion and President of Phi Rho Pi, Modesto Chapter, before graduating from the University of the Pacific in 1998 with a degree in Persuasion. He earned his J.D. from Humphreys College, Lawrence Drivon School of Law in 2005.

He joined the San Joaquin County Public Defender's office in 2006, rose through the ranks to become a felony trial attorney — recognized at the office as a "trial dog" — and founded The Bogan Law Firm, A Professional Corporation in 2012.
