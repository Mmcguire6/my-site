Our Flagship

Practice Area 01

Chapter 7 Bankruptcy

The most common form of debt relief.

Chapter 7 is the most common, quickest, and simplest form of debt relief available under the United States Bankruptcy Code. For most clients it offers a complete discharge of qualifying unsecured debts — credit cards, medical bills, payday loans — allowing a genuine fresh financial start. Maurice Graham has guided thousands of Ohio families through Chapter 7, making the process understandable, affordable, and as stress-free as possible.

  • Complete discharge of qualifying unsecured debts
  • Creditor calls and collection actions stop immediately
  • Fast resolution — most cases completed in months

Practice Area 02

Chapter 13 Bankruptcy

Reorganize, protect, and rebuild.

Chapter 13 allows individuals with regular income to reorganize their debts and repay creditors over a three-to-five year structured plan — protecting assets like your home from foreclosure while giving you a manageable path forward. Graham & Associates has helped northeastern Ohio homeowners stop foreclosure sales only days before they were scheduled to occur.

  • Stop foreclosure and save your home
  • Structured repayment plan tailored to your income
  • Keep valuable assets while resolving debt
