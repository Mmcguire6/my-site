Our Approach

The discipline that turns preparation into leverage.

Three principles that have shaped how the Marc Lopez Law Firm works since 2009. The investment isn't theoretical — it shows up in every motion, every cross-examination, every closing argument.

01

Principle One

The Prove It Philosophy

Built to win at trial.

While not all cases go to trial, the philosophy of the Marc Lopez Law Firm is to prepare for every single case as if every case will go to trial. The strengths and weaknesses can only be known once a case has been fully investigated and thoroughly prepared as if it were going to trial.

02

Principle Two

Courtroom Champions

No fear of the room.

When a firm is prepared for trial, that firm does not fear the courtroom. Most attorneys don't want to go to trial, and — quite honestly — don't know how to try cases. Hiring a known trial attorney can benefit every case, even those that don't need to be taken to trial.

03

Principle Three

Former Prosecutors on Your Side

Inside knowledge, outside the office.

Multiple attorneys at the Marc Lopez Law Firm are former prosecutors in Marion County. They know exactly how the state builds its cases — and more importantly, how to dismantle them. That insider knowledge shapes every defense strategy we construct.
