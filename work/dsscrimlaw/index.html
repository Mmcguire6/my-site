About the Firm

40 years of vigorous
defence in Western Canada.

Dawson Criminal Defence is one of Western Canada's largest criminal law firms. From our offices in Edmonton and Fort McMurray, we defend clients against all criminal allegations in trial and appeal courts, including the Supreme Court of Canada.

For over 40 years we have defended thousands of clients accused of criminal offences such as theft, assault, impaired driving (DUI), sexual assault, internet crimes, murder, fraud, uttering threats, drug possession and trafficking.

We also serve clients across Alberta — including communities such as Grande Prairie — and throughout the Northwest Territories, ensuring strong legal representation wherever it is needed.
