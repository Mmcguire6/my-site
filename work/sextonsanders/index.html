The Two Areas Sexton & Sanders Is Known For.

Sexton & Sanders focuses where the stakes are highest and the insurance carriers fight hardest. Two flagship areas anchor the practice: big truck wrecks across state lines, and wrongful death claims that demand both legal precision and compassion.

High-Stakes Litigation
No. 01 · Big Truck Wreck

Big Truck Wreck

When eighteen-wheelers cause life-changing harm.

Truck accident cases are among the most complex in personal injury law — involving multiple liable parties, federal trucking regulations, and insurance companies with deep pockets and aggressive legal teams. We have fought and won truck wreck claims across multiple states, from eighteen-wheeler interstate collisions to hit-and-run semi drivers on surface streets. When a commercial carrier causes your injury, you need lawyers who know this terrain.

  • Multi-state truck wreck experience — Arkansas, Oklahoma, Missouri and beyond.
  • Federal trucking regulation expertise — Powell holds a CDL himself.
  • Fighting multi-party insurance claims with the resources required.

No. 02 · Wrongful Death

Wrongful Death

Justice for families. Closure that holds.

Losing a loved one due to another's negligence is devastating. Our firm handles the entire legal process — from filing estate paperwork to pursuing full compensation — so your family can focus on healing. We have recovered justice for families who lost loved ones to employer negligence, defective truck braking systems, and high-speed police pursuits.

  • Estate filing and probate coordination handled by experienced counsel.
  • Multi-defendant wrongful death cases — employers, manufacturers, public agencies.
  • Compassionate, client-first process from intake through resolution.
