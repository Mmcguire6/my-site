07 / 11 — Our Approach Our Approach · Three Commitments

Our Approach

Personal. Accessible. On contingency.

Three principles have defined how this firm treats every client through nearly two decades of practice in the Mobile area.

01

Pillar I

Personal Relationships

Sympathy through every step.

Eric K. Roberts and staff work hard to develop a personal relationship with each client, sympathizing with the pain you have suffered and keeping you informed every step of the way.

02

Pillar II

Contingency Fee Representation

If we don't recover, you don't pay.

In the majority of cases, Eric K. Roberts will work on a contingency fee agreement — if we do not recover any money on your behalf, you will not have to pay any money out of your pocket.

03

Pillar III

Accessible Counsel

We come to you.

We understand you may not have transportation or may have difficulties meeting during your busy work schedule. We will meet with you in your home, office or any location, and provide evening and weekend appointments if necessary.
