Principal Lawyer

Marti E. Wilson, B.A. LL.B.

Sault Ste. Marie · Toronto · Banff · Guelph

Born and raised in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario, Marti received her Bachelor of Arts in English Literature from the University of Guelph and her law degree from Queen's University law school. She articled in Toronto with a boutique litigation firm and was called to the Ontario bar in 1992.

In 1998 she headed out to Banff, Alberta for a change of scenery and stayed for almost thirteen years — joining the Alberta bar in 2004 and practising with Eric Harvie Law Office from 2006 until 2011. That year, she moved back to Guelph to be closer to extended family and friends, practising with Ted Farley at Farley Law Office from 2011 to December 2016 before opening Wilson Law.

When she is not working, Marti sings in the University of Guelph Symphonic Choir, volunteers at an animal sanctuary, plays golf, practices yoga, goes to the Guelph YMCA/YWCA, and follows the Blue Jays.
