The Attorney

Principal Attorney

Kurt W. Bruderly

Pronounced "Brewed-early"

Kurt W. Bruderly is the principal attorney at Law KWB, dedicated to providing dynamic criminal defense representation to clients across the Metro Toledo area. His practice spans the full range of criminal charges — from state felonies and federal investigations to DUI/OVI, juvenile matters, and expungements — with a commitment to actively engaging each client to fully comprehend their legal position and achieve their goals for the future.

Kurt received his Juris Doctorate from the University of Toledo College of Law ('07), after first receiving his Bachelor's Degree in Economics from the University of Michigan ('03). While in law school, Kurt worked as an extern, gaining hands-on experience in Ohio courtrooms long before opening the practice that today serves Lucas, Wood, and surrounding counties.
