Biography

From McCallie to The DOJ.

A Chattanooga lawyer with significant institutional depth.

J. Micah Guster III is an experienced attorney serving clients across Tennessee and Georgia. Born and raised in Chattanooga, he graduated from the McCallie School, earned a full scholarship to Tennessee State University, and went on to attend the University of Arkansas School of Law.

Micah's career includes working in the Tennessee State Senate, the U.S. House of Representatives, and serving as a trial attorney with the U.S. Department of Justice during the Great Recession. At Advice Law Firm, he is committed to protecting his clients' rights with skill, integrity, and personal attention.
