Our Attorneys

A father, a son, one practice.

An international origin story — pre-Independence Rhodesia, Harare, England, and Turlock, California — all flowing into one Fresno firm built on faith, service, and rigorous criminal defence.

Attorney & Founder

Christopher J. Caine

Law Degree (with Honours), University of Rhodesia, 1973.

Christopher Caine was born in Zimbabwe (then Rhodesia) and graduated at the University of Rhodesia, receiving his Law Degree with Honours in 1973. His was one of the last classes in pre-Independence Rhodesia to be admitted to the Bar under the external examination. He brought those deep foundations of legal principle and advocacy to California, where he founded The Caine Law Firm to serve the Central Valley.

  • University of Rhodesia · Law Degree with Honours, 1973
  • Admitted, State Bar of California
  • Criminal Defence, DUI & Felony Defence

Attorney

Gary Caine

Harare · England · the U.S. Navy · Fresno.

Gary was born in Harare, Zimbabwe in 1976, and raised in England until immigrating to America with his parents in his late teens. After finishing high school in Turlock, California in 1994, Gary served in the U.S. Navy from 1994 to 1997, receiving several awards and honours during his service before pursuing his legal career alongside his father.

  • Turlock High School, California, 1994
  • U.S. Navy Service, 1994–1997
  • Admitted, State Bar of California
