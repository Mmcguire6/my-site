№ 04 Meet your provider

Erin Bailey

PMHNP · MSN · RN — Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner

Erin Bailey has been practicing as a nurse since 1996. She specialized and graduated from Allen College in Waterloo, Iowa with a certification as a Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner in 2019. Since then she has continued to grow in her knowledge and experience in treating patients and working alongside other providers in the same profession.

She strives to make every patient feel heard, understood, and to involve them as much as possible in their care to help ensure therapeutic treatment success.

“I have grown up in the rural communities of Iowa. I choose to specialize in Mental Health because growing up in this area I realize that there is still quiet a stigma around Mental Health, and I have personally struggled myself, along with loved ones. I look forward to utilizing my skills to help others and their loved ones on their own Mental Health Journeys!”
