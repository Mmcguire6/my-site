Practice Area 01

DUI Defense

The clock starts ticking the moment they take your license.

When you're arrested for a DUI, the police officer will confiscate your license and issue you an Order of Suspension. It is absolutely critical that you contact an experienced attorney to request an Administrative Hearing from the DMV within 10 days of being issued your Order of Suspension — once this time limit has elapsed, it is too late to prevent the administrative suspension. Driving under the influence can have costly consequences, including jail time, fines, probation, and license suspension, and Mr. Nagra's prosecutorial experience gives him a unique understanding of how to dismantle the state's case.

  • DMV Administrative Hearing filed within the critical 10-day window
  • Criminal court DUI defense — misdemeanor and felony
  • Former prosecutor's strategic insight applied to every step
