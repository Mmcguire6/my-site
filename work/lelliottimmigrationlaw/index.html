The Lawyer

Meet Corey J. Lelliott.

Principal of Lelliott Immigration Law — barrister and solicitor, immigrant, and the only handshake on every file.

CL

Barrister & Solicitor · Principal

Corey J. Lelliott

LL.B. · B.A. · Barrister & Solicitor (Ontario)

Corey J. Lelliott is the principal of Lelliott Immigration Law, a boutique firm focusing exclusively on Canadian Immigration and Citizenship Law. Having immigrated to Canada from New Zealand himself, Corey brings firsthand experience with the Canadian immigration system to every client file. In New Zealand, he graduated from the University of Canterbury (Christchurch) with degrees in Law and Psychology, and practiced as a Solicitor in New Zealand for several years following his call to the Bar in 2004.

Since immigrating to Canada in 2008, Corey has worked exclusively in and gained extensive experience in Canadian Immigration, Refugee and Citizenship Law. From 2011 to 2019, he held the designation of Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant, having completed an Immigration Consultant Certificate program with Honors at Humber College in 2010. He was called to the bar in Ontario in June 2019.
