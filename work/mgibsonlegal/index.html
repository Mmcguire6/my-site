Matthew E. Gibson

B.A. Honours · J.D. (Dean's List) · Called to the Bar, Ontario, 2013

Matthew Gibson is Hamilton's local real estate lawyer with a client-focused approach. A proud graduate of McMaster University (B.A. Hons, 2008) and Queen's University (J.D., Dean's List, 2012), Matthew calls Hamilton home with his wife and their various pets.

Matthew is an experienced lawyer, having started his career on Bay Street before spending several years working in-house in the craft beverage industry. Combining his big-firm experience, client-focused mindset and his passion for real estate, Matthew is excited to offer his expertise to first-time homebuyers and experienced real estate investors alike.

Whether it is purchasing a condo or a new investment property, Matthew will tailor his approach to get clients the results they need. In his spare time he can be found renovating his East Hamilton home and hiking the Escarpment.
