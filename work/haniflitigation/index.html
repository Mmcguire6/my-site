Specialty Practice

When the regulator becomes the prosecutor.

For licensed professionals, the most consequential matter rarely arrives as a criminal charge — it arrives as a complaint to your regulatory body, and it can end a career as decisively as a courtroom verdict.

Hanif Litigation applies a criminal-law lens to quasi-criminal regulatory matters — from preliminary information requests through to contested disciplinary hearings, including matters before professional bodies such as the College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC.

Practice

Shahid has appeared in all levels of court in BC and has gained experience advocating for clients by regularly appearing in contested applications in court — both during articles and in his own practice.

During articles in Northern BC, Shahid appeared and assisted on serious criminal matters, including criminal negligence causing death. After he completed his articles and began his career as a young downtown lawyer, he also appeared and assisted on serious quasi-criminal matters, including civil contempt applications and regulatory matters that involved dealing with professional bodies, such as the College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC.
