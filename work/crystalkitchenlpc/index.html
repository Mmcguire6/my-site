№ 02 Let's talk about how I can help

A safe place to talk & be heard — without judgement

If you are looking for a safe place to talk and be heard without judgement — if you are enduring and not embracing a life transition — if it's time to acquire new life skills to deal with life's challenges, then I'm the therapist you are looking for. I specialize in integrating cognitive behavioral therapy with other effective evidence-based treatments to help people conquer issues such as social anxiety, relationship issues, and depressed moods. As your therapist I can help you navigate the challenges of life — overcoming the frustration, anger, and resentment that have stood in the way of emotional wellness, growth, and healing.

№ 04 My mission

Three commitments behind every session

i.

Breaking generational trauma

Helping families break cycles of generational trauma, abuse, and neglect — so the next chapter isn't a repeat of the last.

ii.

Uncovering inner strength

Helping individuals look within themselves and acknowledge the inner strength that was there all along.

iii.

Community empowerment

Providing the entire community with therapeutic tools to uplift and empower them to live their best life.
