Practice Area II

Wrongful Discharge

When you're fired — or forced to resign.

If you've been unfairly fired from your job, you're not looking for soothing words and legal jargon — you're looking to get your job back, or to be compensated for your loss of income. When intolerable treatment has driven you to resign, the law still recognizes that as wrongful. Thomas Sobecki represents workers in State and Federal Courts seeking reinstatement or full compensation for the income they were unjustly denied.

  • Wrongful termination and constructive dismissal claims
  • Reinstatement, back pay, and front pay recovery
  • Retaliation tied to whistleblower and protected activity
