Firm Overview

A Vegreville firm, built on community.

Cohoe Law was established in 2013 by Lori-Ann Cohoe. Lori-Ann has proudly served the community of Vegreville and surrounding areas since 1993. We moved to our current location in May 2016.

Our entire staff take pride in providing quality services to our clients. Our lawyers serve Vegreville as well as clients throughout the surrounding area. We have conducted matters in Edmonton, St. Paul, Wainwright, Vermillion, Lloydminster, Wetaskiwin and surrounding areas in both the Provincial and Queen's Bench levels of Court.

Who We Are

Lori-Ann Cohoe

B.A. · LL.B. · Alberta Bar, 1993

“In 2013, Lori-Ann decided to move to her own practice to better serve her Clients.”

Lori-Ann Cohoe grew up in the Westlock area. She attended the University of Alberta where she obtained a Bachelor of Arts Degree as well as her Bachelor of Law. Lori-Ann began her legal career in Vegreville with a large firm.

Lori-Ann has a daughter and 3 grandchildren and enjoys watching hockey and the outdoors. Her deep roots in Vegreville — stretching back to 1993 — mean she knows her clients, her community, and the matters that matter here.
