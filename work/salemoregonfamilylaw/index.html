ii. · Juvenile Defense

Juvenile Defense

Standing between your child and the system.

When a child is charged with a crime, it affects the entire family. Whether your child has committed a crime, been wrongfully accused, or has simply made a mistake, the guidance of a Juvenile Defense lawyer can help you get through this difficult time. Lindsay Soto assists juvenile clients and their families by negotiating with the prosecution to reduce the charges and by working to ensure children are charged as minors rather than adults.

  • Negotiation to reduce charges
  • Representation before DHS and youth courts
  • Both court-appointed and privately retained cases
