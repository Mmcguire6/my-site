Impaired Driving

When your licence and your future are on the line.

Impaired driving charges carry severe consequences — licence suspension, criminal records, and potential incarceration. Hadford Defence Group's lawyers bring deep expertise to every aspect of DUI defence, rigorously examining roadside procedures, breath test results, and Charter rights to achieve the best possible outcome for every client.
