About the park

A wilderness park by the lake.

Hilah Ayers Wilderness RV Park is a 45-acre gated campground just half a kilometre from the shores of Pigeon Lake, with 208 seasonal sites and 34 overnight sites — all fully serviced with power, water and sewer.

It's less than an hour south of Edmonton, with a public boat launch within walking distance and a real sense of the outdoors right at your site.

Gated & secure24-hour security cameras and a gated entrance throughout the park.

The sites

Seasonal or overnight.

Two ways to stay — settle in for the season, or roll in for a night or two. Booking is online through Campspot.

Seasonal sites

208 fully-serviced seasonal lots

  • Full hookups — power, water & sewer
  • Tenant events through the season
  • The Hideaway & The Meadows areas
  • 2026 lease agreement available

Overnight sites

34 fully-serviced overnight lots

  • Full hookups for a night or a week
  • Book online through Campspot
  • Shower house, laundry & dump station
  • Full run of the park's amenities

Seasonal and overnight rates are listed on the park's booking pages. Overnight reservations are made online through Campspot — call (780) 389-4071 with any questions about seasonal lots or availability.

Things to do

There's a lot going on.

This is a park built for doing things — not just parking the trailer.

01
On the grounds

Mini-golf, movies & more.

A 9-hole mini-golf course, beach volleyball, horseshoe pits, walking trails and an outdoor movie theatre — there's something for every evening.

Park-like settings give kids room to play, and James & Tasha's Camping Kitchen food trailer is on site (opening May 9).

02
The essentials

Everything you need on site.

A shower house and laundry facilities, a dumping station, and a gated entrance with 24-hour security cameras throughout.

It's a true wilderness park — the outdoors at your door, with the comforts handled.
