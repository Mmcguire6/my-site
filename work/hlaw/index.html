Practice Areas

From the first will you sign in your forties to the executor letter you receive in your sixties — Hergott Law walks every Okanagan family through the documents that shape their legacy.

I

Wills & Trusts

Legally sound wills and customised trust arrangements that clearly outline your wishes for asset distribution — the foundation document of a well-crafted estate plan.

Wills Trusts Estate Plans

II

Enduring Powers of Attorney

Appoint a trusted individual to manage your financial and legal affairs if you lose the cognitive capacity to do so yourself — the most important document most people never get around to.

POA Incapacity Finance
Learn More
III

Representation Agreements

British Columbia's signature health-care planning instrument — appointing the right people to make medical and personal care decisions on your behalf, in language that holds up in a hospital room.

Health Care Personal Care BC Specific
Learn More
IV

Advance Directives

Clear, written instructions about the medical treatment you do — and do not — want, in the event you cannot speak for yourself. The document that protects both you and the family being asked to decide.

Medical End-of-Life Directive
Learn More
V

Probate & Administration

Achieving an estate grant — whether that's with a will (probate) or without (letters of administration). The court process that gives an executor the legal authority to actually act.

Probate Letters of Admin. Estate Grant

Executor Services
