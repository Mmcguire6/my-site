02 / How it works

Here’s what happens behind the scenes.

The Peak System connects three essential parts of your business.

  • 01Your Customer Touchpoints

    Customers reach your business through phone calls, website forms, and messages. The Peak System captures these inquiries and organizes customer information so you know who’s reaching out and what they need.

  • 02Your Central Platform

    Your conversations, contacts, estimates, and opportunities live in one organized workspace. See new inquiries, manage active opportunities, track outstanding estimates, and review previous interactions without searching through multiple tools.

  • 03Your Automations

    The system responds to specific events in your business. A missed call can trigger an immediate text. An outstanding estimate can trigger a follow-up reminder. A completed job can trigger a review request. Everything is connected, with the timing and messaging configured around your business.

05 / Automation

The right action. At the right time.

Not every task needs your attention. The Peak System handles repetitive communication through automations designed specifically for home-service businesses.

  • Missed Call Recovery

    When you can’t answer the phone, an automatic text starts the conversation so the customer isn’t left waiting.

  • Estimate Follow-Up

    Friendly reminders keep outstanding estimates from being forgotten, and stop when the customer responds.

  • Reputation Management

    Automatic review requests make it easy for customers to share their experience.

  • Customer Retention

    Seasonal reminders and reactivation campaigns help you reconnect with people who’ve already done business with you.
