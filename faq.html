Getting started

What does Northern Peak Systems actually do?
We help home service businesses answer every lead and book more jobs. The Peak System is a fast website plus missed call text-back, estimate follow-up, lead management and booking, review requests, and customer retention — all connected. Instead of a pile of disconnected apps that don’t talk to each other, you get one system where everything works together.
What happens on the demo?
It’s a free 15-minute conversation. We learn about your business, show you the system running, answer your questions, and recommend the right plan. If it’s a fit, you subscribe, tell us about your business in your Client Hub, and we build your website and system — most businesses are live within 7–10 days of completing setup. Book a Demo whenever you’re ready.
Is the demo really free?
Yes — the demo costs nothing and there’s no commitment. We’d rather show you what the system does than pressure you into it.
How do I get started?
Subscribe online from the pricing page in a few minutes, or Book a Demo or send us a message if you’d like to talk first. Once you subscribe, you tell us about your business and share your logo and photos in your Client Hub, and our team takes it from there.

Pricing & plans

How much does it cost?
Two simple monthly plans. The Base System is $97/month — we build your website free, and the monthly covers hosting, security, unlimited edits, and local SEO. The Peak System is $297/month flat and includes everything: your website plus missed call text-back, estimate follow-up, lead management and booking, reputation management, and customer retention. No setup fees, no contracts. See pricing.
Is there a big upfront cost?
No. There are no setup fees and no upfront build cost. You pay one flat monthly fee for the plan you choose, starting when you subscribe.
Can I sign up online?
Yes. Choose a plan on the pricing page and subscribe online in a few minutes. Your first month is charged at checkout, then you tell us about your business in your Client Hub and our team handles the rest. Prefer to talk first? Book a free demo.
Are there long-term contracts?
No contracts — it’s month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days’ notice. You stay because the system works for your business, not because you’re locked in.
What’s included in the monthly fee?
Both plans include your website, hosting, security, unlimited edits, local SEO, and ongoing support. The Peak System adds missed call text-back, estimate follow-up, lead management and booking, reputation management, and customer retention — all for one flat $297/month. No add-ons to piece together.

What we build

What is The Peak System?
It’s your website plus everything around it — missed call text-back, estimate follow-up, lead management and booking, reputation management, and customer retention — all connected so they work as one. Most businesses run these as separate apps that don’t talk to each other; we wire them together.
What is missed call text-back?
When a call to your business goes unanswered, the system texts the caller back within seconds — starting a conversation that ends in a booking instead of a lost lead. It runs 24/7, including nights and weekends.
Is my data and client information secure?
Yes. Messages and lead data are handled securely, kept only as long as you want them, and managed with Canadian privacy rules in mind. We walk through the specifics for your business during setup.
Do you do SEO?
Yes. Local SEO is included in both plans — we help you rank in Google’s map results and get found by customers searching in your area.
What if I already have a website?
That’s fine. We can rebuild it, improve it, or connect the system to what you already have. Book a Demo and we’ll tell you straight whether to rebuild or build on what you’ve got.
How long does it take to launch?
Most businesses are live within 7–10 days of completing setup. Setups with several integrations can take a little longer — we’ll give you a clear timeline up front.

Ownership & support

Do I own my website?
Your domain and everything you give us (logo, photos, copy) are yours. The website itself is built and owned by us, and your monthly plan keeps it hosted, secure, and up to date. That’s how we can build it free with no setup fee. If you ever cancel, you keep your domain and content; if you’d rather own the site outright, ask us about a one-time buyout.
What kind of support do I get?
Direct support from the team that builds your system — no call centres, no ticket queues. Edits and questions are handled quickly.
What areas do you serve?
We’re based in Edmonton, Alberta, and work with home service businesses across Canada and the US. Everything is handled remotely, so location is never a barrier.
What if I want to cancel?
No contracts means no hassle. If it’s not working for you, just email us with 30 days’ notice. No cancellation fee, and you keep your domain and any content you provided. We’d rather earn your business every month than trap you in it.
