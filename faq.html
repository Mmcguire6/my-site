Getting started

What does Northern Peak Systems actually do?
We build connected business systems for small businesses — a fast website plus the AI automation, booking, reviews, and SEO tools to grow it. Instead of a pile of disconnected apps that don’t talk to each other, you get one system where everything works together.
How does the free demo work?
Tell us about your business and we set up a live AI receptionist demo tuned to it — then you call in, hear it handle a real intake, and watch the lead land in an inbox. Love it? We roll it out on your line. If not, no obligation. Book your free demo →
Is the demo really free?
Yes — there’s no charge and no commitment to hear the demo. We’d rather show you what the AI can do than try to sell it to you over a call.
How do I get started?
Book a free demo or send us a message. We’ll ask a couple of quick questions, build your demo, and take it from there — at your pace, with no pressure.

Pricing & plans

How much does it cost?
Pricing is simple and monthly. Your website foundation starts at $99/month, and you add only the systems you need — like the AI receptionist, lead qualification, or local SEO. Build your own plan and see the price update instantly.
Is there a big upfront cost?
No. There’s no large setup fee or upfront build cost. You pay one predictable monthly fee for the services you choose, and you only start once you’ve approved your demo.
Are there long-term contracts?
No long-term contracts. You stay because the system works for your business, not because you’re locked in.
What’s included in the monthly fee?
Every plan includes hosting, security, backups, and ongoing support — plus unlimited edits to your site. Beyond that, you only pay for the systems you switch on, like automation, reviews, or SEO.

What we build

What is a “business system”?
It’s your website plus the tools around it — lead capture, AI follow-up, booking, reviews, SEO, and reporting — all connected so they work as one. Most businesses run these as separate apps that don’t talk to each other; we wire them together.
What is the AI receptionist?
A 24/7 AI that answers your calls and texts, qualifies leads, answers common questions, and books jobs straight into your calendar — so you never miss a customer, even after hours.
Can it handle my law firm’s intake?
Yes — law firms are our specialty. It runs client intake for firms: leads with empathy, qualifies by practice area, is conflict-aware (it captures the opposing party), books consultations, and texts you the lead with a recording. See our law-firm page for the full breakdown.
Is my data and client information secure?
Yes. Calls are handled securely, recordings and transcripts are kept only as long as you want them, and data is handled with Canadian privacy rules in mind. We walk through the specifics for your business during setup.
Will the AI receptionist replace my staff?
No — it’s an overflow and after-hours front desk. It catches the calls your team can’t: nights, weekends, lunch, and when everyone is already on the phone.
Do you do SEO?
Yes. Local SEO is one of the systems you can add — we help you rank in Google’s map results and get found by customers searching in your area.
What if I already have a website?
That’s fine. We can rebuild it, improve it, or connect automation and systems to what you already have. The free demo is the easiest way to see what an upgrade would look like.
How long does it take to launch?
Most websites launch within one to two weeks after you approve your demo. More complex systems with several integrations can take a little longer — we’ll give you a clear timeline up front.

Ownership & support

Do I own my website?
Yes. Your website and content are yours. We handle the hosting, security, and upkeep as part of your plan so you don’t have to think about it.
What kind of support do I get?
Founder-led, direct support. You work with the person actually building your system — no account managers, no ticket queues. Edits and questions are handled quickly.
What areas do you serve?
We’re founder-led in Edmonton, Alberta, and work with small businesses across Canada and the US. Everything is handled remotely, so location is never a barrier.
What if I want to cancel?
No contracts means no hassle. If it’s not working for you, you can cancel — we’d rather earn your business every month than trap you in it.
