What the studio provides.

I design and build websites for small-to-mid professional services firms. Engagements are run on a single inclusive monthly plan that covers design, build, hosting, ongoing edits, security patches, and small additions. Specifics of any individual engagement — page count, content scope, integrations — are documented in a service agreement signed by both parties before work begins.

Pricing and payment.

  • $0 down to sign. No deposit is collected when the service agreement is signed.
  • $250 per month, all-inclusive. The monthly plan begins on your site’s launch day and covers design, build, hosting, edits, security, and small additions.

  • Major add-ons are quoted separately. New features outside the agreed scope (booking systems, e-commerce, custom calculators, full redesigns) are quoted in writing and rolled into the monthly plan when added.

Invoices are sent monthly. Payment is due within fourteen days of receipt. Late payments more than thirty days past due may result in suspension of the monthly plan until brought current.
