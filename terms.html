What the studio provides.

I design and build websites for small-to-mid professional services firms. Engagements run as either a one-time lump-sum build (you own the site outright) or an inclusive monthly subscription (the studio hosts and maintains the site as a service). Specifics of any individual engagement — page count, content scope, integrations, included pages — are documented in a service agreement signed by both parties before work begins.

Pricing and payment.

Lump sum tier.

  • $3,800 one-time. Covers design, hand-coded build, up to five pages, mobile-responsive layout, on-page SEO setup, contact form, domain & SSL setup, and 30 days of post-launch support.
  • + $25 / month hosting after launch (optional). If the studio continues to host the site. The client may also host the code with any provider of their choosing.
  • Add-ons: $150 per additional page beyond five, $250 to add a blog, $50/month for unlimited edits.
  • Payment terms: 50% deposit on signing, 50% at launch. Both invoices due within fourteen days of receipt.
  • Ownership: the client owns the site, code, and design outright at the end of the engagement. No subscription required after launch.

Monthly subscription tier.

  • $0 down to sign. No deposit is collected when the service agreement is signed.
  • $199 per month, all-inclusive. The monthly subscription begins on your site’s launch day and covers design, build, hosting, ongoing edits, security patches, lifetime updates, and small additions.
  • 12-month minimum commitment. The first 12 months of the subscription are committed; after that, the subscription continues month-to-month indefinitely.
  • Add-ons: $150 per additional page beyond five, $250 to add a blog. Major new features (e-commerce, booking systems, custom calculators, full redesigns) are quoted in writing and rolled into the monthly when added.
  • Domain and SSL fees are passed through at cost — the domain is registered in your firm’s name and owned by you, regardless of subscription status.
  • Ownership: the built site (code, design, deployed URL) remains the property of the studio. This is a subscription, not a buyout. See Cancellation below for details.

Invoices are sent monthly for the subscription tier, or per the deposit/launch schedule for the lump-sum tier. Payment is due within fourteen days of receipt. Late payments more than thirty days past due may result in suspension of services and, for the subscription tier, the site going offline until brought current.

Cancellation — monthly subscription only.

The lump-sum tier has no recurring obligation; once the engagement is complete and the site is delivered, there is nothing to cancel. The cancellation rules below apply only to the monthly subscription.

Before the 12-month minimum — if you cancel before completing the 12-month minimum, an early termination fee equal to the remaining months of the commitment multiplied by the $199 monthly rate is due on cancellation. For example, if you cancel at month 6, the remaining 6 months × $199 = $1,194 is owed. This is what makes $0 down financially viable for a one-person studio.

After the 12-month minimum — you may cancel at any time with 30 days’ written notice, no early termination fee. At the end of the notice period, the subscription ends and the site is taken offline.

The monthly subscription does not include a buyout or upgrade-to-ownership option. Clients who want to own the site outright should choose the lump-sum tier from the start, or be prepared to commission a fresh build with a different vendor at the end of the subscription. This separation is how the $0-down monthly pricing is financially viable for a one-person studio.

What you keep on cancellation.

If your monthly subscription ends for any reason, you retain ownership of:

  • Your domain. Registered in your firm’s name from day one, transferred to a registrar of your choosing if needed.
  • Your content. Anything you provided to the studio — logo, photos, written copy, branding files — is yours to take to whatever new vendor you engage.
  • Anything you produced during the engagement. Bios you wrote, photos you commissioned, blog posts you authored.

What does not transfer on cancellation of the monthly subscription:

  • The built site itself — its code, design, layout, custom typography work, hosting setup, and deployed URL. These remain the property of the studio and the live site goes offline at the end of the subscription period.

(Lump-sum clients have no equivalent loss — they own the site outright from the start of the engagement, and nothing changes if their hosting arrangement with the studio ends.)
