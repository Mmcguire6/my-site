Frequently asked questions

Got questions? We’ve got answers.

Everything you need to know about estimate follow-up and how it works with The Peak System.

How does the estimate follow-up work?

When you send an estimate, it’s added to your pipeline and a follow-up sequence starts. Friendly check-ins go out on the schedule you choose — for example on day 3 and day 7 — until the customer replies.

Can I customize the messages?

Yes. We write the messages and the timing with you during setup so they sound like your business, and you can change them whenever you like.

What if the customer replies?

Their reply lands in your inbox, you get an alert on your phone, and the follow-ups stop for that estimate — so nobody gets a reminder after they’ve already answered.

Does it work with my current estimating software?

Estimates you send from The Peak System start follow-ups on their own. If you quote in another program, you can mark the estimate as sent in your pipeline and the follow-ups start from there. Bring your software up on your demo call and we’ll show you how it fits.

Will it send too many messages?

No. Follow-ups only go out on the schedule you set — usually a couple of friendly check-ins — and they stop as soon as the customer replies.
