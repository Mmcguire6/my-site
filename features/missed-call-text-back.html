Frequently asked questions

Got questions? We’ve got answers.

What does the text message say?

Whatever fits your business. A typical one reads: “Hi! Thanks for calling [your business] — sorry we missed you. How can we help?” We write it with you during setup so it sounds like you, not a robot.

Can I customize the message?

Yes. We write it with you during the build so it sounds like your business, and we can change the wording whenever you like.

What if the customer replies?

Their reply lands in your Peak System inbox and you get an alert on your phone, so you can pick up the conversation right away. The call, the texts and their details stay together on the customer’s record.

Do I need to answer the texts myself?

The first text goes out automatically, within seconds of the missed call. After that, you or anyone on your team can reply from the app on your phone or from a computer — whoever is free.
