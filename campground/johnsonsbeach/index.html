Welcome to Johnson's Beach

South side of Red Deer Lake — between Bashaw and Ponoka.

We are looking forward to a great season in 2026. Please watch the Summer Events page and join us for some exciting activities this summer. We appreciate your patience as we move forward with improvements to the park.

Located between Bashaw and Ponoka on the south side of Red Deer Lake, Johnson's Beach is less than 30 minutes from 4 different golf courses — Ponoka, Bashaw, Country 9 and Silver Creek.

Open May through September. Dates subject to change — please call to confirm.

From the front gate

"Enjoy your stay. Please be courteous of others." — the welcome that's greeted every camper for years.

Three ways to stay

Pick your stay length.

A few nights, a full month, or the whole summer — all on the same shore.

Overnight & Weekly

Non-power sites from $25/night + GST. 15-amp power sites from $30/night + GST. Add GST to all rates.

Monthly Stays

Non-power $500/month + GST. 15-amp power $600/month + GST. Settle in for a longer summer stretch.

Full Season (May 1 – Sept 30)

Non-power seasonal $1,300 + GST. 15-amp power seasonal $1,400 + GST. The whole summer, one spot.

On the grounds

A working set of amenities.

The campground keeps it simple — what you need to be comfortable, without the chrome.

Picnic shelter & concession

A covered shelter for rain-day refuge plus the on-site concession (where you register on arrival).

Mini-golf

A 9-hole course on the grounds. Day play, or grab a season pass — $20 individual, $50 family for 2026.

Fire pits at every site

Firewood available for purchase on the grounds. Quiet time 11:00 pm until 7:00 am.

Pets welcome

Bring the dog. Standard leash and clean-up rules — all pets on a leash at all times.

Lake access on foot

Walking distance to the lake. Note: no motorized boats — keeping the lake quiet.

Family-friendly

Playgrounds, horseshoe pits, cornhole boards and ladder golf — plus the kind of sunsets the kids will remember.
