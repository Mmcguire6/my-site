Location & contact

56309 Range Road 13, Onoway.

About thirty minutes northwest of Edmonton — between Morinville and Onoway, 5 km north of Highway 642 on Range Road 13. Look for the wagon-wheel iron gate and the white-and-green Memory Lane sign on the east side of the road.

Driving directions

Three ways to get here.

From Edmonton, from south of the city, or from Highway 43 — all routes meet on Range Road 13, just north of Highway 642.

From Edmonton.

West on Highway 16 to Highway 44. North on Highway 44 about 27 km / 17 mi to Highway 642. West on Highway 642 about 16 km / 10 mi to Range Road 13. Turn north and follow Range Road 13 about 5 km / 3 mi to the campground.

From south of Edmonton.

Take Highway 2 to the Anthony Henday West (AB 216) and continue to the junction with Highway 16. From there, follow the route above — west on 16 to Highway 44, then north to Highway 642, west to Range Road 13, and north 5 km to the campground.

From Highway 43.

Turn east at Onoway. From Onoway, travel 10 km / 6 mi north on Highway 777 to Highway 642. East on Highway 642 for 6.5 km / 4 mi to Range Road 13. North on Range Road 13 about 5 km / 3 mi to the campground.

A quick note: Memory Lane is gated, with an on-site caretaker. The office closes at 8 PM — please call ahead if you'll arrive after that so we can have your site ready.
