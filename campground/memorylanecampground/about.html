About the campground

Country camping, built for the long stays.

Memory Lane Campground & RV Park is a quiet, gated country campground thirty minutes northwest of Edmonton — built around large fully-serviced sites, an on-site caretaker, and the small things that make a country stay easy. Daily, weekly, monthly and seasonal campers are all welcome.

The campground

Forty-five large sites in the country.

Memory Lane sits in the quiet Alberta countryside between Morinville and Onoway, five kilometres north of Highway 642 on Range Road 13. The grounds are open and green, with a stocked trout pond at the front, a walking trail through the trees, a playground for the kids, and birdwatching the kind of country campers go looking for.

Every one of the forty-five sites is large and fully-serviced — power, water and sewer, with both 30-amp and 50-amp service available. Sites are 30, 35 or 40 feet wide and 65 feet long, so there's room for big rigs and slide-outs without anybody on top of anybody. The campground is served with Edmonton city water.

At every site there's a fire pit and a picnic table. The grounds are gated with an on-site caretaker, fully wheelchair accessible — including the washroom facility, with wheelchair-accessible picnic tables available on request. A small campground store carries basic supplies, ice and treats; firewood and propane are sold at the office. Memory Lane is an eco-friendly location.

On the grounds

What's on offer.

The campground sites, the on-site facilities, and the activities and wildlife that make Memory Lane more than just a place to park the rig.

Large full-service sites

30, 35 or 40 ft wide and 65 ft long — power, water and sewer, with 30 and 50 amp service. Big-rig friendly, room for decks.

Fire pit & picnic table

Every site has its own fire pit and picnic table — proper country evenings, every night.

Edmonton city water

The campground is served with Edmonton city water — reliable and good.

Safe & gated · caretaker

A safe, gated facility with an on-site caretaker — and a power gate that closes with the office.

Wheelchair accessible

Fully wheelchair accessible — including the washroom facility. Wheelchair-accessible picnic tables on request.

Walking trail

A walking trail leaves from the grounds, lined with wildflowers in season — a short, easy country stroll.

Stocked trout pond

A stocked rainbow trout pond on the grounds — pleasant to watch and even more fun to fish. Father's Day & Mother's Day fishing weekends are an annual favourite.

Playground & lawn games

A playground for the kids, with croquet and other lawn games — bring your own if you'd like.

Birdwatching & wildlife

Great Gray owls are frequent fliers, springtime brings the baby rabbits, and the wading birds are easy to find on the causeway at nearby Sandy Beach.

Campground store

Basic supplies, ice and treats at the store. Firewood and propane sold at the office — bring marshmallows.

Eco-friendly

Memory Lane is an eco-friendly location — quiet hours respected, fireworks prohibited, and a leave-no-trace ethos.

Stay your way

Daily, weekly, monthly and seasonal campers are all welcome. Groups too.

Things to do

More than a place to park.

A stocked trout pond, a playground, lawn games, a walking trail and the kind of country wildlife you go looking for. Plus a couple of special weekends each season.
