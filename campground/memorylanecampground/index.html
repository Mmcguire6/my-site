About the campground

Enjoy camping in the countryside.

Memory Lane Campground & RV Park offers large sites with full services in a safe, gated location close to Edmonton — power, water and sewer, with a fire pit and picnic table at every site.

It's a quiet country retreat just a short drive from Edmonton, St. Albert, Spruce Grove and Stony Plain, with an on-site caretaker and full wheelchair accessibility. Daily, weekly, monthly and seasonal campers are all welcome.

Big-rig friendlySites are 30, 35 or 40 ft wide and 65 ft long, served with Edmonton city water.

A day at Memory Lane

The kind of place that earns its name.

Big country skies, a fire pit at every site, and the kind of quiet you remember.

1

Country mornings

Coffee on your own deck, the walking trail through wildflowers, and a line in the water at the stocked rainbow trout pond.

2

Easy afternoons

Croquet and lawn games in the playground, the kids on a kite-string, and birdwatching for Great Gray owls in the aspens.

3

Big sky evenings

Light the fire pit on your site — firewood and propane sold at the office — then watch a wide-open prairie sky go from sunset to stars.
