Good to know

The questions most asked.

Pulled directly from the campground's FAQ — the practical stuff guests want to know before they pull in.

Where is the campground?

Thirty minutes northwest of Edmonton, Alberta, between Morinville and Onoway — 5 km north of Highway 642 on Range Road 13. Full driving directions are on the location page.

What is the check-in period?

You can check in any time between 2 PM and 8 PM. The office is open 8 AM to 8 PM daily.

What is check-out time?

11 AM.

How do I pay?

Cash (Canadian funds), MasterCard, Visa, American Express and JCB card.

How do I make a reservation?

Call 1-780-995-5555. Daily, weekly, monthly and seasonal rates are available — see the rates page for the full list.

Can I have a fire?

Yes — a fire pit is provided on every site. Fireworks are not permitted on the grounds.

Is firewood available?

Yes — firewood is for sale at the office. Propane is also available.

Are pets welcome?

Yes — $5 per pet. Pets must be on a leash at all times (no exceptions), kept quiet, never left unattended, and cleaned up after. Pets are not permitted in the playground area.

Is the campground wheelchair accessible?

Yes — the campground is fully wheelchair accessible, including the washroom facility. A wheelchair-accessible picnic table is available on request.

Where can I get more information?

Call 1-780-995-5555 or send a message through the contact page.

Anything else?

The on-site caretaker is the fastest way to get an answer. Call any time between 8 AM and 8 PM.
