Events & links

What's on at Memory Lane.

Open House Sundays in May, the annual Mother's Day and Father's Day fishing weekends, daily birdwatching at the Sandy Beach causeway — plus a few astronomical highlights for the wide-open Alberta sky.

2026 events

Mark the calendar.

A handful of special weekends through the season — and one country-wide invitation that runs the whole summer.

Every Sunday in May

Open House

Every Sunday afternoon in May, drop by to walk the grounds and pick your favourite site for the season. No appointment needed.

May 9 & 10, 2026

Mother's Day Fishing Weekend

Registered female guests may catch and keep two pond-raised rainbow trout from the campground's stocked trout pond. No fishing licence required.

June 20 & 21, 2026

Father's Day Fishing Weekend

Registered male guests may catch and keep two pond-raised rainbow trout from the campground's stocked trout pond. No fishing licence required.

Every summer day

Birdwatching at Sandy Beach

A short drive from Memory Lane, look for wading and water birds along the causeway at Sandy Beach — every summer day, no schedule required.
