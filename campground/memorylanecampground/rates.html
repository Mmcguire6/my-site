Policies & payment

The small print.

The other things to know before you book — what's included, how to pay, the check-in/out times and the campground's couple of small rules.

Check-in & check-out

Check in anytime between 2 PM and 8 PM. Check-out is at 11 AM. The office is open 8 AM – 8 PM daily.

Payment methods

Cash (Canadian funds), MasterCard, Visa, American Express and JCB card.

Cancellations

A one-night charge applies for cancellations made less than 5 days in advance of the reservation.

Long-weekend minimum

A two-night minimum booking applies for long weekends.

GST

Rates do not include GST.

Firewood & propane

Both are sold at the office. The site comes with the fire pit; bring (or buy) the wood and the matches.

Reservations

Call 780-995-5555 to check availability and book — the on-site caretaker will help with the rest.

Campground store

Basic campground supplies, ice and treats are available at the store. Open during office hours.

Accessibility

The campground is fully wheelchair accessible, including the washroom facility. Wheelchair-accessible picnic tables available on request.

Pets welcome

$5 per pet. Pets must be leashed at all times (no exceptions), kept quiet, never left unattended, cleaned up after, and are not permitted in the playground area.

Fireworks prohibited

To prevent fires and so everyone can sleep, fireworks are not permitted on the grounds.

Eco-friendly & tidy

Memory Lane is an eco-friendly campground — please keep campsites clean and sanitary so the next campers find them the same way.

Ready to book?

Call the campground to check availability — the on-site caretaker will help you with the rest.
