A summer playground at Mulhurst Bay

One of central Alberta's most popular summer playgrounds.

Less than an hour's drive south of Edmonton and a half kilometre from the shores of Pigeon Lake — this is Hilah Ayers Wilderness RV Park.

A 45 acre gated campground with both treed and open space, 208 seasonal sites and 34 overnight sites. Family oriented, with park-like settings for the children, full services at every lot, and the kind of summer programming that keeps the same families coming back year after year.

Walk to the lake. Tee off at one of twelve golf courses within a 30-minute drive. Catch a movie under the trees. And when the gates open for the Pigeon Lake Music Festival, the campground turns into central Alberta's summer stage.

On the grounds

What a summer at Hilah Ayers looks like.

A working campground with the amenities to back it up — and a season's worth of programming that turns it into something more than just a place to park the trailer.

Fully-serviced sites

Power, water and sewer at every lot — both seasonal and overnight, treed and open.

Make it your own

Seasonal sites get customised over the years — pergolas, decks, flower beds, named site signs.

Park-like grounds

Gazebos, wildflowers, picnic spots — the simple beauty that makes a long stay feel easy.

Outdoor movie nights

A real wood-pavilion screen on the grounds — pull up a chair when the sun goes down.

Family programming

Bubble balls, bouncy obstacle courses, piñatas, kids' events — the season has a calendar.

Yard games + mini-golf

9-hole mini-golf, beach volleyball, horseshoe pits, ladder golf — the slate of yard games is full.

Pigeon Lake at the doorstep

A half-kilometre walk gets you to the lake and the public boat launch.
