Available lots

Looking for a spot? Here's what's open.

The following lots are available for lease — however, the previous renter of each site is afforded the opportunity to either sell or remove trailers and structural upgrades (sheds, decks, etc.) prior to leaving, so be prepared to negotiate directly with them.

Management approval required

All trailer and upgrade sales are subject to management approval — refrain from exchanging currency or title until an endorsement is obtained.

$1,000 damage deposit

New tenants pay a $1,000 damage deposit, refunded at lease termination when the site is properly maintained, the lease was honoured and no balances remain.

Season runs May 1 – September 30

The resort operates seasonally. Negotiations with previous renters typically wrap before the May 1st start.

Wetaskiwin County overage fees

The County identified certain lots as exceeding permitted building regulations. Modest fees apply to oversized structures — covered decks, Arizona rooms, park model trailers, bunkhouses, cottages, carports, permanent gazebos, oversized sheds. See the overage schedule.
