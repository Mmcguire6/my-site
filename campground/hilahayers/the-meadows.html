The Meadows · Outdoor Event Venue at Hilah Ayers

We provide the setting — you bring the experience.

A self-hosted outdoor wedding and gathering venue at Mulhurst Bay. The wood pavilion, the signature fire pit and the canopy posts are already in place — what goes on inside the day is yours to design.

What makes The Meadows unique

The space, set up for the day.

Rustic outdoor ceremony space

Wooden bench seating in place for the ceremony itself.

Covered stage

For music, speeches, performances — or use as an elevated ceremony platform.

Standalone ceremony altar

An optional altar for the ceremony moment — set it where the day calls for it.

Tensioned canopy

Gathering space under a tensioned canopy — shade through the day, weather cover when needed.

Signature fire pit

An evening centrepiece for everyone to gather around when the speeches end.

Sea-can bar structure

A built-in shipping-container bar setup, ready for your bartender and beverage service.

Bridal preparation room

A powder room for the bridal party to get ready on-site.

Ample guest parking

Generous on-site parking for the full guest list.

Designed as a self-hosted outdoor venue, The Meadows lets you bring in your own vendors, décor, catering, utilities and event services. Optional overnight group camping is available with every package for guests who want to extend their stay.
