An RV'er Welcome

A community gathering point for an RV'ing culture.

Early on, we at the Kinsmen Club of St. Albert, Alberta, recognized RV'ers as a distinct culture. So, to us, nothing is more heart-warming than the spontaneous gatherings, handshakes and pitch-in attitudes marking the arrival of any unit.

You'll appreciate our location: so close to any part of nearby Edmonton & the Capital Region; so close to nature-based recreation. St. Albert is a thoroughly modern city in its own right, proclaiming convenient shopping, services, culture, and sports attractions.

And when you're ready to gather, the Kinsmen Banquet Centre — a short walk east of the RV park — caters every imaginable gathering, from family socials and reunions to mid-sized conventions.

What's on the grounds

A premier park built for full-service stays.

Large pull-through and back-in sites with 50 amp power and full hook-ups, paired with manicured green space, walking trails and a fully-equipped banquet centre right next door.

50 amp power

Every one of our 93 stalls is wired for 50 amp service — comfortable headroom for the biggest rigs.

Full hook-ups

Water, sewer and power at every site — no juggling fresh tanks or hauling waste mid-stay.

Manicured grounds

Wide-open lawns, gravel pads and landscaped edges — clean, level sites with room to set up camp.

Walking & cycling trails

Scenic trails alongside Red Willow Park — bird and nature-viewing minutes from your site.

Kinsmen Banquet Centre

A short walk east of the park — host family socials, reunions, weddings or mid-sized conventions.

St. Albert & the Capital Region — convenience and nature in one trip.

You're settled at the park, but never far from the rest of the city or the country. Here's the lay of the land.

1

Red Willow Park

Scenic cycling and walking trails plus dedicated bird and nature-viewing areas, just outside the gate.

2

Lois Hole Centennial Provincial Park

Big Lake's protected wetland — a major staging area for migratory birds, with viewing platforms and trails.

3

Downtown St. Albert

A modern, walkable city with shopping, services, culture and sports — minutes from the park.

4

West Edmonton Mall

One of North America's largest malls — fifteen minutes south of the park.

5

The Kinsmen Banquet Centre

Right next door — a full banquet hall available for socials, reunions, weddings and conventions.

6

Edmonton International Airport

A straightforward drive south makes the park a comfortable first or last stop on a longer Alberta trip.
