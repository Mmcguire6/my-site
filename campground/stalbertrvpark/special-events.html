On the Kinsmen Grounds

Special events.

The Kinsmen of St. Albert have been hosting community events here since 1965. Below are two of the signature gatherings the grounds support.

First Responders

Edmonton First Responders Rodeo Association

Hosted at the Kinsmen grounds Not-for-profit

The Edmonton First Responders Rodeo Association is a newly formed association hosting an amateur rodeo and recreational events for first responders and their families.

  • Host an annual amateur rodeo/recreational event for first responders and their families that promotes positive community interaction.
  • Operate as a not-for-profit raising funds for The Zebra Child Protection Centre and Legacy Place Society.
  • Promote and preserve the western lifestyle and heritage that rodeo represents.
  • Facilitate inter-agency competition while supporting mental health support for First Responders.

All proceeds benefit the two charitable organizations above.

Annual Festival

The St. Albert Rainmaker

Hosted since 1965 On the Kinsmen grounds

The Rainmaker is a large festival in St. Albert, Alberta that the St. Albert Family of Kin has been hosting since it began in 1965.

  • Rodeo and fairgrounds.
  • Fun and exciting rock and country concerts on Friday and Saturday night.
  • Family entertainment including wobbly balls.
  • Marketplace with food trucks.
  • Saturday parade and pancake breakfast.

Megapass sales, pre-sales for the grounds and concert series announcements are coming soon.

Organized by the Kinsmen Club, Kinette Club, K-40 Club, and Kayette Club of St. Albert.

Volunteers & sponsors → Rainmaker@stalbertkin.ca
Where the proceeds go

Net revenue funds thirty-plus community programs.

The Club's primary fundraising activity is the Rainmaker Rodeo & Exhibition, funded by the resources of the Kinsmen Club of St. Albert. Net revenue generated from the Rainmaker is distributed to provide programs and services from over thirty worthy community, social service, youth and minor sports organizations in and around St. Albert.
