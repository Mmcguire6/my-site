An RV resort on the North Saskatchewan.

Rustic River offers Seasonal / Yearly sites, Long-term leases (20 years) and Overnight Camping (bring your own RV). Don't have an RV? Rent a spot at Rustic River with a Cabin or RV.

On the shores of the beautiful North Saskatchewan River with great jet boating and fishing. Many amenities on site: a store, a liquor store, a swimming pool, a playground, frisbee golf, rentals, jet boat tours, a couple of ponds and lots of events for our members — a yearly golf tournament, music nights, Nerf gun and other summer camps, lots of ATV trails nearby and much more.

Pick your stay

Four ways to stay at Rustic River.

From a single night to a 20-year lease — the resort accommodates the whole range.

1

Overnight camping

A few sites set aside for overnight stays. Bring your own RV — $59.08–$63.08 per night. No tenting.

2

Group camping

Two large group spots — Creek View $498/$578 wkday/wknd; River View $398/$428 wkday/wknd. Hookups + covered kitchens.

3

Seasonals & yearly

Settle in for the season or the year. Average seasonal: around $4,000. See the Seasonals page for available lots.

4

Long-term lease (20 yr)

The longest commitment we offer. Average around $40,000. Email info@rusticriver.ca for the price list.
