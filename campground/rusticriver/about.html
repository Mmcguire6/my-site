About Rustic River

An RV resort with 180+ acres on the river.

A perfect day would be: get in the car and drive out to Rustic River to camp.

The shores of the North Saskatchewan

Where the river bends.

An RV Resort on the shores of the beautiful North Saskatchewan River and with over 180 acres of secluded parkland. We offer Seasonal and long term leases. A few sites for overnight camping and 2 large group camping spots.

Make memories in all 4 seasons at Rustic River Resort. Rustic River offers Seasonal/Yearly sites, Long term leases (20 years) and Overnight Camping (bring your own RV). Don't have an RV? Rent a spot at Rustic River with a Cabin or RV.

Features include a store on site, liquor store, swimming pool, playground, frisbee golf, rentals, jet boat tours, a couple of ponds and lots of events.

"Money can't buy happiness, but it can buy a camper, which is kind of the same thing."
In four seasons

Make memories year-round.

Quieter grounds, river fog, and the parkland in a different mood.

Spring

The thaw, the runoff, the first overnight stays of the season.
