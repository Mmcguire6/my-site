Site changes

We aim to honor the site you book. Changes may occur if:

  • The site requires maintenance or upgrades, or weather conditions (e.g. strong winds, heavy rain, downed trees) make it unusable.
  • The site is leased long-term or seasonally, and the lease overlaps your reservation.

If you are camping with a group, we will make every effort to keep the group together. Please notify us if your group's placement needs adjustment.

We will attempt to contact you before making changes. If we cannot reach you, the change will be made and you will be notified of your new site.

Cancellations & refunds

1. Standard reservations

  • Cancel up to 30 days before check-in for a refund minus a $25 administration fee.
  • Cancellations within 30 days of check-in are non-refundable. You may transfer your reservation to another date (excluding weekends and long weekends) for a $30 transfer fee if done at least 24 hours before check-in.
  • Rental trailers and group camping are non-refundable once booked.

2. Reservation transfers

  • Within the 30-day period, Management may allow rebooking to another date based on availability (weekends excluded).
  • Rebooked dates remain subject to the original 30-day notice for cancellation.
  • Transfers must be used within the same calendar year.

3. Holding a reservation

  • If you do not have a new date within the 30-day period, you may request to hold your reservation until December and select a specific date later (in the same calendar year of your booking).

Health emergencies & government mandates

  • If the resort is closed to overnight camping by government mandate, overnight bookings will be refunded.
  • If the resort is open but some services are restricted, overnight camping fees are non-refundable; fees for restricted services will be refunded.
  • If a guest chooses not to come due to health concerns (e.g. COVID) or is required to isolate, the reservation may be transferred to another date per the rules above.

Non-refundable situations

This policy does not provide refunds for:

  • Group camping, rental trailers, or seasonal bookings
  • Illness, injury, or unexpected disease
  • Government obligations (jury duty, court, military)
  • Travel advisories not resulting in prohibitions
  • Acts of God (floods, wildfires, storms, snow / ice, etc.)
  • Transportation disruptions (flight, train, bus, ferry cancellations)
  • Event cancellations or rescheduling for which the reservation was made

Definitions

Act of God — Events outside human control, including natural disasters or severe weather, for which no one is legally responsible.
