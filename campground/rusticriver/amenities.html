No. 04 · Amenities

Everything you need on the property, all summer long.

A pool with a slide, two ponds, jet boats on the river, paintball, frisbee golf, a canopy bike — and an event almost every weekend.

scroll
A resort, not a campground

You don’t leave Rustic once you’re set up. The pool runs all summer, the slide opens in May, the jet boats run from the river, the paintball field opens to the public via the second highway access. The store handles the things you forgot. Make a long weekend of it.

On the water

The pool, the slide, the pond.

01 · Swimming pool

A real outdoor pool, with a slide.

Heated pool, big red-white-and-blue waterslide, decked surrounds. The pool opens around mid-May (weather permitting) and closes in September.

Pool users must sign a waiver, wear a wristband and pay the fee. Anyone 1.3 m and under must wear a life jacket (no metal buckles, no zippers).

Get the waiver →
02 · Two ponds

For canoe, SUP, and a fountain.

A pair of ponds on the property — one set up for canoes and paddleboards, with a fountain in the middle and a beach for the little ones. The other is quieter, more cast-and-evening.

Rentals on site: paddleboards, life jackets, canopy bike. See the rentals page.

03 · Waterpark

The whole water-side, in one spot.

Pool, slide and pond all sit together at the centre of the resort. Walk-able from any site. Contact the office for pricing and current operating hours.

04 · Jet boat tours

Run the North Saskatchewan.

Jet boat tours operate from the resort's river beach. Yearly Jet Boat Rally on the calendar. The river is wide here, with rapids and quiet stretches both.

See the calendar →
05 · Paintball field

Field opens in June.

Games at 11 AM and 2 PM Saturday and Sunday through the summer, plus select weekdays. This year the field is open to non-RRR members via the 2nd highway access. Pre-book for Canada Day weekend.

06 · Canopy bike + frisbee golf

Pedal the property, throw a disc.

The four-person canopy bike is a yes-the-roof-is-striped, yes-it-takes-the-whole-family piece of comedy and transport. Frisbee golf is a full disc-golf layout on the grounds — bring your discs or borrow at the store.
