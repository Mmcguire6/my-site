2026 Calendar

A year at Rustic River.

Poker rallies, paintball, family game days, Christmas in July, Halloween in August, jet boat rallies, indoor VR golf, skating rink, ski-doo trips — there's something happening almost every weekend.

By the month

What's on, when.

Verbatim from the 2026 Rustic River Resort calendar. Some events recur weekly through the season — paintball every weekend, 50-50 draws Saturday 6 PM at the bar, and movie afternoons in the Kids Game Center.

April

  • Sat Apr 4
    Poker Ralley + Easter Egg Hunt — Poker Ralley begins 10 AM, register at office or online. Open to skidoos, quads, SXS's. Begins at the Paint Ball Shack, finishes at the bar for final cards. Fire and hot dogs at midway point. Easter Egg Hunt for kids 11 AM at the bar.
  • Recurring
    Bingo 5 PM inside the bar (or outside weather permitting). 50-50 draws every weekend. Paintball every weekend once started.

May

  • Fri May 14
    Pool and slide opening — weather permitting.
  • Sat May 15
    2nd Poker Rally — 10 AM start, same format as April.
  • Sat May 16
    Bingo Night — 5 PM.
  • Sat May 23
    Garbage Clean-up Event for kids & teens — 11 AM–3 PM, prizes and candy, hot dogs at 4 PM at the bar / store. Plus Yardzee.
  • Sat May 30
    Texas Hold'em Men/Women (registration 3 PM, check-in 4 PM, seating 4:30 PM, games 5 PM). Golf League play. The new RRR indoor VR golf league starts ($10/member/game; see Brian for annual memberships); league play 11 AM through the day.

June

  • Sat Jun 6
    First Family Trivia (register 1 hr before at store). Paintball 1st play — field opens for summer; games at 11 AM and 2 PM Sat/Sun. Open to non-RRR members this year via the 2nd highway access. Movie Afternoon.
  • Sat Jun 13
    Golf league play. Team Jenga 1 PM at the bar (all ages, register by 11 AM). Paintball + 50-50 + Movie Afternoon.
  • Sat Jun 20
    Paintball + 50-50 + Movie Afternoon + Bingo.
  • Sat Jun 27
    Games Night (adults, at the bar, 5–10 PM). Golf league play. Paintball + 50-50 + Movie Afternoon.

July Canada Day weekend

  • Sat Jul 4
    Canada Day at Rustic River. SXS / quad / golf cart decoration contest. Parade 1 PM – 2:30 PM. Chili Cook-Off at the bar 11 AM – 1 PM, judging 5 PM. Fireworks at dark. Smoked Meat Cook-Off. No paintball unless pre-booked. 50-50 draw 7 PM at the bar.
  • Sun Jul 5
    Level 1 hike — hiking club leaves RRR 8 AM.
  • Sat Jul 11
    Golf league play 11 AM. Family Games Day (Monopoly + Risk) 11 AM – 8 PM. Gnome Making — register at store by Jul 11 (30 spaces only).
  • Sat Jul 18
    Christmas at RRR. Cookie making 1–4 PM. Lot decoration competition. Secret Santa for all registered kids — judging 4–6 PM, draw 6:30 PM at the bar.
  • Sun Jul 19
    Level 2 Hike.
  • Sat Jul 25
    Cornhole Tournament (register by Jul 22, 8 PM) — 10 AM – 6 PM. Bingo 5 PM. Golf league.

August

  • Sat Aug 1
    Pig Roast Potluck — members bring side dishes (see Suzane). Band Day — band plays 3–5 PM and 7–9 PM; bar closes 10 PM.
  • Sun Aug 2
    Paintball. Kids Duck Hunt. Kids Movie Day.
  • Wed Aug 5
    Adult Sip & Paint with Katarina — limited spots, register by Aug 4.
  • Sat Aug 8
    Golf league. Farmers Market. Lot / Yard Sale. Gnome Making. Paintball.
  • Sat Aug 15
    RRR Amazing Race (team / family, 1–6 PM, register at store by Aug 14). Bingo 7 PM. Level 3 Hiking.
  • Sat Aug 22
    RRR Annual Halloween — best costume prize, best lot decoration prize. Golf League. Paintball.
  • Wed Aug 26
    Adult Sip & Paint with Katarina.
  • Sat Aug 29
    Cornhole Tourney Round 2. Mexican Taco Day at store / bar all day. Members wrap-up bar night. Level 3 Hike. Paintball.

September

  • Fri Sep 4 & Sat Sep 5
    Golf Summer League Championships — RRR's 1st annual VR golf championships.
  • Sat Sep 5
    Jet Boating experience for kids and select adults.
  • Sun Sep 6 or 13
    Pool closes — weather & attendance dependent.
  • Sat Sep 19
    Winter golf league start-up — must play 10 games over winter to qualify for spring club championships. Skeet Shoot at the river, 1–5 PM, 12 and 14 gauge.
  • Sat Sep 26
    Summer Poker Rally Wrap-Up — last before winter conditions.

October

  • Sat Oct 3, 17, 31
    Winter golf league continues.
  • Sat Oct 31
    Poker Rally — first winter ride (SXS, quads, or skidoos).

November

  • Sat Nov 14 & 28
    Winter golf league continues.

December Winter season

  • Sat Dec 5
    Skating rink opening — weather dependent.
  • Sat Dec 12 & 26
    Winter golf league.
  • Sat Dec 19
    Winter ski-doo / quad trip.
  • Dec 20 – Jan 2
    Series of casual rides for members and staff — join in for casual rides and warm fires.
  • Thu Dec 31
    New Year's at Rustic Winter Party.
