Rules & Regulations.

Welcome to Rustic River Resort. Please help us maintain a safe, clean and enjoyable experience for all campers. By staying with us, you agree to follow the rules below.

Download the full PDF

Campsite & vehicles

  • Each site accommodates 2 adults + children, max 6 people. Extra guests: $5/day. Update us at check-in if more than 6.
  • Campers under 18 must be with a parent or guardian.
  • One RV / camping unit per site. No tenting sites, except for minor children (must be registered at check-in).
  • Vehicles must remain within your site; extra vehicles park in designated overflow areas.

Check-in & check-out

  • Check-in: 3 PM  ·  Check-out: 11 AM.
  • Late check-out: daily camping fee per hour past 11 AM.
  • Registration and payment must be complete before occupying your site.
  • Site numbers must be followed; occupying the wrong site may result in relocation or eviction without refund.

Access & quiet time

  • Gates are closed; guests must be let in / out by you.
  • Quiet hours: 9 PM. By 11 PM, no music or loud noises should be audible.
  • Light pollution: turn off exterior lights at night.

Garbage, recycling & firepits

  • Dispose of garbage and recyclables at the Sani dump station blue bins.
  • Take out garbage daily to avoid wildlife issues (skunks, bears).
  • Leaving firepits unattended or garbage behind may result in fines.

Grey water & RV sanitation

  • RVs must have self-contained sewage systems and sealed connections.
  • Dumping grey / black water improperly will result in clean-up charges.

Fires & firewood

  • Only RRR store-purchased firewood or propane / electric firepits allowed. Outside firewood is not allowed.
  • Follow provincial fire regulations — albertafirebans.ca.
  • Fire only in designated fire rings; each site has a firepit and picnic table.

Pets

  • Dogs and cats must be leashed and under control.
  • Pick up after pets; do not use washroom garbage.
  • Service dogs are allowed in all resort areas.
  • Management may request removal of unruly pets.

Pool & recreational facilities

  • Pool users must sign a waiver, wear a wristband and pay the fee.
  • Anyone 1.3 m and under must wear a life jacket (no metal buckles, no zippers).
  • Use of resort facilities is at your own risk.
  • Boats and watercraft only in designated launch areas. Quiet hours apply.
  • ATV / UTV use: only with flagged leaseholder approval — you must know a member at Rustic who will lend their flag to you after doing an orientation.
  • No firearms, fireworks or weapons.

Behavior & safety

  • Harassment, foul language, vandalism, theft, fighting or impaired driving will not be tolerated.
  • Excessive noise, disputes or unsafe behavior may result in eviction and / or RCMP involvement.
  • Drive max 10 km/h. No motorized vehicles before 8 AM or after 9 PM (children's motorized allowed 11 AM – 7 PM).

Wi-Fi & connectivity

  • "Rustic Guest" network — daily fee. Tokens available in the store for a reliable connection.
  • Satellite dishes only at your site.

Liability

  • RRR is not responsible for personal injury or damage due to weather, natural events or other campers.
  • Acts of God (storms, floods, fires) are outside of human control.
  • Failure to follow rules may result in fines, clean-up charges or eviction.

Rules may change periodically. The latest version is always on the source site — rusticriver.ca — and posted at the office.
