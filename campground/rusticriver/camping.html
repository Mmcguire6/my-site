A few overnight sites, two group spots.

A long weekend with the crew, a Tuesday night with the rig, or 12 trailers for a wedding — Rustic River accommodates all three.

scroll
Overnight at Rustic

The overnight inventory is small — only a handful of sites set aside for the night-or-two crowd. Bring your own RV, register at the office, set up on a treed gravel pad, and the rest of the resort is yours. No tenting — one RV per site, no exceptions.

Sites move quickly on event weekends, particularly Canada Day and the August long weekend. The reservation system opens February 1st for the new season; the booking link is at the top of every page.

Two large group spots

For when you bring the crew.

01 · Creek View

Big covered kitchen, 9 hookups.

Pricing includes 6 RV hookups (power 30 amp + water) plus a large covered kitchen with industrial appliances and a covered firepit. Up to 12 trailers, with hookups for 9 of them; extras at $50/night.

Weekday$498/ night
Weekend$578/ night

Group camping is non-refundable once booked.

02 · River View

Smaller covered kitchen, river-side.

Pricing is for 6 RVs with hookups. A smaller covered kitchen with fridge, stove and barbecue — pots, pans and utensils not included. Up to 10 trailers; extras (no hookups) at $50/night.

Weekday$398/ night
Weekend$428/ night

Group camping is non-refundable once booked.

A note on firewood

Outside firewood is not allowed. Buy at the on-site store, or use a propane or electric firepit. The rule is in service of the trees, the soil, and the bugs that travel in firewood from somewhere else.
