About Outwest

The last private land before Banff.

Outwest Camping & RV Park Ltd. is a family owned and operated campground in the Prairie Creek Valley, between the Ram and Clearwater rivers — 300 metres off the pavement at 3,900 feet of foothills elevation.

It's open year round: a real escape in the warm months, and a quiet winter base in the off-season. High season runs May through September; low season October through April.

Ways to stay

RV, tent, or cabin.

However you like to get out here — there's a spot for it.

01
Cabins

Fully equipped cabins.

Prefer four walls? Outwest has fully-equipped cabins for a comfortable basecamp with the wilderness right outside.

02
Tent sites

Tent sites in the trees.

Tent sites come with 15-amp power — keep it simple, with just enough to stay charged under the pines.

03
RV sites

RV sites with power & water.

RV sites are available with 30 or 50 amp power and water — room for the rig, with the views to match.

All guests review and sign the campground rules and regulations at check-in; failure to follow them may result in immediate removal. Cancellations: long weekends require a minimum fee equal to three nights unless cancelled within seven days of booking; other reservations need three days' notice, and cancellations within three days of arrival incur a fee equal to a three-night stay.
