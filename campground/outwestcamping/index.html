The last privately owned land before Banff

Easy access. Wild country.

Overlooking Prairie Creek Valley at 3,900 feet, Outwest Camping & RV Park sits between the Ram and Clearwater rivers. Just 15 minutes to great fishing lakes and 30 minutes to Rocky Mountain House.

Easy access — only 300 meters off pavement — but far enough out to feel like your own piece of the wild.

Open year round: High Season May through September, Low Season October through April. RV sites, tent sites and fully-equipped cabins.

Three ways to stay

RV, tent or cabin.

Outwest takes all sizes of rigs and all kinds of campers — pick the one that fits the trip.

RV Sites — 30 or 50 amp

Choose from 30 amp or 50 amp sites — all include power and water. $45/night (30A) or $50/night (50A).

Tent Sites — 15 amp

15 amp power only — for the tents, the small trailers and the simpler set-ups. $30/night.

Fully-equipped cabins

Nestled in the far end of the campground are the fully equipped cabins. Phone the office for nightly rates and availability.

Fully equipped cabins

Heat, kitchen, bath. Outside your door.

Nestled in the far end of the campground are the fully equipped cabins — log walls, vaulted wood ceilings, a real kitchen, a real bathroom, real heat. For when the tent isn't the answer.

Open year-round, including the cold months when nothing else around here is.
