Amenities & Maps

Made for adventurers.

Coin-operated quad wash, a camp store for what you forgot, three shower houses across the grounds — and the luxury of idling your quad straight from your site to the trails.

Just a ride away

Idle from your site, straight to the trails.

Outwest sits at the end of the foothills with quad-friendly access throughout. ATVs are permitted at idle speed only from your site to the entrance — and from there, the trail system is yours.

This isn't a quiet-retreat campground. It's a base camp for the people who came for the riding, the rivers and the back country.

On the grounds

The three things everyone uses.

Verbatim from the Outwest amenities page — quad wash, camp store, shower houses.

Quad Wash

Coin-operated, dual-sided quad wash bays for added convenience.

Camp Store

Whether you're in the mood for a quick snack or need to pick up a few essentials you forgot to pack, the store offers a wide selection to choose from.

Shower House

Three conveniently located, coin-operated facilities are available for your use — a more spacious shower when the weekend needs one.

Three site types

RV, tent or cabin.

Outwest accommodates RVs of all sizes, tents and fully-equipped cabin stays. Pick the one that fits the trip.

30 or 50 amp

RV Sites

Choose from 30 amp or 50 amp sites, all include power and water. Treed gravel pads for all sizes of rigs.

15 amp

Tent Sites

15 amp power only — for the tents, the small trailers and the simpler set-ups.

Fully equipped

Cabins

Nestled in the far end of the campground are the fully equipped cabins. Phone the office for nightly rates and availability.
