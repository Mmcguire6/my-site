Cancellation policy

If plans change.

Two windows — long weekends and everything else.

Long weekends

A minimum cancellation fee equivalent to a 3-night stay applies unless the reservation is cancelled within 7 days.

All other reservations

At least 3 days notice is required for cancellations. Reservations cancelled within 3 days of the booking start date will incur a fee equivalent to a 3-night stay.

Rules & Regulations

What every guest agrees to.

All guests must review and sign the campground rules and regulations at check-in to help maintain a safe and enjoyable environment for everyone. Failure to follow these rules may result in immediate removal from the campground.

  • 01

    ATVs may be driven at idle speed only from your site to the entrance. No riding around the campground.

  • 02

    We enforce zero tolerance for excessive speed or noise.

  • 03

    Keep all vehicles, trailers, tents, and equipment on graveled roads and pads only — not in treed areas between sites.

  • 04

    Vehicle and quad washing is permitted at the designated wash station only.

  • 05

    Pets must be leashed, quiet, and tied to your trailer at all times. Keep pets on gravel areas, not in the trees. Owners must clean up after them. Pets causing noise, disturbance, or posing a threat will be required to leave.

  • 06

    No cutting or trimming of trees.

  • 07

    Do not move or alter fire pits.

  • 08

    Management is not responsible for accidents, injuries, or loss due to any cause, including natural events.

  • 09

    Management reserves the right to refuse entry or remove anyone for inappropriate behavior.

  • 10

    All parking is at the owner's risk.

  • 11

    Please dispose of cigarette butts in fire pits — help keep the campground clean.

  • 12

    No ATV use within the campground after dark.

  • 13

    Fireworks are not permitted in campsites — use the designated parking lot area only.

  • 14

    Barbecues are not allowed on picnic tables.
