About Wood Pines

A family-owned resort on 160 acres of pines.

Wood Pines is a family-owned and operated, gated RV resort set on 160 acres of mature pine trees near Pigeon Lake. We built it as a solution to affordable vacationing right here close to home — your own little piece of heaven, with no more storage fees or driving around looking for a spot.

Large, fully serviced lots are available for seasonal stays and 35-year leases. The community is family-oriented and pet-friendly, with everything you need on site and the lake village just twenty minutes away.
