Rules

The house rules.

A quick read before you arrive — the campground's rules are designed to make everyone's stay easy and quiet. Hosted spaces stay great when everyone pulls their weight.

Quick reference

What to know.

The shortlist: arrival and departure, quiet hours, pets, fires, the pool, and a couple of small but firm rules.

Check-in & check-out

Check-in 2 PM, check-out 11 AM. Full payment required upon booking. Minimum two nights weekends, three on long weekends.

Quiet hours

11 PM – 8 AM. Keep music at minimum levels. Generators are not permitted on the campground at any time.

Pets

Pets must be leashed and with you at all times. Owners must clean up after their pets — $25 cleanup fee otherwise. Minimize barking.

Fires & firewood

Firewood from the campground: $15 per tote (10 AM – 8 PM), $20 after 8 PM. Never leave a fire unattended ($25 fee). No garbage in the firepit.

Pool

Pool hours 10 AM – 8 PM. No lifeguard on duty, no diving. Remove shoes before entering the pool house. Infants/toddlers not potty trained must wear swim diapers.

Speed limit

10 km/hour throughout the campground, ONE WAY ONLY. Respect posted limits on public roads outside the gate too.

Visitors

All visitors must register at the office. $5 per guest, regardless of age. Guests must be off site by 11 PM. Maximum four adults per site.

Prohibited

No fireworks at any time. No water balloons on site ($25+ cleanup fee if violated). No generators.

Garbage & cleanup

Use designated bins. The site must be left the way you arrived — a $25 cleanup fee applies to substantial debris left behind.

Cancellations

Individual sites: by 2:00 PM, 10 days prior. Group sites: 30 days notice by 2:00 PM. $10 anytime cancellation fee per site, plus a non-refundable $5 booking fee.

No refunds

No refunds for weather or non-utilization. Management not responsible for loss or damage on the grounds.

Questions?

Anything not covered? Text 780-668-6523 or email — Bernie or Krystina will get back to you.
