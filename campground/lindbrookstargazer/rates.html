Stay a month, save a week.

Monthly stays get every 8th night complimentary — maximum one-month stays. The longer you stay, the more you save.

Prices do not include GST and are subject to change without notice. Two-night minimum on weekends, three-night minimum on long weekends. Extra units $43/night (high season) or $33/night (off-season). Firewood $15/tote (before 8 PM) or $20/tote after 8 PM. Sorry, no refunds.

Groups, day use & non-camper fees

The other numbers.

Group sites, day-use pricing, sani-dump and water fill-up for non-campers passing through.

Group A — 6 sites

Six-site minimum at $50 per site per night. All sites in the group must be booked together. 30-day cancellation policy.

Group B — 4 sites

Four-site minimum at $50 per site per night. All four must be booked together. 30-day cancellation policy applies.

Day use / swimming

$5 per person or $20 per family — pool access during posted swimming hours.

Sani dump (non-camper)

$20 for non-campers passing through who need to use the on-site sani dump.

Water fill-up (non-camper)

$15 for non-campers who need to fill their water tank.

Cancellation fee

$10 per site, anytime — plus a non-refundable $5 booking fee. Refund only if site is rebooked.
