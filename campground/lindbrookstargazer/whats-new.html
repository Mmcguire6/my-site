What's on at Star Gazer.

The notes the hosts want you to know before booking — quick read.

10-day cancellation policy

Implemented for individual sites. We recommend re-checking availability — guests often cancel within the window, so spots open up. Follow on Facebook for last-minute openings.

Group sites

Online booking only. Two group areas: Group A (6 sites), Group B (4 sites). All sites in the group must be booked and paid for upfront. 30-day cancellation policy.

Public swimming

Hours vary by week and are posted on Facebook. Day-use cost: $5 per person (whether swimming or not).

Seasonal spots

Currently unavailable. Interested parties can join a waitlist by emailing Lindbrookstargazercampground@gmail.com.
