Pick the site that suits you.

Sites at Lindbrook are well-treed and private — pick a layout that fits your rig. Individual sites are bookable through Campspot; group sites by inquiry. Site requests are accepted but specific site numbers are not guaranteed.

Sites at Star Gazer

What's here.

Individually numbered sites plus two private group areas. View the campground photos by site for a feel of each spot before you book.

Individual sites

Sites numbered through the campground — including 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 14, 16, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 26, 28, 29 — varying in size and tree cover.

Star Sites

Special "Star Sites" — premium spots on the grounds, perfect for the stargazing the campground is named for.

Group A — 6 sites

A private group area of six sites, ideal for family reunions or larger get-togethers. All six sites must be booked together.

Group B — 4 sites

A smaller group area of four sites — friends-camping size. All four sites must be booked together.

Well-treed & private

Mature trees throughout the grounds — sites have good shade and privacy from neighbours.

Site requests

Requests for specific site numbers are accepted but not guaranteed. The campground does its best to honour preferences.

The grounds at a glanceSites laid out among the trees, with the pool and playground at the centre and the pond on the grounds.
