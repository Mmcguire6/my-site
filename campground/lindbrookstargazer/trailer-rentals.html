What's included

You bring the food.

Each rental comes ready to camp — outdoor setup, indoor essentials, even the hot-dog sticks. Just bring groceries and your clothes.

Outdoor setup

Chairs, BBQ, stove, rug, propane, hot dog sticks, table and garbage holder — your site is set up the moment you arrive.

Indoor essentials

Cookware, dishes, utensils, cleaning supplies, linens and storage containers — fully stocked kitchen ready to use.

Check-in & check-out

Check-in 3 PM. Check-out 11 AM. Renters must be 25 years old or over.

Deposits

$100 secures the rental (refundable up to 30 days prior, less $10 admin). At check-in: full trailer charge plus $150 damage deposit ($250 if no booking deposit).

Restrictions

No smoking indoors. No pets except dogs (written permission required). Maximum 6 people per trailer. Upper bunks weighted for children. Trailers cannot be moved.
