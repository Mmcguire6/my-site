About the campground

A boutique campground, family-run.

Lindbrook Star Gazer is a semi full-service boutique campground near Tofield, Alberta — well-treed, private sites with a picnic table and firepit at each, run personally by Bernie and Krystina.

It's the kind of small, cared-for place where families come back year after year. As they put it: where memories are created.

Reservations

Booking your stay.

Reservations are made online — here's how booking and cancellations work.

01
How to book

Reserve online, anytime.

Bookings are made through the campground's online reservation system. There's a 2-night minimum on regular weekends and a 3-night minimum on long weekends.

A $10.00 cancellation fee applies per site, under a 10-day cancellation policy with a conditional refund if the site is refilled. Group sites require 30 days' notice. Please note: sorry, no refunds.

Seasonal campers are not being accepted at this time — a waitlist is available by email.

The setting

Wide skies, close to home.

Thirty minutes east of Edmonton, near Tofield — far enough for big country quiet, close enough for an easy weekend.

01
Star Gazer

Named for the night sky.

Out past the city lights near Tofield, the prairie sky opens up — the wide, dark skies the campground takes its name from.

Spend the day at the pool or exploring the area, then end it around the firepit under the stars. That's the idea.

02
The little things

Touches that stay with you.

The fairy garden, the pond and fountain, the well-treed private sites — small, cared-for details are what bring families back.

Trailer rentals are available, and there's plenty in the surrounding area to fill a weekend.
