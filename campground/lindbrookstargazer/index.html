About the campground

A boutique campground under the stars.

Lindbrook Star Gazer Campground & RV Park is a semi full-service boutique campground just thirty minutes east of Edmonton — powered sites, city water, well-treed private lots, a heated pool, playground, on-site security, sani dump, picnic tables and fire pits at every site.

It's the kind of country campground that still feels close to home — quiet enough to hear the birds in the morning, dark enough at night to see the stars the place is named for. Hosted by Bernie & Krystina.

A day at Star Gazer

From sunrise to starlight.

A day at Lindbrook moves slow — coffee in the morning, the pool in the afternoon, and a sky that earns the campground's name.

1

Country mornings

Coffee on your own site, birdsong from the treeline, and the rest of the campground slow to wake.

2

Heated-pool afternoons

The kids in the pool, the parents in chairs, and a stop at the playground when the cannonballs are done.

3

Stargazer evenings

Fire pit lit, marshmallows out, and the show begins overhead — the namesake of the place.
