About Bluebird

An RV enthusiast's paradise.

Bluebird RV Park is a 50+ community set on 15 private acres in Camrose County — 35 large, fully-serviced gravel sites with dedicated water and 30/15 or 50/15 amp metered power.

Sites are separated by ornamental shrubs and trees or privacy screens, and guests can add decks, sheds and gazebos. It's a seasonal community — no nightly or weekend campers, just neighbours who settle in for the season.

Sites & rates

Settle in for the season.

Seasonal, monthly and weekly rates for 30 and 50 amp sites. Regular season runs May 1 – October 1.

Site / termRate
Seasonal · 30 amp (May 1–Oct 1)$3,000 – $3,300
Seasonal · 50 amp (May 1–Oct 1)$3,050 – $3,500
Extended season (Apr 15–Oct 15)+ $800
Monthly$850 / $900 / $950 + power
Weekly · 30 amp / 50 amp$350 / $375

Extended season can also be split: $450 (Apr 15–30) and $350 (Oct 1–15). Deposits: $400 new guests, $300 existing, plus a $350 power deposit the first season. Winter and summer RV storage $50/month. One RV and two registered-guest vehicles per site; sites are non-transferable. Visitors are welcome while you're on site.

Good to know

How Bluebird works.

A few things that make this a community rather than a campground.

1

Seasonal only

No nightly or weekend campers — other than occasional friends of existing guests.

2

A 50+ community

Bluebird is an adult 50+ community. There is no playground on site.

3

One RV per site

One RV and up to two registered-guest vehicles per site. Sites are non-transferable.

4

Visitors

Visitors are permitted while you're on site.

5

Tours by arrangement

Mike or Dawn are happy to give a personal tour by prior arrangement.

6

Dark skies nearby

Close to the Beaver Hills Biosphere and the Beaver Hills Dark Sky area.
