About Bluebird

An RV enthusiast's paradise.

Bluebird is built around quiet, long stays — not a weekend campground. Thirty-five large fully-serviced sites with dedicated water and 30/15 or 50/15 amp metered power, separated by ornamental shrubs and privacy screens, with permission to add a deck, shed or gazebo.

Seasonal, monthly and weekly rates available — including an extended six-month Snowbird Season for guests who want a full Alberta summer at their lot. No nightly or weekend campers; just neighbours who come back year after year.

Run by Mike & DawnFamily owners, on site through the season.
An evening at Bluebird

The kind of day you circle on the calendar.

Slow mornings, easy afternoons, and big skies after sundown — there's a reason guests come back year after year.

Morning

Coffee on the deck

Fresh coffee on your own deck, the prairie waking up around you, and a wave to a neighbour walking past with the dog.

Afternoon

Walk the trails, watch the birds

Short walking trails through the grounds, the water-fowl viewing benches by the pond — quiet places to read a chapter or just sit.

Evening

Fire pit, friends, fireflies

Light the fire pit, pour a cold one, drift over to the Birdsnest for Sociable Saturday — then look up at one of the darkest skies in the area.
