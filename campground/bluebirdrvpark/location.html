Driving directions

Two ways to get here.

From Edmonton or from Camrose — both routes meet at Township Road 500, past the Miquelon Hills Golf Course sign.

From Edmonton.

Take Highway 14 southeast to Secondary Highway 833 South. Proceed approximately 10 km to Township Road 500 (past the Miquelon Hills Golf Course sign). Turn right and continue west approximately ¼ mile to the fourth driveway on the left, with an automatic gate.

From Camrose.

Head north on Secondary Highway 833 to Highway 617. Turn right (east), then return to Highway 833 heading north to Township Road 500 (past the golf course marker). Turn left (west) for ¼ mile to the fourth left driveway, with an automatic gate and banner.

Heads up: Bluebird is a power-gated, private 50+ community. Please call or text ahead so we can let you through the gate, or share the access code if you've already booked a tour.

Plan a tour.

Send a request or call ahead and we'll have the gate open and the kettle on.
