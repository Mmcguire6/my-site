Amenities

What's on the grounds.

Bluebird is a private, gated 50+ community on 15 well-maintained acres — quiet, friendly, and built around long stays. Here's what you'll find when you settle in.

On site

The essentials, done right.

Fully serviced sites, a private clubhouse, and the simple things that make a season comfortable.

35 fully serviced sites

35 large sites, including 5 pull-throughs — dedicated water and 30/15 or 50/15 amp metered power at each.

Power gate

A private, gated 50+ facility — power gate with access cards or codes.

Weekly propane

Weekly propane delivery through Ross Agri Camrose — straight to your site.

Fire pits on request

Fire pits available on request — bring your own wood.

Common BBQ area

A shared BBQ area for friendly cookouts — or to host the neighbours.

Short walking trails

Short walking trails through the property — easy strolls without leaving home.

Water-fowl viewing

A water-fowl viewing area with benches — quiet seats for quiet moments.

Decks & sheds permitted

Decks, sheds and gazebos permitted with approval — make the site your own.

Pets welcome

Pets welcome — with an off-leash area (not fenced).

Sociable Saturdays

Sociable Saturdays through the season — community, when you want it.

Raspberry patch

An on-site raspberry patch — little surprises in the everyday.

Owners on site

Mike & Dawn are on site through the season — you know who to ask.
