About the studio

A studio built for small business.

I’m Matthew, founder of the studio. We build modern websites for small businesses across Canada and the US, trades and contractors, clinics, shops, studios, consultants, and professional services, out of Edmonton, Alberta.

You work directly with the studio from first preview to launch. No project managers, no rotating cast of junior designers, no offshore handoffs. We own the whole process: strategy, brand, design, code, deploy.

What we care about is the small details that make a website feel designed-not-templated. Typography that respects the work it’s framing. Custom palettes built around your business’s actual identity. Honest copy. Sites that load quickly on a laptop at the front desk and make sense on a customer’s phone on the bus home.

We work in focused stretches with each business we build for, so the work gets our full attention from preview to launch. If your business needs a website that signals what your work actually is, let’s talk.

The web should look like the businesses behind it, not the other way around.”

Common questions

Straight answers.

How much does a small business website cost?

A small business website costs $0 down and $75 per month, all-inclusive — design, development, hosting, SSL, security, edits, and support in one subscription, with no upfront build fee. It runs on a 12-month minimum, then month-to-month.

What’s included in the website subscription?

The $75/month subscription includes everything: a custom, hand-built website, hosting, SSL, unlimited content edits, security, and ongoing support — no separate hosting bill and no hourly fees.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most small business websites go live in about three weeks. You see a working preview of your real site first, and you only start paying once it’s live.
