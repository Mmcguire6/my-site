What do I actually get for $297?

A custom-built website plus the system behind it — missed-call text-back, lead and estimate follow-up, booking, review requests, and reactivation campaigns — hosted, set up, and supported. No setup fee.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel anytime, and you keep your phone number.

Does it work with my current phone and website?

Yes. It runs on the number you already have. We build you a new website as part of the $297 — or we wire the automations onto the site you’ve got.

How long does setup take?

7–10 days from your intake to launch. We build the website and set up your follow-up, booking, and review requests, then walk you through it on a short launch call.

When will I see results?

Day one, every missed call gets a text back within seconds and every lead gets followed up. How many turn into customers depends on your volume and your market — and we’ll be honest about that instead of promising the moon.
