About me

A one-person studio. By design.

I’m Matthew. I run a one-person web studio out of Edmonton, Alberta, building modern websites for small-to-mid professional services firms across Canada — lawyers, consultants, CPAs, dental and medical clinics.

I work directly with the firms I build for. There’s no project manager between you and the person making the design decisions, no rotating cast of junior designers, no offshore handoffs. Just me, doing the whole thing — strategy, brand, design, code, deploy.

What I care about is the small details that make a website feel designed-not-templated. Typography that respects the work it’s framing. Custom palettes built around your firm’s actual identity. Honest copy. Sites that load quickly on a partner’s MacBook in chambers and make sense on a client’s phone on the bus home.

I work in focused stretches with each firm I build for, so the work gets my full attention from preview to launch. If your firm needs a website that signals what your work actually is, let’s talk.

The web should look like the firms behind it — not the other way around.”

How I work

See it first. Then decide.

Most engagements start with a working preview I’ve already built — your firm’s actual site, refined and ready to look at. No discovery calls, no pitch decks, no commitment until you’ve seen what’s possible.

01

Preview & quote

You see a working preview of your firm’s site — real brand, real content polished from your existing copy, real working build. If it’s a fit, I send a fixed scope and a service agreement — sign and return, no money down. No pressure, no presentations, no calls.

  • Working preview built up front
  • Fixed scope & agreement
  • $0 down to sign
02

Build & refine

From the preview, I refine into the final site — tightening copy, expanding pages, dialing in typography and palette around your firm’s identity. Two rounds of revisions baked into scope. You see real working pages, not Figma mockups.

  • Two rounds of revisions
  • Content polished from your copy
  • Custom typography & palette

Launch & maintain

Domain, hosting, SSL, analytics, contact-form wiring, search-engine submission. Your $199/month subscription begins on launch day — that’s the only invoice, and it covers design, hosting, edits, security, and small additions. 12-month minimum, then month-to-month.
