What happens next

From note to preview.

Here’s what the first ten days usually look like after you send a message — so you know what to expect, and there’s nothing to wonder about.

Day 01

A direct reply.

Within one business day, you’ll get a personal email back from me — not a templated auto-response. I’ll ask a couple of clarifying questions if I have them, and confirm whether the studio is the right fit for what you’re after.

Days 02–05

A working preview.

If we’re a fit, I build a real preview of your firm’s site — not a Figma mockup, not a stock template. Your brand, your content polished from your existing copy, real responsive pages you can click around on. No commitment yet.

Days 06–10

Scope, deposit, build.

If the preview lands, I send a fixed scope and a service agreement — sign and return, no money down. From there it’s two to three weeks of focused build, two rounds of revisions, and a launch date you can plan around. Your $200/month subscription begins on launch day — that’s the only invoice, and it covers everything ongoing.
