What happens next

From note to preview.

Here’s what the first ten days usually look like after you send a message, so you know what to expect, and there’s nothing to wonder about.

Day 01

A direct reply.

Within one business day, you’ll get a personal email back, not a templated auto-response. Expect a couple of clarifying questions where they help, plus an honest read on whether the studio is the right fit for what you’re after.

Days 02–05

A working preview.

If we’re a fit, the next step is a real preview of your business’s site, not a Figma mockup, not a stock template. Your brand, your content polished from your existing copy, a real, responsive landing page, a redone homepage so you can feel how the full site would come together. No commitment yet.

Days 06–10

Scope, deposit, build.

If the preview lands, you get a fixed scope and a service agreement, sign and return, no money down. From there it’s two to three weeks of focused build, two rounds of revisions, and a launch date you can plan around. Your $75/month subscription begins on launch day, that’s the only invoice, and it covers everything ongoing.
